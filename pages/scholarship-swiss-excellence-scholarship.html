About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend (CHF 1,920/month), flight ticket, health insurance, and Swiss public transport pass for PhD/postdoc in Switzerland. 12-36 months (depending on programme)

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (ESKAT) eligibility criteria

  • Citizenship of a country on the SERI list (including Pakistan)
  • Under 35 years of age for PhD; under 35 for postdoc
  • Master's degree with excellent academic record
  • Admitted or applying to a Swiss university
  • Not already residing in Switzerland
  • Research proposal accepted by a Swiss professor

Benefits and coverage

  • Monthly stipend CHF 1,920
  • Full tuition fees (most Swiss universities charge low fees anyway)
  • Flight ticket to Switzerland
  • Health insurance
  • Swiss public transport half-fare card
  • Mentoring support
  • Cultural and networking events

Required documents

  • ESKAT application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Research proposal
  • Letter of acceptance from Swiss professor
  • Two recommendation letters
  • CV/Resume
  • Copy of passport
  • English/French/German/Italian proficiency certificate

How to apply for the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (ESKAT)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open in August and close in December. Results announced March-April. Submit a complete application before the December deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
