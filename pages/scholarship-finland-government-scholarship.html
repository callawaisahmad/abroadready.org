Finnish Government Scholarship Pool eligibility criteria

  • Non-Finnish citizen from an eligible country
  • Admitted to a Master's or PhD programme at a Finnish university
  • Excellent academic record
  • Meet the university's language requirements
  • Not already holding a residence permit in Finland
  • Applications submitted by the host university (not directly by students)

Benefits and coverage

  • Monthly stipend of €1,500-€2,000
  • Health insurance (for the grant period)
  • Access to Finnish research infrastructure
  • Possible accommodation support from host university
  • Visa support and orientation

Required documents

  • University admission letter
  • Research plan (for PhD applicants)
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • CV/Resume
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Motivation letter
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the Finnish Government Scholarship Pool

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by university; typically February-April for autumn intake. Universities nominate candidates to EDUFI. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
