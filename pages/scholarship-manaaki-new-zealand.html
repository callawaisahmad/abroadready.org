Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of an eligible developing country (current cycle: Pacific and Asia)
  • At least 18 when signing the offer letter
  • Postgraduate applicants: 1 year full-time relevant work experience (some waivers)
  • Meet institution academic entry standards
  • English proficiency (postgrad generally IELTS 6.5; undergrad IELTS 6.0)
  • Resided in home country for 2 years before applying (some exceptions)
  • Commit to return and contribute to home country for at least 2 years

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Living allowance for basic expenses
  • Establishment allowance for set-up costs
  • Medical insurance while in New Zealand
  • Return airfares (plus one home visit for programs over 1.6 years)
  • Travel insurance
  • Reintegration allowance on return home
  • Research/thesis cost support and tutoring where needed

Required documents

  • Online application form
  • Academic transcripts including the grading scale
  • Verified English-translated transcripts and completion certificates
  • Evidence of English proficiency
  • Proof of citizenship/eligibility
  • Work-experience evidence (postgraduate)

How to apply for the Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by country/region (roughly February-March); high-volume countries may close early, so apply early. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
