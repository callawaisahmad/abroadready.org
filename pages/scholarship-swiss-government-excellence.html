Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships eligibility criteria

  • Born after 31 December 1991
  • Master's degree or equivalent completed by 31 July 2027 (research/PhD); Bachelor's for art scholarships
  • Detailed research/project plan required
  • Letter of support/acceptance from a Swiss academic supervisor (research scholarships)
  • Priority to applicants with no prior Swiss study or research experience
  • Applicant's country must be on the ESKAS eligible list (apply via home country)
  • Previous ESKAS scholarship recipients are ineligible

Benefits and coverage

  • Monthly stipend of CHF 2,450 for basic living expenses
  • Health insurance premiums covered (non-EU/EFTA nationals)
  • One-time rental deposit grant of CHF 600
  • Half-Fare public transport travelcard
  • Return airfare to Switzerland (non-EU/EFTA nationals)
  • Social and cultural activities via the coordinating body

Required documents

  • Completed ESKAS online application form
  • Detailed CV (education, awards, publications)
  • Letter of motivation (max 2 pages)
  • Research proposal on the FCS/ESKAS form (max 5 pages)
  • Letter of support from a Swiss supervisor plus supervisor's CV
  • Two academic reference letters
  • Certified copies of university degrees and transcripts
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadline varies by country; the call opens around August/September with country deadlines running from autumn to winter. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
