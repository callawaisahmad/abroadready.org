About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend, accommodation, comprehensive medical insurance, and round-trip international airfare for students from Belt and Road partner countries. Bachelors 4-5 years; Masters 2-3 years; PhD 3-4 years (+ Chinese language preparation if needed)

Belt and Road Scholarship (China) eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of a Belt and Road partner country (including Pakistan)
  • Non-Chinese citizen in good health
  • Bachelors: high school graduate, under 25; Masters: Bachelor's degree, under 35; PhD: Master's degree, under 40
  • Meet university admission requirements
  • Not a current Chinese government scholarship holder
  • English proficiency or Chinese proficiency (HSK 4+ for Chinese-taught programmes)

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Monthly stipend: Bachelors CNY 2,500, Masters CNY 3,000, PhD CNY 3,500
  • Free on-campus accommodation
  • Comprehensive medical insurance
  • Round-trip international airfare (economy class)
  • Chinese language preparatory course (up to 1 year)
  • Settlement allowance upon arrival

Required documents

  • Chinese Government Scholarship application form
  • Notarized highest diploma and transcripts
  • Study plan or research proposal
  • Two recommendation letters (for Masters/PhD)
  • Foreigner Physical Examination Form
  • Copy of passport photo page
  • Language proficiency certificate (if available)

How to apply for the Belt and Road Scholarship (China)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications open in January and close in April. Check with the Chinese Embassy in your country for specific deadlines. Submit a complete application before the April deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
