Orange Tulip Scholarship (OTS) eligibility criteria

  • Historically limited to nationals of Neso countries: Brazil, China, India, Indonesia, Mexico, Russia, South Africa, South Korea and Vietnam
  • Pakistan was never an eligible Neso country, so Pakistani applicants could not use the central scheme
  • The central programme is closed; check directly with a specific university if an award under this name is advertised
  • Any live listing should be confirmed in writing with that institution before you rely on it

Benefits and coverage

  • Historic partial to full tuition awards, set by each participating university
  • Packages were tailored by the individual institution rather than centrally
  • Only relevant now if a specific university still runs an award under this name
  • Not a live central programme for the 2026-2027 or 2027-2028 cycles

Required documents

  • Proof of admission or application to a participating Dutch programme
  • Academic transcripts and diplomas
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Motivation letter
  • Proof of nationality (passport)
  • English language test results (IELTS/TOEFL)

How to apply for the Orange Tulip Scholarship (OTS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. No central deadline. The Neso network that administered Orange Tulip was phased out and its ministry subsidy ended on 1 January 2024. Any award still using the name is run by the individual university, so confirm it directly by email before applying. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
