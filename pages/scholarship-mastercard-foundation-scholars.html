Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program eligibility criteria

  • Primarily African youth from economically disadvantaged backgrounds
  • Academically talented and high-achieving
  • Service-oriented with leadership potential
  • Financial need demonstrated
  • Meets partner institution admission requirements
  • Not already hold a degree from a partner institution

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Accommodation
  • Books and academic materials
  • Living expenses/stipend
  • Mentoring and psychosocial support
  • Leadership development programme
  • Transition support post-graduation
  • Return airfare where necessary

Required documents

  • Academic transcripts
  • Proof of admission to partner institution
  • Evidence of financial need
  • Personal statement/essay
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Proof of identity/nationality

How to apply for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Each partner institution sets its own deadlines. Apply directly through the chosen partner institution. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
