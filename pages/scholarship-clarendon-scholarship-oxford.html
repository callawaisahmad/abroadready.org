Clarendon Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Applying for a full-time or part-time Master's or DPhil (PhD) at Oxford
  • Open to all subject areas and all fields of study
  • No restrictions on nationality or country of residence
  • Awarded on outstanding academic merit and potential
  • Must apply by the relevant December or January course deadline
  • New students only (not those with deferred offers or continuing the same degree)

Benefits and coverage

  • Full course fees paid (Home and Overseas rates)
  • Living-costs grant of at least £20,780/year (2025-26 rate)
  • Funding for the full period of fee liability of the course
  • Over 200 new scholarships awarded each year
  • Membership of the Clarendon Scholars' community and events
  • No separate application or application fee required

Required documents

  • Completed Oxford graduate application form
  • Academic transcripts
  • Letters of reference
  • Statement of purpose / personal statement
  • CV / résumé
  • Written work sample (where required by course)
  • Proof of English language proficiency (if applicable)

How to apply for the Clarendon Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. No separate application; apply for your course by its December or January deadline. Most offers sent by end of April. Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
