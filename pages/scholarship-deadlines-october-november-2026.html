Scholarship Deadlines October November 2026

🗓️ Sep 29, 2026⏱️ 24 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team✅ Verified against official sources

Scholarship deadlines october november 2026 are the busiest intersection of the funding calendar, and they decide who is funded for a 2027 start. Six schemes below close inside these two months, two more run on annual cycles that usually end here, and one opens on 1 November and stays open into late January.

The concentration is not accidental. Universities award entrance money to the students they already plan to admit, so the scholarship deadline and the admission deadline for the same intake are usually the same day or sit within weeks of each other. Starting a scholarship search after you receive an offer is the most expensive mistake in this entire process.

This is a deadline hub, not a directory of everything that exists. Every date, amount and eligibility rule below comes from the awarding body's own published material, and where an administrator sets the date, the official page always overrides anything written here. Dates shift, and award values get revised between cycles.

Read it as a working calendar rather than a reading list. Mark each closing date in your own planner, count back six weeks, and treat that earlier date as the real one. International student scholarships deadline planning works much like exam revision: the plan is the product, not the final sitting.

So when you are ready to apply for scholarships 2026 intake funding, work out which of these eight you actually qualify for before you touch a single document. Two of them will not apply to you, and one of those is the most valuable on the page.

Write down the closing date for each relevant award, count back six weeks, and put that earlier date in your calendar instead. Everything else on this page is detail; that single line is the whole plan.

Why the October–November 2026 Window Matters

Three separate systems collide in these two months, and each has its own logic. New-student entrance rounds reward academic achievement at the point of admission. Exchange rounds reward mobility for students already inside a university. Graduate fellowships reward research output, and they run on a committee calendar rather than a marketing calendar.

That collision is why fall 2027 scholarship deadlines appear in October and November rather than in the spring. By the time a university is deciding who enters its 2027 cohort, the money attached to that entry has usually been allocated, so the funding decision and the admissions decision travel together. You cannot optimise one without the other.

For applicants from Pakistan and nearby markets, the timing is tighter than the calendar suggests. Document certification, referee letters and portal load all slow down as October fills up, and a reference letter requested on 15 October can easily arrive after 21 October. Assume anything that requires another human being needs three weeks, not three days.

Scholarship application season is also when agents and scammers are busiest. A legitimate award will always be listed on the awarding institution's own website, with a portal you can reach yourself. No legitimate administrator will ask for a bank transfer in order to release a decision to you.

If you are starting from zero, our guide to finding scholarships you are actually eligible for explains how to filter by nationality, degree level and field before spending six weeks on applications you cannot win. Ten minutes of filtering routinely saves a month of drafting.

And if your intake is not settled yet, work that out first. An autumn 2027 start, a spring 2027 start and a 2028 start have entirely different calendars, and applying in the wrong round is the most common reason strong candidates end up with nothing at all.

Scholarship Deadlines October November 2026: The Full List

Eight awards, ordered by closing date, with the verified facts and the official link for each one. Read the eligibility line honestly. Most applications are lost to eligibility mistakes rather than to weak writing, and the cheapest fix in this whole process is reading the criteria list twice.

University of Auckland International Student Excellence Scholarship (opens 9 September, closes 21 October 2026)

The University of Auckland International Student Excellence Scholarship is the earliest deadline on this list, and one of the most generous for undergraduates. Applications open on 9 September 2026 and close on 21 October 2026 for the Semester 1, Quarter 1 and Quarter 2 2027 intake, so it funds a full year of study rather than a short course.

It is open to new international students entering an undergraduate degree, a Postgraduate Diploma or a taught masters programme, and around 50 awards are offered per round. That is a small pool for a popular university, so treat the award as genuinely competitive rather than automatic.

No separate GPA is listed in the published regulations, but an Admissions GPE of at least 6.0 is needed for consideration, and a separate masters document references GPE 7.0 for the masters variant. Strong grades are the expectation either way, so aim higher if you can.

A personal statement is required, and it decides most of the borderline cases. With roughly fifty places and strong applicant pools, the statement has to show why your academic record and your intended programme deserve money more than the several hundred applicants with comparable grades. Specifics beat adjectives here.

Amount: Up to NZ$10,000 toward compulsory tuition. Who it is for: New international students in undergraduate, Postgraduate Diploma or taught masters programmes. Deadline: Opens 9 September 2026, closes 21 October 2026. How to apply: Online through the University of Auckland scholarship portal, including a personal statement. See the official University of Auckland scholarship page for full terms.

University of Calgary International Entrance Scholarship (closes 1 December 2026)

The Calgary award is the largest single cheque here for an undergraduate, and the reason is that it renews. The University of Calgary International Entrance Scholarship pays CAD$20,000 per year and is renewable, which builds to CAD$80,000 over four years for an international undergraduate student entering first year.

It is applied for through the My UCalgary Portal as part of the university's Prestige Awards process, so you work inside the same system you use for admission. The published deadline for the 2027 Prestige Awards round is 1 December 2026, which places it just beyond the November cluster but inside the same planning cycle.

Because that date belongs to a wider awards round rather than to this scholarship alone, confirm it on the registrar's official page before you build a calendar around it.

Pretige Awards dates are set per round, and a different round may carry a different closing day. Treat 1 December 2026 as the working date and verify it rather than trusting a summary, including this one.

Amount: CAD$20,000 per year, renewable, up to CAD$80,000 over four years. Who it is for: International undergraduate students entering first year. Deadline: 1 December 2026 for the 2027 Prestige Awards round, confirm on the official registrar page. How to apply: My UCalgary Portal, Prestige Awards. Details on the University of Calgary registrar awards page.

York University International Student Scholarship & Award Application (opens 1 November 2026, closes 27 January 2027)

York is the outlier on this list, and the difference matters more than the amount. Instead of one award with one deadline, York runs a single application that unlocks a whole portfolio, and it opens on 1 November 2026. Award submissions are then accepted from 2 November 2026 until 27 January 2027, so the window runs almost three months.

The headline award inside that portfolio is the President's International Scholarship of Excellence, worth CAD$180,000 across a degree and paid as CAD$45,000 per year for four years. For an international undergraduate comparing offers, that single number can reorder an entire shortlist faster than any other figure in a funding table.

You need a York reference number, which is generated when you submit your admission application, so the two processes are linked rather than parallel. York recommends submitting your admission application by 19 January 2027 to leave room before the 27 January award deadline, which is an eight-day buffer that most applicants underestimate.

If you already hold a reference number, this is the least urgent item on the page. If you have not yet applied to York, the November opening is your trigger to start. Our guide to studying in Canada covers the wider funding landscape for international applicants.

Amount: A portfolio of awards, led by the President's International Scholarship of Excellence at CAD$180,000 (CAD$45,000 per year for four years). Who it is for: Incoming international students holding a York reference number. Deadline: Opens 1 November 2026, submissions 2 November 2026 to 27 January 2027. How to apply: York Future Students portal, see the official York international scholarships page.

Zonta International Amelia Earhart Fellowship (closes 15 November 2026)

This is the only doctoral fellowship on the list, and the only award restricted by both field and applicant profile. The Zonta International Amelia Earhart Fellowship closes on 15 November 2026 and is open to women pursuing a PhD or doctoral research in aerospace-related sciences and engineering.

The value is USD$10,000, which for most applicants will not fund a doctoral programme, so treat it as a research award that removes one element of cost rather than as full funding. Aerospace doctoral candidates usually apply to several fellowships at once, and this one slots into a mid-November cluster alongside departmental and national awards.

Applications of this kind are won on a research proposal and a supervisor's backing, not on a general personal statement. If you are early in a PhD with no published work, check whether your programme registers you as a doctoral researcher before the closing date, because the eligibility wording matters far more here than it does for an entrance award.

Amount: USD$10,000. Who it is for: Women pursuing PhD or doctoral research in aerospace-related sciences and engineering. Deadline: 15 November 2026. How to apply: Online through Zonta International. Check the official Zonta Amelia Earhart Fellowship page for the current cycle and any required endorsements.

360 International Award for Equity Students (closes 18 November 2026)

The 360 International Award for Equity Students is aimed at students who already study at the University of Auckland and are going out on exchange, not at incoming applicants. The second round opened on 7 October 2026 and closes on 18 November 2026, the only round here that opens and closes entirely inside the window.

The award is worth up to NZ$6,000, which usually covers a meaningful share of an exchange's travel and living costs but rarely all of it. Because it is equity-focused, selection weighs financial need alongside academic record, and applicants who can show a specific, itemised budget tend to be stronger than those describing the exchange in general terms.

Exchange timelines are unforgiving in a way degree applications are not. Host universities set their own nomination deadlines, and those usually fall before the scholarship deadline, which makes the funding application the last step rather than the first. Work backwards from your host's requirements instead of forwards from the closing date.

Amount: Up to NZ$6,000. Who it is for: University of Auckland students on an approved exchange. Deadline: Second round, opens 7 October 2026 and closes 18 November 2026. How to apply: Through the University's exchange and scholarship channels, with rounds confirmed on auckland.ac.nz before you start.

LIC Patrick Shannon Scholarship (closes 28 November 2026)

The LIC Patrick Shannon Scholarship is a postgraduate award with a narrow field definition and a specific purpose, and it closes on 28 November 2026. It is open to students in agriculture, science, genetics and machine learning, which is a broader list than it first appears when you read it as research areas rather than degree titles.

Two payment levels exist. Honours year students receive a one-off grant of NZ$5,000, and master's students can receive up to NZ$10,000. Because those amounts are modest against international tuition, this award works best as one layer in a funding stack rather than as a standalone solution to a programme's cost.

28 November is the last verified date on this page, which makes it the most forgiving close in practical terms, but the same discipline applies. Late November falls inside a university holiday period in the northern hemisphere, when referees are unavailable and administrative offices slow down. Treat 28 November as a date to beat by two weeks.

Amount: NZ$5,000 one-off grant for an Honours year, or up to NZ$10,000 for master's students. Who it is for: Students in agriculture, science, genetics and machine learning. Deadline: 28 November 2026. How to apply: Direct to the awarding body, after checking the current published criteria on the official page.

Chevening Awards, United Kingdom (annual cycle, typically closes early November)

Chevening is the only fully funded scheme on this list, and it is also the one where a fixed date matters least. The application window runs once a year, typically opening in early August and closing around early November, which places it at the far edge of the window this guide covers.

Because the exact dates move, do not build your calendar from a third-party summary, including this one. Check chevening.org for the current year's dates, confirm the closing time and the time zone it is counted in, and work backwards from there. The three-week rule for references still applies, and interviews follow in the spring.

The award is fully funded and covers tuition, living costs and flights, which makes it the highest-value item here for anyone without an independent income. It also attracts more applications per place than anything else listed.

A demonstrated record of leadership and networking therefore matters as much as the written sections, and both are built over years rather than weeks. Our Chevening scholarship complete guide covers the whole cycle in detail.

Amount: Fully funded, covering tuition, living costs and flights. Who it is for: Professionals with the required experience applying to a one-year UK master's. Deadline: Annual window, typically early August to early November. How to apply: Online through chevening.org only; confirm the exact current-year dates there.

DAAD Scholarships, Germany (staggered annual windows)

DAAD programmes sit under one organisation but run on separate schedules, so there is no single DAAD deadline worth memorising. Programmes such as EPOS operate on staggered annual windows, and applications are often due between late August and early October for study starting in the following year.

That places several DAAD programmes just before this window rather than inside it, which matters for anyone reading this page in October: some cycles will already have closed while others are still running. Check daad.de for each programme's current dates and treat the individual programme page, not the DAAD homepage, as the source of truth.

As with Chevening, use this entry as a prompt to research rather than a date to diarise. DAAD funding also interacts with blocked account requirements and admission timelines in Germany, so map the funding window against your visa and enrolment calendar before committing to a programme you cannot pay for twice.

Amount: Varies by programme, from monthly study allowances to complete funding. Who it is for: Varies by programme, from students to researchers. Deadline: Staggered annual windows, often late August to early October for the following year. How to apply: Online through daad.de and the relevant university portal; check dates per programme.

How to Apply: A Quick Checklist

Every item below applies to all eight schemes, and doing the preparation once saves you three weeks on each new application. The order matters considerably more than the speed. Scholarship deadlines 2026 are unforgiving in one specific way: the closing date is not when you decide to apply, it is when your file must already be complete.

  • Confirm eligibility once, carefully. Degree level, nationality status, year of study and whether you are a new or continuing student disqualify more applicants than any document problem. Work from the award's own criteria list, not from a comparison table.
  • Order transcripts on day one. Sealed, certified or electronically verified transcripts take days to weeks, and they are the most common reason a portal will not let you press submit.
  • Request references early and with context. Give each referee the award criteria, the real deadline and two sentences on why you are a strong candidate. A blind request produces a polite, forgettable letter.
  • Draft one master statement, then adapt it. A single personal statement edited to fit each award's language and field requirements beats six separate first drafts written under deadline pressure. Our guide to writing a scholarship motivation letter covers the structure.
  • Set an internal deadline five to seven days before the official close. It absorbs portal outages, time zone confusion and a late reference without changing your chances at all.
  • Watch time zones, not just dates. Auckland, Calgary and York publish deadlines in their own local time. 21 October in Auckland is a different moment from 21 October where you are sitting.
  • Save every confirmation email and reference number. Your York reference number and your Auckland application ID cannot be reconstructed later, and support desks ask for them first.
  • Run one final eligibility pass before submitting. Read the criteria against your application as a stranger would. Most rejections happen at this check, not at the panel table.
  • Apply to several and stagger them. Three to five well-matched applications spread across the window beat one rushed attempt, and the failure of one stops mattering. See our guide to common scholarship application mistakes.
  • Keep a documents folder organised by document type, not by award name. When a portal asks for something unexpected at 11pm, you want a filename you can find immediately.
  • Pay no processing fee to anyone for the awards on this page. Every one is applied for directly through the awarding institution, and legitimate administrators never take payment to release a result.

Two of these deserve extra attention from international applicants. Transcripts often need certified translation and an apostille, which is a two-week process on its own, and referees are frequently academics who have never supervised an international application and will reply with questions you should anticipate rather than guess at.

What Happens After You Miss an October or November Deadline?

Missing an October or November deadline is disappointing, but for most of these awards it is not the end of the cycle. The next round is usually between one and six months away, and several of these schemes are built to run more than once a year. The useful question is which round, not whether.

York is the clearest example. Its award window runs from 2 November 2026 to 27 January 2027, so an October date you missed elsewhere on this list has no effect on it. If York was always your target, you are still in time, and the correct response is to submit your admission application now rather than in January.

Auckland runs further rounds as well. The University of Auckland's excellence scholarship opens again on 1 April 2027 for a later 2027 intake, which is a genuine second chance for anyone who missed 21 October. Treat that as a different intake with a different start date rather than as a retry of the same one.

Beyond those two, the January to April 2027 window is when the next cluster of international funding opens across Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the UK. That is where a missed autumn round is usually recovered.

It is the window our fall intake scholarship timeline and 2027 intake deadline guide cover. If you want the wider funding picture for your nationality, our scholarships for Pakistani students round-up is worth reading alongside it.

For UK universities, the term Autumn entry usually means a September start, and most UK scholarship rounds for that entry close earlier in the year, often in the winter or spring before. If you missed an autumn 2026 scholarship deadline for a 2027 UK start, look at the next available cycle instead of assuming the year is finished for you.

The practical response is to write down what actually stopped you. A missing transcript, an unavailable referee and an unclear eligibility rule need three completely different fixes, and each one requires a different amount of lead time. Naming the cause is the only step that reliably changes the outcome next time.

One more option is worth naming for anyone who applied and was unsuccessful. Many universities keep unsuccessful scholarship files open for a subsequent round, and some will consider a previous application without requiring a fresh submission. Ask the scholarships office directly and in writing, rather than assuming either way.

There is also a research-funding route that does not depend on university entrance awards at all. Doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships, including conference and travel awards, run on their own calendars and frequently close in the months immediately after the main admission cycle. If you are a researcher, they belong on the same calendar as these eight.

Finally, note the value of stacking small awards. The 360 International Award, the LIC Patrick Shannon Scholarship and the Zonta fellowship are all modest amounts that nobody can live on alone. Pairing one with an entrance scholarship or a paid exchange placement is a legitimate strategy, which means a missed big award is a setback rather than a closed door.

Frequently asked questions about scholarship deadlines october november 2026

Six questions that come up most often during this window, answered as precisely as the published rules allow. Where an administrator sets a date, their own page overrides any answer given here, including these.

What scholarships have a deadline in October or November 2026?

Six verified items fall inside the window. The University of Auckland International Student Excellence Scholarship closes 21 October 2026, the Zonta Amelia Earhart Fellowship closes 15 November 2026, the 360 International Award closes 18 November 2026 and the LIC Patrick Shannon Scholarship closes 28 November 2026. York opens 1 November 2026 and runs to 27 January 2027, and Calgary's Prestige Awards round closes 1 December 2026. Chevening typically closes in early November.

Do I need an admission offer to apply for entrance scholarships?

Usually the opposite is true. Auckland and Calgary both sit inside the same portal you use for admission, and York requires a reference number generated by an admission application rather than a completed offer. In practice you apply for admission and funding together, and the offer follows the funding decision. Always read the wording, because a minority of awards are open only to students already enrolled at the awarding institution.

How early should I submit a scholarship application?

Six weeks before the closing date is comfortable, three weeks is workable and the final week is a coin flip. References and certified transcripts need human time, and both get harder to obtain as deadlines crowd together in late October. Set an internal deadline five to seven days before the official one and treat it as if a penalty applied for being late.

Which fully funded scholarships have deadlines in October and November?

Only one on this list is fully funded in the usual sense. Chevening covers tuition, living costs and flights, and its annual window typically closes in early November. Auckland, Calgary, York, the two New Zealand awards and the Zonta fellowship are all partial: they contribute toward tuition, toward a research budget or toward exchange costs, and none of them pays for a complete international programme.

What if I miss the October deadline this year?

You still have options. York's window runs to 27 January 2027, Auckland opens another round on 1 April 2027, and Chevening and DAAD reopen on their next annual cycles. Most universities also run a second intake, commonly called autumn entry in the UK, with its own funding rounds. The best next step is to identify which document stopped you and fix it before the next window opens.

Can international students apply for scholarships october 2026 without an admission offer?

Usually yes, and in several cases that is the only route. Auckland, Calgary and York all run their funding applications inside or alongside the admission process, so you submit one combined file rather than waiting for an offer. Chevening asks for a UK university offer at a later stage of its own process instead. Read each eligibility section carefully, because a few awards are restricted to students already enrolled at the awarding institution.

What scholarships have a deadline in October or November 2026?+

Do I need an admission offer to apply for entrance scholarships?+
