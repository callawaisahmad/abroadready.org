About this scholarship

Entrance scholarship ranging from CAD $5,000 to $40,000 for international undergraduate students entering the University of Calgary. One-time entrance award or up to 4 years (varies by award type)

University of Calgary International Entrance Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • International student (non-Canadian) entering first year of undergraduate study
  • Strong academic record (high school average of 80% or higher)
  • Must apply for admission to University of Calgary by the scholarship deadline
  • Meet English language requirements (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 86+)
  • Automatic consideration on admission — no separate scholarship application

Benefits and coverage

  • Scholarship amount CAD $5,000 to $40,000 (one-time or renewable)
  • Awarded on admission based on academic merit
  • No separate scholarship application required (automatic consideration)
  • Available for any undergraduate programme at UCalgary
  • Can be combined with other awards

Required documents

  • University of Calgary admission application
  • High school transcripts
  • English proficiency test scores
  • Personal statement (for some programmes)

How to apply for the University of Calgary International Entrance Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Scholarship consideration deadline: March 1 for September intake. Apply for admission by this date. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
