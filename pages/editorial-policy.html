Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

The short version: every factual claim on AbroadReady is checked against an official source before it is published, dated facts always show the year they were confirmed, and we correct errors when readers report them. We never guarantee that you will be admitted, funded or sponsored.

Why this page exists

Study abroad decisions involve money, deadlines and legal status. A mistake can cost an applicant a year or a significant sum. This policy explains exactly how our content is produced so you can judge it for yourself instead of taking it on trust.

Who writes the guides

Articles are produced by the AbroadReady editorial team and are signed as "AbroadReady Team" on each guide. We are a small independent site, not a university, an immigration adviser or an accredited agency. We do not represent any government, embassy, university or scholarship provider.

How we verify information

  • Official sources first. Fees, deadlines, visa rules and eligibility are taken from the issuing body: government departments, embassies, universities, uni-assist, APS, IRCC, UKVI and the scholarship provider itself.
  • We link to the source. Guides that state a fee, a deadline or a visa condition link to the official page so you can confirm it yourself.
  • Dated facts. Any figure that changes over time is written with the year it was confirmed, for example "as of September 2026".
  • Primary over secondary. Where a number circulates on blogs and social media without a source, we either trace it to the original document or leave it out.
  • No invented specifics. We do not publish application statistics, success rates or funding amounts that we cannot evidence.

What we do not do

  • We do not guarantee admission, scholarship awards, visa approval or job outcomes. Decisions rest with the awarding body or immigration authority.
  • We do not sell, broker or handle applications, and we never charge a fee to read a guide or use our tools.
  • We do not publish sponsored or paid content as editorial. Where a page carries advertising, that is advertising, not a recommendation.
  • We do not reproduce copyrighted material. We summarise official rules in our own words and link back to the original.

Corrections

If you find an error, an out-of-date figure or a deadline that no longer matches the official source, tell us and we will fix it. Include the page URL and a link to the official source where possible. Verified corrections are made on the page itself, because silently changing a deadline is worse than admitting a mistake.

How content is funded

AbroadReady is free and funded by advertising through Google AdSense. Ads are labelled by Google and are not endorsements. Our editorial decisions are not influenced by advertisers, and no advertiser has paid to change a fact, a ranking or a recommendation.
