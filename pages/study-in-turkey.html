Study in Turkey: The Complete 2026 Guide

Turkey combines genuinely affordable tuition — public universities from around US$600/year — with several fully English-medium universities ranked in the world's top 500. The state's Türkiye Bursları scholarship is one of the most generous in the world (tuition + dorm + stipend + flights). Here's how the visa, universities and costs actually work.

Why Study in Turkey?

Turkey is the value play in study abroad. Public universities cost US$600–4,000/year — a fraction of Western tuition — and universities like Koç, METU, Boğaziçi, Sabancı and Bilkent teach almost entirely in English. Istanbul is a world city with a low cost of living (US$500–800/month comfortable), and Türkiye Bursları offers a fully funded ride to thousands of international students each year. Over 250,000 international students already study there.

Heads-up: there is no traditional "student visa" — you enter on a Student Entry Visa from a Turkish consulate, then apply for a Student Residence Permit within 30 days of arrival. A tourist e-visa can never be converted. Budget ~US$540–600 all-in for the first year's visa + permit.

Affordable & Easy-Entry Universities

Turkey is one of the cheapest quality study destinations on Earth: state universities charge just $600–3,000/year for internationals and many admit without an entrance exam (some on diploma grades alone), while private (vakıf) universities like Altınbaş and Istanbul Ticaret run $4,000–7,000/year with easy ~50-60% GPA admission. Turkish-medium programs usually need no IELTS.

Degrees, Intakes & Admission

🎓 Bachelor's — 4 years

Medicine 6 years, dentistry 5. Internationals usually apply via YÖS (university-specific exam) or SAT/IB equivalents — no national exam needed.

📘 Master's — 2 years

Apply directly to the department with transcripts, references and English proof. Many top programs are English-medium.

🔬 PhD — 3–4 years

Fully English research environments at METU/Boğaziçi/Koç; Türkiye Bursları PhD stipend 9,000 TL/month.

🗓️ Intakes & language

Main intake September/October. Turkish-taught programs need TÖMER prep (1 year, B1–B2) — included free in Türkiye Bursları. Medicine is offered in English at ~9 universities.

Türkiye Bursları — The Fully Funded Scholarship

The state scholarship for international students. It covers 100% tuition at an assigned (mostly public) university, free KYK/state dormitory, a monthly stipend, one year of Turkish (TÖMER) language training, SGK health insurance and round-trip flights.

  • 2026 stipends: 4,500 TL/month undergrad, 6,500 TL/month master's, 9,000 TL/month PhD
  • Applications: open ~Jan 10 – Feb 20 each year; ~4,500–5,000 awards from 150,000+ applicants (~170 countries)
  • Selection: interviews Apr–Jul, results August; you list up to 12 program choices
  • Odds: roughly 2–5% acceptance — competitive but far better odds than US full rides
  • Merit scholarships (25–100% waivers) also exist at Koç, Sabancı and Bilkent
Strategy: apply for Türkiye Bursları and directly to universities in parallel. If you miss the scholarship window, METU/İTÜ/Boğaziçi public tuition is low enough to self-fund comfortably.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Turkey a good place to study if I don't speak Turkish?+

Yes — for English-medium programs at Koç, METU, Boğaziçi, Sabancı and Bilkent, instruction is almost entirely in English and IELTS/TOEFL is accepted. Many public universities also run English-taught degrees. The Türkiye Bursları scholarship includes a free Turkish prep year so you can study Turkish-taught programs too.

How much money do I need to show for the Turkish student visa?+

Consulates typically expect a personal bank statement of ~US$6,000–10,000 (or a scholarship letter). After arrival the residence permit application needs proof of funds plus health insurance. Actual living costs run US$300–800/month depending on city.

Can I work while studying in Turkey?+

Yes — undergraduate students may work from their 2nd year (graduates from the 1st semester), up to ~24 hrs/week during term and full-time in official holidays. Typical part-time pay is TRY 10,000–15,000/month.

Is the Türkiye Bursları scholarship hard to get?+

It's competitive — about 4,500–5,000 awards from 150,000+ applicants (roughly 2–5% acceptance). Interviews run April–July. Strong academics, a clear study plan and good achievements matter. Apply early in the January–February window and list the full 12 choices.

Can I stay in Turkey after graduation?+

Yes — switch to a work permit (çalışma izni) with a Turkish employer, or continue with a residence permit. After 8 years of permitted work you can qualify for a permanent work permit; the Turquoise Card is a "green-card equivalent" for highly qualified professionals. See Immigrate to Turkey.
