Belgium Scholarships for Developing Countries (ARES) eligibility criteria

  • Citizenship of an ARES partner developing country (including Pakistan)
  • Bachelor's degree for Masters; Master's for PhD
  • Professional experience of at least 2 years (for Masters)
  • Not already holding a degree from a Belgian institution
  • Proficient in French or English depending on the programme
  • Under 40 years of age for Masters, 45 for PhD

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Monthly living allowance €1,170
  • International travel costs
  • Visa fees reimbursement
  • Accommodation support
  • Health insurance
  • Supplement for dissertation/thesis costs

Required documents

  • ARES online application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • CV/Resume
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Motivation letter
  • Proof of nationality (passport)
  • Professional experience certificates
  • Language proficiency certificate

How to apply for the Belgium Scholarships for Developing Countries (ARES)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open in November and close in February. Check ares-ac.be. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
