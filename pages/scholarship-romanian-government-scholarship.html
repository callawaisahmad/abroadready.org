Romanian Government Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Non-EU citizen (not from EU member states)
  • Not of Romanian origin or from Romanian historical communities
  • Minimum study-years average of at least 7.0 (or 'Good' equivalent)
  • Not already a beneficiary of a Romanian state scholarship for the same cycle
  • Not holding protection/stateless status recognized in Romania
  • Submit a complete application file with recognized education documents

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition exemption for the study cycle
  • Monthly stipend for the duration of studies
  • Free tuition for the Romanian language preparatory year
  • Dormitory accommodation subject to availability
  • Exemption from registration and application fees
  • Study at accredited Romanian state universities

Required documents

  • Diplomas and academic transcripts
  • Birth certificate
  • Copy of passport (first 3 pages)
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Letter of intent and research project (PhD applicants)
  • Tutor/supervisor agreement (PhD applicants)
  • Apostilled/authenticated translations in Romanian, English, French or Spanish

How to apply for the Romanian Government Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open mid-February and close end of March; results around mid-July. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
