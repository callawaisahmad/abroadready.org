Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS) eligibility criteria

  • Bachelor's degree (typically 4 years) in a relevant subject
  • At least two years' professional experience after the degree
  • National of an eligible developing / newly industrialised country
  • Academic degree normally not older than 6 years
  • Not resided in Germany more than 15 months at the application deadline
  • Upper-third academic performance
  • Language proof (min. B1 German or required English level)

Benefits and coverage

  • Monthly stipend of €934 for graduates / €1,300 for doctoral candidates
  • Health, accident and personal liability insurance
  • Travel allowance (unless covered otherwise)
  • Possible monthly rent subsidy
  • Possible family allowance
  • Up to 6-month intensive German language course before studies
  • Study and research allowance

Required documents

  • DAAD application form and checklist
  • Europass-format CV
  • Motivation letter
  • Employer recommendation letter
  • Employment certificates proving 2+ years of experience
  • Language certificate (IELTS/TOEFL or DSH/TestDaF)
  • Certified degree certificates and transcript of records
  • APS certificate (applicants from China only)

How to apply for the Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Varies by course Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
