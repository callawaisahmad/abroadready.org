France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship Programme eligibility criteria

  • Foreign (non-French) nationality
  • Applications submitted only by French higher education institutions, not directly by students
  • Applying for a Master's (M1/M2), engineering, or joint doctoral programme in a priority discipline
  • Cannot be combined with another French government or Erasmus+ scholarship
  • New doctoral candidates seeking a 36-month grant must not already be studying in France
  • Selection based on academic excellence of the nominated student

Benefits and coverage

  • Monthly allowance of €1,200 (Master's) or €2,100 (Doctorate) from January 2026
  • International and domestic transport costs covered
  • Health and social insurance coverage
  • Housing assistance
  • Cultural activities allowance

Required documents

How to apply for the France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship Programme

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Institutions submit applications in early January (8 January for the 2026 intake). Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
