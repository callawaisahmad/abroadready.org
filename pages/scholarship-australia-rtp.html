About this scholarship

Government support for research-degree students covering tuition offset, a tax-free living stipend and allowances, administered by each university. Research Doctorate 3-4 yr FT; Research Masters up to 2 yr FT

Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) eligibility criteria

  • Open to both domestic and international students
  • Enrolled in or admitted to a Research Doctorate or Research Masters
  • Awarded competitively on academic merit and research potential
  • Meet the university's admission and English-language requirements
  • Not concurrently holding an equivalent living-allowance scholarship
  • Commencing or early in the research degree

Benefits and coverage

  • RTP Fees Offset (tuition offset for the research degree)
  • Tax-free living stipend (base rate approx. AUD 37,010/year in 2026, indexed annually)
  • Relocation/removal allowance (university-dependent)
  • Thesis allowance (university-dependent)
  • Paid sick, maternity and parental leave (university-dependent)
  • OSHC health-cover reimbursement for international recipients (university-dependent)

Required documents

  • Academic transcripts and degree evidence
  • Research proposal
  • Evidence of research capacity or publications
  • CV
  • Referee reports
  • English-language test results (international applicants)

How to apply for the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. No single national deadline; each university sets and runs its own RTP scholarship rounds. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
