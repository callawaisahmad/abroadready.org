Gates Cambridge Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of any country outside the United Kingdom
  • Applying for a PhD, MLitt (full-time), or a one-year postgraduate course
  • Not eligible for undergraduate, MBA/EMBA, MFin, PGCE or certain professional courses
  • Demonstrated outstanding academic excellence
  • Commitment to improving the lives of others
  • Capacity for leadership
  • Must secure admission to the University of Cambridge

Benefits and coverage

  • University Composition Fee (tuition) at the appropriate rate
  • Maintenance allowance of £22,050 for 12 months (2025-26 rate)
  • One economy single airfare at the start and end of the course
  • Inbound visa costs and the Immigration Health Surcharge
  • Academic development funding of £500-£2,000 for conferences and courses
  • Dependent children allowance
  • Up to 6 months maternity/paternity leave with continued maintenance
  • Discretionary fieldwork and hardship funding

Required documents

  • Graduate application submitted via the University's Applicant Portal
  • Personal statement on suitability for the course
  • Four Gates Cambridge criteria statements (200-300 words each)
  • Two academic references
  • One Gates Cambridge-specific reference
  • Research proposal (PhD applicants only)
  • Academic transcripts

How to apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. US citizens/residents round closes mid-October; international round closes December/early January depending on course. Submit a complete application before the October deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
