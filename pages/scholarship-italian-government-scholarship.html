Italian Government Scholarship (Invest Your Talent in Italy) eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of eligible countries (check MAECI website)
  • Bachelor's degree for Masters; Master's for PhD
  • English proficiency (B2 level or higher)
  • Age limit: 28 years for Masters, 40 for PhD
  • Not already residing in Italy
  • Meet university-specific admission requirements

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Monthly stipend €900
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Italian language and culture course
  • Access to Italian university network
  • No tuition at public universities

Required documents

  • MAECI online application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • English/Italian language certificate
  • CV/Resume
  • Motivation letter
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Copy of passport
  • University admission letter (if already obtained)

How to apply for the Italian Government Scholarship (Invest Your Talent in Italy)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open in April and close in May. Results announced July-August. Submit a complete application before the May deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
