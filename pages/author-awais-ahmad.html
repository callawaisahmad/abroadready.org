The short version: I am the founder and editor of AbroadReady. I am not a former admissions officer, a licensed immigration adviser, a university representative or a scholarship winner. The guides on this site are built by reading the issuing body's own published documents and linking to them, so you can check every figure yourself.

What I actually do

I research and write the guides, maintain the scholarship database and build the tools on this site — the deadline tracker, the SOP builder and the AI advisor. My work is closer to compilation and fact-checking than to personal experience, and the site is written to reflect that.

How the guides are researched

  • Issuing body first. Deadlines, fees, eligibility and visa rules come from the government department, embassy, university, uni-assist, APS, IRCC, UKVI or scholarship provider that sets them.
  • Linked, not paraphrased away. If a guide states a fee, a deadline or a visa condition, it links to the page that states it.
  • Dated figures. Anything that changes over time carries the month it was confirmed, so you can see how current it is.
  • Nothing invented. I do not publish success rates, application statistics or award amounts that I cannot trace to a source.
  • Corrections in public. When a figure turns out to be wrong it is corrected on the page, not quietly deleted.

Why the site exists

Scholarship information is scattered across government portals, embassy pages, university PDFs and outdated blog posts. AbroadReady puts the opportunities in one filterable board and writes the application steps around them. It is free, and it is funded by advertising rather than by selling application services.

What I cannot do for you

I cannot assess your specific eligibility, guarantee an admission, a scholarship or a visa, or act as your agent. Decisions rest entirely with the awarding body or the immigration authority. If a guide helps you ask the right question of the right office, it has done its job.

Contact

Spotted an error, a stale deadline or a fee that has changed? Use the contact page and include the page URL plus a link to the official source. The full research process is set out in the editorial policy, and there is more about the platform on the about page.
