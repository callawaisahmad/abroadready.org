Rhodes Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Age typically 18-23 (or under 27 with a recently completed first degree); limits vary by constituency
  • Completed an undergraduate/Bachelor's degree by the course start
  • Citizen or qualifying resident of an eligible Rhodes constituency
  • Strong academic record (e.g. US GPA of 3.70/4.0 preferred)
  • Meet the entry requirements of the chosen Oxford course
  • Demonstrated leadership and moral force of character
  • Commitment to service and the wellbeing of others

Benefits and coverage

  • University of Oxford course fees
  • Annual living stipend of £20,400 (2025-26)
  • Oxford application fees covered
  • Student visa and Immigration Health Surcharge fees
  • Two economy flights to and from the UK
  • Settling-in allowance on arrival
  • Access to Rhodes House and a global community of 4,500+ scholars
  • Academic and pastoral support, plus emergency financial assistance

Required documents

  • Personal statement (max ~1,000 words)
  • Curriculum vitae (max 2 pages)
  • Academic statement of proposed study (max ~350 words)
  • Between 5 and 8 references (at least 4 academic, at least 1 character)
  • Official academic transcripts
  • Proof of age and citizenship (passport/birth certificate/ID)
  • Head-and-shoulders colour photograph
  • Institutional endorsement from your college/university

How to apply for the Rhodes Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by constituency/country; the US deadline is 1 October. Submit a complete application before the October deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
