Amsterdam Merit Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Non-EU/EEA international student
  • (Provisionally) admitted to a Master's programme at UvA
  • Excellent academic record (top 10-15% of your graduating class)
  • Meet UvA English language requirements (IELTS 6.5-7.0 / TOEFL 92-100)
  • No previous degree from UvA
  • Not eligible for Dutch government student finance

Benefits and coverage

  • Scholarship of €6,000–€25,000 (full tuition) for one year
  • Possible renewal for a second year
  • Awarded based on academic merit
  • Covers full-time Master's at UvA
  • Can be combined with other funding sources

Required documents

  • UvA admission application (Studielink)
  • AMS application form (submitted to UvA after admission)
  • Academic transcripts and GPA ranking
  • Motivation letter
  • CV/Resume
  • English proficiency certificate
  • Two letters of recommendation

How to apply for the Amsterdam Merit Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Admission application typically by March 1 or May 1; AMS scholarship application by March 1 for September intake. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
