New Zealand Scholarships (Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships) eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of an eligible developing country (including Pakistan)
  • Under 40 years of age
  • Bachelor's degree with good grades
  • Not already a NZ citizen/permanent resident
  • Meet university admission requirements
  • Not previously studied in NZ for 12+ months
  • IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Living allowance NZD $400 per week
  • Establishment allowance NZD $3,000
  • Return economy airfare
  • Travel allowance within New Zealand
  • Health insurance
  • Research and thesis costs support

Required documents

  • Online application
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • English proficiency certificate
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Statement of purpose
  • CV/Resume
  • Copy of passport
  • Medical certificate

How to apply for the New Zealand Scholarships (Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open in February and close in March. Check scholarships.mfat.govt.nz. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
