Apply for Masters in Germany Roadmap & Timeline

🗓️ Sep 26, 2026⏱️ 27 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

Masters in Germany roadmap at a glance: eighteen months, not twelve

Most applicants do not fail a German master's application on academics. They fail on timing. This masters in Germany roadmap turns a vague intention into a calendar, running from intake selection to the day you land.

The sequence it covers is shortlisting, language testing, the APS procedure, uni-assist, the blocked account and the Type D student visa. It assumes an October winter intake and plans eighteen months, because that is the honest window for anyone applying from South Asia, where every stage sits behind a queue.

The most useful thing this roadmap does is put dates on everything. Universities publish hard deadlines, the APS procedure has its own queue, the blocked account must be funded before the visa appointment, and the visa takes six to twelve weeks.

Work the sequence below rather than reacting to it, and the calendar becomes a checklist instead of a source of stress.

Three phases structure the whole process. Phase 1 (months 1 to 11) is research and preparation: the intake, a shortlist of ten to fourteen programmes, your language test, APS and scholarship applications.

Phase 2 (months 12 to 15) is submission, clustering around the 15 July winter deadline. Phase 3 (months 16 to 18) is money and paper: the offer, the blocked account, the visa appointment and the flight. Each phase feeds the next, so the order is not a suggestion.

The one step you cannot take back is month one: fix the intake, the field and the start date, then schedule everything backwards from the 15 July deadline.

How long does it take to apply for masters in Germany?

Twelve to eighteen months from your first serious university search to your arrival day. Anyone quoting six months is describing a best case that skips the language test, the APS queue or the visa backlog, and all three sit outside your control.

The 12 month roadmap for masters in Germany, and who it actually fits

A twelve-month plan works if you already have a settled direction, a decent IELTS or TOEFL score, money identified for the blocked account and a clear field. Eighteen months is the safer window if you are finishing a degree, working, or funding everything from family savings, because it absorbs one failed attempt without breaking the schedule.

Winter intake versus summer intake

Target the winter intake, starting in October or early November. It has by far the largest number of programmes open to international applicants, including most English-taught options, and it is the intake scholarships are designed around.

The summer intake starting in April exists, but it is thin in data science, business and engineering, and many schemes do not fund it. Our wider guide to study in Germany from Pakistan in 2026 covers the intake comparison in depth.

The two risks that cost applicants a full year

The biggest risk is treating the visa as a formality. A Pakistani file submitted in mid-September for an October start will not land in time.

The second is funding the blocked account late, because the transfer, the confirmation certificate and the visa appointment must happen in that order, and that chain needs three to four weeks minimum. Plan backwards from your arrival date.

The Eignungsfeststellungsverfahren and why it filters people out

For non-EU applicants, most German master's programmes run an aptitude assessment called the Eignungsfeststellungsverfahren. It converts your school-leaving certificate and bachelor's grade into the German grading system and checks them against the programme's threshold.

It usually runs through uni-assist, and it is where a surprising number of otherwise-strong files are quietly filtered out. The documents it needs are your attested and translated intermediate certificate, matriculation certificate, degree and transcripts, which is why HEC attestation must be finished by month five or six.

Booking your language test in the right window

IELTS Academic is the default, because the widest number of German programmes accept it, followed by TOEFL iBT. Book the test in month five or six for a sitting in month six or seven, which leaves a December to February retake window.

German-taught programmes want a German proof such as TestDaF, and medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine and pharmacy run through the Studienkolleg route with a separate FSP exam. If you would rather skip an English test, studying abroad without IELTS as a Pakistani student sets out which programmes genuinely waive it.

APS before the visa, not before the applications

Only before the visa. Universities do not check APS status, so you can submit every application without it. But it is a hard gate before the visa appointment, and the German mission will not let you book a Type D appointment before it is done.

The fee runs about PKR 35,000 to 40,000, normal processing is four to six weeks, and it stretches to eight to ten weeks in the April to July peak. Run it between August and November if you can, not between April and June.

How large a shortlist should be?

Ten to fourteen programmes, split across three tiers. Tier one: three or four ambitious but realistic targets. Tier two: five or six safe programmes in cities you can afford, with English-taught options and a clear aptitude route.

Tier three is insurance: rolling programmes you would genuinely attend. Never build a shortlist from twelve universities in one city, because results arrive in overlapping weeks and you end up choosing blind.

Where to search for programmes

Three sources cover almost everything. Hochschulkompass is the official database covering every German university, including subject-specific admission rules, language of instruction and deadlines.

The DAAD study-in-Germany portal is the best filter for English-taught programmes and scholarship deadlines. uni-assist is the centralised application and assessment service used by a large share of German universities.

Always finish on the individual programme page, because the same programme title can carry a different admission rule at two different universities. When you are down to two or three, compare their deadlines and aptitude routes side by side.

Scholarship applications belong earlier than admission applications

Yes, and much earlier. DAAD and most university scholarships close between September and early October for the following winter, so funding work belongs in months three to six while admission work belongs in months twelve to fifteen.

Studying masters in Germany on a scholarship is a plan, not a fallback. Apply on the original schedule and fund the admission application anyway.

Shifting the calendar when your deadline is not 15 July

Keep the month offsets and move the calendar column. If you are chasing the 15 January summer deadline, shift everything forward by six months. If a programme advertises a 1 March or 1 May deadline, treat that date as the anchor and pull your own submission forward to month eleven.

Rolling programmes give you flexibility, and they are why the tier three shortlist exists.

Can the timeline be compressed if you are starting late?

You can compress the research and test phase, because Hochschulkompass does not care which month you read it. You cannot compress HEC attestation, the uni-assist assessment, the blocked-account transfer or the visa.

If you have eight months rather than eighteen, the honest move is to target the following winter intake, because a rushed German application fails in the specific way where a missing translation is discovered in month fourteen.

Masters in Germany application process: what you submit and when

Months twelve to fifteen are about documents and deadlines. Most of the masters in Germany application process is administrative rather than personal, so completeness beats charisma every time. Budget for it as paperwork, not as writing.

The German document checklist

Expect a checklist rather than a creative brief: a passport copy, a tabular CV, a one-page motivation letter, your degree certificate and transcript, HEC-attested copies with certified translations, your APS certificate, your language result, and passport photographs.

Some programmes also ask for a GRE or GMAT score, a portfolio, recommendation letters, or proof of a pre-study internship. Each programme page lists the exact set, and that page is the only checklist that counts.

uni-assist fees and what they actually cover

It depends on the university. Some accept direct applications; the majority use uni-assist, and many require the central aptitude assessment through it. It also does the verification work, so skipping it means repeating that at the university.

uni-assist charges roughly EUR 75 for your first application and about EUR 30 for each additional university, so a ten-university shortlist costs around EUR 345 in fees. Budget four to six weeks for the assessment, longer at peak.

Masters in Germany deadlines: the two dates that matter

Two dates dominate: 15 July for the winter semester and 15 January for the summer semester. Those are the standard deadlines printed on most programme pages, but they are not universal.

A significant minority set earlier internal cut-offs so assessment finishes before the date, and many English-taught programmes run rolling admissions with a stated final date. Treat 15 July as your anchor and each programme page as the operative rule, and submit three to four weeks early wherever the portal allows it.

Print the masters in Germany deadlines for your shortlist into one table with the language score beside each. It takes an hour, and it is the only way to spot the programme you have run out of time for.

Attest first, translate second

Your degree certificate, transcripts, FSC or equivalent, and matriculation record should be attested by the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan, then translated into English or German by a certified translator who stamps and signs it.

Start this in month five because it is the longest-lead document task in the roadmap, and because no assessment can be completed without it. If you are unsure which document needs which treatment, check the list on your top three universities.

One master CV, twelve tailored motivation letters

Build a single master document in month eight, then adjust the summary, module list and motivation letter per programme. the difference between a resume and a CV for study abroad explains the format German applications expect: a tabular, dated, two-page CV with no photo.

A generic letter sent to twelve admissions offices is the most common reason strong Pakistani applicants are quietly rejected.

Offers, waitlists and second-round admissions

You get a portal or uni-assist confirmation, and then you wait. Most winter decisions land between mid-July and early September, with the bulk in August. Expect one of four outcomes: an offer, a waitlist position, a request for supplementary documents, or a refusal.

If your first choice refuses, the second-round procedure usually opens in September for programmes with remaining capacity, which is why tier two and tier three exist. Medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and veterinary medicine follow an FSP aptitude route instead.

When to apply for masters in Germany: the intake that decides everything

Work backwards from the intake, not forwards from today. For a winter start, applications land by 15 July, the visa is filed by early September, and research begins roughly ten months before that. For a summer start, everything shifts six months and the programme list shrinks.

Most people get the answer to when to apply for masters in Germany wrong by treating the deadline as the start of the process instead of the end of it.

Recovery routes if you miss the 15 July deadline

You have more routes than you think. Some programmes accept late or rolling applications into September. The 15 January summer deadline opens a second round for the following April, though the programme list is shorter.

German-language programmes and some Fachhochschule routes recruit later, and the Studienkolleg route for medicine runs on its own cycle. The worst option is panicking and applying somewhere you would not study.

When the visa window starts to bite

From roughly the end of June, the visa queue is competing with the peak APS season, so anything filed from Pakistan needs the ten-week case as your planning number rather than the four-week one. Anyone aiming for an October arrival should have the blocked account funded by the end of August.

Blocked account: EUR 11,904 and the providers

The blocked account, or Sperrkonto, is a German student account that proves you can support yourself for one year. For 2026 the required amount is EUR 11,904, which is exactly EUR 992 per month for twelve months, unchanged since September 2024.

The money is real and yours, but the bank releases it in monthly instalments of up to one-twelfth, so it works as proof rather than a lump sum. Licensed providers include Expatrio, Fintiba and Coracle, plus German bank branches with an international student desk.

You need the provider's confirmation certificate for the visa file, and proof of where the funds came from.

Opening the blocked account before you have an offer

Sometimes. If a university issues a conditional admission or pre-application approval letter, many Pakistani families fund the account on the strength of that document, which buys two months of visa margin. With only a pending application, do not open it blind, because an account opened for nothing still costs the setup fee and the transfer.

Have the money ready to instruct within 48 hours of your first offer, since the transfer plus certificate takes two to three weeks.

Where the APS certificate sits in the sequence

Immediately before the visa appointment, and never after it. Pakistani nationals complete the APS individual procedure through the German mission network, submitting attested academic documents, passport copies, the APS form and a fee of roughly PKR 35,000 to 40,000.

You cannot book the visa appointment until APS is concluded, which is the most misunderstood dependency in this whole timeline.

What you actually file at the visa appointment

A Type D national visa, because a study stay exceeds 90 days, filed through VFS Global in Pakistan and decided by the German mission. The fee is EUR 75.

The file contains your admission letter, the blocked-account confirmation certificate, German health insurance valid for the visa period, the APS certificate, passport, photographs, the application form, your attested and translated academic certificates, proof of accommodation, and evidence of where the funds came from.

Expect biometric capture and a short consular interview, and check the current checklist on the mission's own pages before you book.

Visa processing time and what slows it down

Officially six to twelve weeks from a complete submission, and longer at peak. There is no fast-track, and Pakistani applicants often add weeks because the mission writes back to the university to verify an admission.

That is why this roadmap files the visa in month seventeen and treats arrival in month eighteen as the optimistic case. Do not book non-refundable flights before the passport is in your hand, and ask about deferred enrolment, which most faculties allow for November or December arrivals.

The first 30 days after landing

Register your address with the local Bürgeramt within 14 days, because almost everything else depends on that registration. Then apply for your residence permit, enrol, take out German statutory health insurance if you arrived on travel insurance, and collect the semester ticket.

Students under 30 pay roughly EUR 128 to EUR 140 per month for statutory student insurance, which sits inside your EUR 992 monthly allowance. Apply for a dormitory place before you fly, because waiting lists in popular cities run for months. the most common visa refusals for Pakistani students is worth reading once before you file.

A realistic first-year budget for a Pakistani applicant

For eight to ten applications in one winter round, a realistic first-year outlay is roughly EUR 13,300 to EUR 14,000: EUR 11,904 for the blocked account, EUR 75 for the visa, roughly EUR 250 in uni-assist fees, EUR 200 to 300 for the language test, about EUR 115 equivalent for APS, and EUR 500 to 900 for flights and setup.

Add PKR 20,000 to 40,000 for attestation and translation, plus a ten to fifteen percent contingency, which absorbs a retake, a second uni-assist fee, or a rescheduled flight. Fees for masters in Germany beyond the blocked account

Public universities charge no tuition to international students, only a semester contribution of roughly EUR 150 to 390. Baden-Wurttemberg charges EUR 1,500 per semester for non-EU students, which is the fee to plan around if your shortlist includes Stuttgart, Karlsruhe, Freiburg or Mannheim.

Private institutions charge real money. If cost is the deciding factor, filter your longlist by state as well as by subject, and how to study in Germany for free covers the levers that keep the budget intact.

Is the blocked account enough on its own?

It is designed to be, and for a careful budget in a mid-sized city it works. EUR 992 per month covers a shared room or dorm at EUR 350 to 600, statutory insurance at EUR 128 to 140, food at EUR 200 to 300, transport, phone and study materials, with a margin left over.

It does not comfortably cover Munich, Frankfurt, or a private flat in Berlin, so your city choice is a financial decision as much as an academic one.

Can a scholarship replace the blocked account?

Partly, in the right cases. A DAAD stipend counts as financial evidence, and many Pakistani applicants combine a partial scholarship, a family top-up, and a smaller blocked account covering the gap. What does not work is assuming a scholarship you have only applied for.

Treat the award as a bonus until the letter is in hand, and never build a funding plan around a decision arriving in the same month as your visa appointment. DAAD scholarships explained for study in Germany details which schemes are open to Pakistani applicants.

If your family cannot release EUR 11,904 at once

Work on it in month two, not month sixteen. Pakistani banks run education financing schemes, and several private lenders offer overseas tuition products, though approval cycles are slow. Combine a loan for flights and setup with family funds for the blocked account, and apply in parallel for every scholarship with a September or October deadline.

How the roadmap changes for applicants from Pakistan, Asia and the USA

The eighteen-month calendar holds for everyone, but the dependencies inside it do not. Pakistani nationals, the largest group reading about masters in Germany from Pakistan, have one gate the others do not: the APS individual procedure, completed before any visa appointment can be booked.

India, Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka meet the same requirement through their own German missions, with different fee levels, appointment systems and document lists. That is the detail most people searching for masters in Germany from Asia get wrong, because they copy a checklist written for another country.

US students skip APS entirely and fund the blocked account from a US bank or a 529 plan, which compresses the money phase. For anyone weighing masters in Germany from USA, that is the biggest practical difference in the process.

Degree titles confuse too: what one system calls an MS in Germany is often awarded as an MSc, and a research track sold as an MPhil in Germany is usually an MSc with a thesis. The Germany masters degree title must be recognised by your home country's accrediting body.

Where applications break, and how to fix them

Almost every failed German application traces back to one of five specific failures, and knowing them in advance is why you run a masters in Germany timeline eighteen months early. None are academic; all are administrative.

The failures, in order of frequency

In order: incomplete document sets at the university stage, missing or late APS certificates before the visa, a blocked account funded but not certified, and translations that are not certified. Time pressure is the underlying cause of nearly all of them.

Students with smooth applications are not more talented; they finished the roadmap in month six when nobody was watching, and spent months twelve to fifteen submitting rather than scrambling.

How to avoid a visa refusal filed from Pakistan

Check four numbers against each other before you book: the intake dates on your admission letter, the validity dates on your insurance, the amount in your blocked account, and the travel dates in your cover letter. Mismatches in any of these are the classic refusal triggers.

Confirm your passport has the required remaining validity, your APS certificate covers the current procedure, and the university issued a proper admission letter rather than a provisional note. student visa refusal reasons for Pakistan in 2026 goes through the refusal categories case by case.

What to do if your English is not strong enough yet

Book the test, but plan for a retake, and pick a shortlist with a mix of requirements rather than a uniform one. Some German master's programmes accept TOEFL, PTE or a university internal test, and a small number waive the English test entirely for applicants with a certain degree background.

Working after you arrive

Work within the rule: 20 hours per week during lecture time, or a maximum of 120 full days per calendar year, at a statutory minimum wage of EUR 13.90 per hour. A 20-hour week at that rate is roughly EUR 540 a month, which turns a tight budget into a comfortable one within a semester.

Running the roadmap from Pakistan while you work or study

This timeline is designed to run alongside a job or a final year of degree, because the heavy weeks are short and the quiet months are long.

Weekly hours across the three phases

Five to eight hours a week for months one to nine, and fifteen to twenty for months ten to fifteen. Months one to nine are research, tests, attestation and scholarship writing, all of which slot into evenings.

Months twelve to fifteen are document assembly and submission, which are concentrated and deadline-driven, so protect daytime hours if you are employed. Months sixteen to eighteen are the visa phase: mostly waiting, with a few fixed appointments.

One spreadsheet, five columns

The date, the task, the dependency, the document produced, and the status. Dependencies are the part most people miss. The language test date drives the application date; the attestation drives the uni-assist assessment; the assessment drives the admission.

The admission drives the blocked account; the certificate drives the visa appointment; the appointment drives the flight. Write each task beside the task it blocks, and the Germany masters application timeline stops being a source of stress.

If you are starting this month

Pick your intake, open the tracker, and start the two longest-lead items in the same week: HEC attestation and the APS booking. Both have queues you cannot shorten and lead times you cannot compress, and everything else is easier to arrange once they are moving.

Then sit your language test before you write a single application, because a score below the threshold turns a twelve-programme shortlist into a three-programme one.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the whole Germany masters application process take?+

Plan 12 to 18 months from your first university search to landing in Germany: roughly 4 to 6 months for research and tests, 3 to 4 months for documents and applications, 4 to 8 weeks for uni-assist, and 6 to 12 weeks for the student visa.

What is the very first step to apply for a masters in Germany?+

The first step is choosing your intake and shortlisting suitable programmes, because every later decision — language test, APS certificate, blocked account and visa — is scheduled backwards from the application deadline you pick.

Which intake should a Pakistani student choose for Germany?+

Winter intake (October start) has the largest number of open programmes, which is why most Pakistani applicants target it; summer intake (April start) has fewer English-taught options, though some fields run both.

How much money is needed overall for a Germany masters application?+

Beyond the blocked account of EUR 11,904 for 2026, budget for uni-assist fees (about EUR 75 per university), language tests (EUR 200 to 300), the EUR 75 visa fee and airfare. Public universities charge no tuition, only a semester fee of roughly EUR 150 to 390.
