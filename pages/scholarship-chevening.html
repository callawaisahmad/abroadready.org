Chevening Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory
  • At least two years' (2,800 hours) work experience after your undergraduate degree
  • Hold an undergraduate degree qualifying you for a UK master's
  • Apply to three eligible UK university courses and secure an unconditional offer from one
  • Meet the Chevening English language requirement by the required deadline
  • Commit to returning to your home country for at least two years after the scholarship
  • Not have previously received UK government or Chevening funding

Benefits and coverage

  • Payment of university tuition fees
  • Monthly living allowance (stipend), with higher rate for London
  • Return economy airfare to and from your country of residence
  • Arrival allowance and departure allowance
  • Cost of an entry clearance (visa) application
  • Contribution of up to £75 for TB testing where required
  • Travel top-up allowance
  • Access to the Chevening engagement and networking programme

Required documents

  • Valid passport or national ID
  • Undergraduate degree certificate / academic transcripts
  • Two reference letters
  • Unconditional UK university offer letter
  • Evidence of meeting the English language requirement
  • Completed online application essays

How to apply for the Chevening Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Early November annually (applications open early August) Submit a complete application before the November deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
