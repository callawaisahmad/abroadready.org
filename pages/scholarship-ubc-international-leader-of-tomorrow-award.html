UBC International Leader of Tomorrow Award eligibility criteria

  • International student entering UBC from a secondary school
  • Demonstrated outstanding academic achievement
  • Demonstrated leadership through extracurricular activities and community involvement
  • Demonstrated financial need that would otherwise prevent study at UBC
  • First-time undergraduate applicant (not a transfer student)
  • Must meet UBC's English language admission requirement

Benefits and coverage

  • Tuition costs covered based on demonstrated financial need (up to full tuition)
  • Renewable for up to 3 additional years
  • Awarded in combination with the UBC International Major Entrance Scholarship
  • Access to UBC's global alumni network
  • Transition and academic support programs

Required documents

  • UBC online admission application
  • Scholarship application (included in admission application)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Personal profile
  • Documentation of financial need (family income)
  • List of extracurricular and leadership activities

How to apply for the UBC International Leader of Tomorrow Award

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. UBC admission application deadline is January 15; scholarship considered automatically based on admission application personal profile. Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
