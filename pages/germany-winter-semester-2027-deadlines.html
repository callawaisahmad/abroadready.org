Germany Winter Semester 2027: Deadlines & Timeline

🗓️ Sep 30, 2026⏱️ 5 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

Planning a Germany winter semester 2027 start? Most German university deadlines land on 15 July 2027. But the APS certificate, language tests and student visa take months, so start now.

This short guide gives the quick answer, the complete timeline, and what to begin today so you hit the winter 2027 cutoff comfortably.

Germany Winter Semester 2027: The Quick Answer

The winter semester is Germany's main intake. It begins on 1 October, so winter semester 2027 means teaching from October 2027 to February 2028.

According to DAAD, the application phase for the winter semester usually starts in early May and ends on 15 July.

LMU Munich confirms 15 July as the binding deadline for international winter-semester applicants. RWTH Aachen applies the same date to restricted-admission (NC) masters programmes.

Some courses differ. Open-admission masters at RWTH close on 1 March, and many master's programmes run earlier or rolling windows. Always verify the date on your programme's official page before relying on the default.

In practice, this means you can apply anywhere between May and 15 July 2027. Because the processing queue grows through June, treat 15 July as the last possible day, not your target day.

Undergraduate applicants should treat the winter semester as their main shot. Most bachelor programmes in Germany admit almost exclusively for the winter start, so October 2027 is effectively your only chance in this cycle. Master's applicants can also enter in summer 2027, which is why some master's courses fill fast in winter.

What to Do Now for the Winter Semester 2027

Start the long-lead items this autumn. The APS certificate, language tests and proof of funds are what delay applicants, not the application form.

First, apply for the APS certificate as soon as possible. Pakistani graduates must submit documents, pay roughly PKR 35,000-40,000, and wait for processing. From April to July, delays stretch to 8-10 weeks, so the autumn window is your friend.

Second, book a language test. Whether you need TestDaF or an English test depends on the programme. Test seats fill months ahead in Pakistan, and you want room for a retake.

If your programme is taught in German, check which certificate the university accepts first. DSH, TestDaF and Goethe C1 are common, but some tests are offered only a few times a year in Pakistan, so an early booking can save you an entire semester of waiting.

Third, shortlist four to six programmes and note each deadline. Most end on 15 July 2027, but master's courses at some universities close earlier. Read the full logistics in the masters in Germany roadmap.

While that runs, scan every document: degrees, transcripts, passport, APS, language certificates and a motivation letter. uni-assist and most portals need clear PDF scans, and having the folder ready removes a week of last-minute stress in May.

You can also engage uni-assist early. The service reviews documents all year, so you can get a document assessment before the winter 2027 portals even open.

After the 15 July 2027 Deadline

Applications are assessed over the following weeks. Admission letters usually arrive between August and mid-September 2027.

Once you are accepted, open the blocked account and book the German student visa appointment immediately. A Type D visa needs 6-12 weeks, and the queue grows from June to August, so apply in August 2027 at the latest.

Money is the other gate. The visa requires a blocked account holding EUR 11,904, which is EUR 992 per month for twelve months. Opening one takes one to two weeks, so start the day a university confirms your place rather than waiting for the paperwork to peak.

Book the visa slot ahead of time even if you are still waiting on your admission letter. The summer queue at the German embassy is the most common reason students miss the semester start, and a confirmed appointment date protects your plan.

Land two to four weeks before classes start. Complete your anmeldung and university enrollment early, then apply for the residence permit at the Auslaenderbehoerde. See the study in Germany from Pakistan guide for the broader picture.

Every week of preparation in 2026 is a week you will not need in 2027. Students who miss the winter semester are rarely the ones who apply late; they are the ones whose APS or language result arrived late.

So start with the APS certificate this month and book your language test now. When the portals open next May, you will be ready to submit on day one instead of scrambling before 15 July 2027.

Frequently asked questions

Is 15 July 2027 the deadline at every German university?+

No. It is the standard date for most winter-semester programmes, but many master's courses close earlier and open-admission courses can differ. Confirm the date on your programme's official page before finalising your plan.

Do I need APS before applying for the winter semester 2027?+

Yes for Pakistani graduates. The APS certificate must accompany your application, and the year-end queue is shorter, so submit between September and December 2026.

Can I start applying in 2026 for an October 2027 start?+

You can prepare documents and request a uni-assist evaluation, but most winter-semester 2027 portals open around May 2027. Use the early months for APS, language tests and shortlisting instead.
