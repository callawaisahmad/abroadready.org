Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • International student (non-Canadian) requiring a study permit
  • Currently in final year of secondary school or have graduated no earlier than June 2025
  • Demonstrated exceptional academic achievement (high 90s average recommended)
  • Demonstrated leadership and community involvement
  • Creative, innovative and impactful in your school and community
  • Must be nominated by your high school (schools must have a Pearson nomination form)

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees for 4 years
  • Books and course materials
  • Incidental fees
  • Full residence accommodation support
  • Mentorship and leadership development
  • Access to Pearson community and events
  • Pre-arrival support and orientation

Required documents

  • University of Toronto online application for admission
  • Pearson Scholarship application (separate from UofT application)
  • School nomination confirmation
  • Academic transcripts
  • Personal essay
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • List of extracurricular activities and leadership roles

How to apply for the Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. School nomination deadline late September; student application deadline late November; supporting documents early December. Submit a complete application before the December deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
