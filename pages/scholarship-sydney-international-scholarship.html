About this scholarship

Full tuition fees and a living allowance for international research students undertaking a Master's by Research or PhD at the University of Sydney. Master's by Research: up to 2 years; PhD: up to 3.5 years

University of Sydney International Scholarship (USydIS) eligibility criteria

  • International student (not Australian or New Zealand citizen/permanent resident)
  • Applied for and meet admission requirements for a Master's by Research or PhD
  • Excellent academic record (Honours Class 1 or 2A, or equivalent research experience)
  • Have a confirmed supervisor at the University of Sydney
  • Strong research proposal
  • Meet English language requirements (IELTS 6.5-7.0 overall)

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees for the research period
  • Living allowance (stipend, approximately AUD $40,109 per year in 2026)
  • Relocation allowance (up to AUD $5,000 for students moving from overseas)
  • Student health cover (OSHC)
  • Research support and conference travel funding
  • Access to University of Sydney facilities and resources

Required documents

  • University of Sydney admission application (online)
  • Research proposal
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • CV including publications and research experience
  • Supervisor support letter
  • Two academic referee reports
  • English proficiency test results

How to apply for the University of Sydney International Scholarship (USydIS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Rolling with two main rounds: Round 1 closes March, Round 2 closes July. Some faculties have separate deadlines. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
