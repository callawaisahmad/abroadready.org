About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend (KRW 1,000,000), round-trip airfare, accommodation, Korean language training, and medical insurance for graduate study in South Korea. Masters 2 years (including 1 year Korean language); PhD 3-4 years (including 1 year Korean language)

KOICA Fellowship Program (South Korea) eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of a KOICA partner developing country (including Pakistan)
  • Under 40 years of age
  • Bachelor's degree for Masters; Master's for PhD
  • At least 2 years of work experience in a relevant field
  • Strong academic record (GPA 3.0/4.0 or higher)
  • Currently employed in a position related to the programme's focus area
  • Commitment to return to home country after completion
  • English proficiency (IELTS 6.0+ or equivalent)

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Monthly stipend KRW 1,000,000
  • Round-trip economy airfare
  • Free accommodation (on-campus dormitory)
  • Korean language training (1 year)
  • Medical insurance
  • Settlement and resettlement allowances
  • Field trip and cultural activities
  • Dissertation/thesis support

Required documents

  • KOICA online application form
  • Official academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Two recommendation letters (one from employer preferred)
  • Certificate of employment
  • Statement of purpose / study plan
  • English proficiency test results
  • Medical examination form
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the KOICA Fellowship Program (South Korea)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications open in February and close in March. KOICA office in your country may set an earlier deadline. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
