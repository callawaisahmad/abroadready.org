Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM) eligibility criteria

  • Hold a bachelor's degree or be in the final year of bachelor studies
  • Must graduate before the Master's programme begins
  • Open to students of all nationalities worldwide
  • Meet the consortium's specific academic entry requirements
  • Meet language proficiency requirements set by the programme
  • Not have received another EMJM scholarship previously

Benefits and coverage

  • Participation/tuition costs covered by the scholarship
  • Monthly subsistence allowance (approx. €1,400/month)
  • Contribution to travel and installation costs
  • Contribution to visa costs
  • Study in at least two different countries
  • Joint or multiple Master's degree awarded
  • Open to students from anywhere in the world

Required documents

  • Bachelor's degree certificate / transcript of records
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Motivation letter
  • Language proficiency certificate (e.g. English)
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Passport or ID copy

How to apply for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Varies by consortium (typically Oct–Jan) Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
