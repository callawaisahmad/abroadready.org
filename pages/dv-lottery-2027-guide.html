DV Lottery 2027: Enter the US Green Card Lottery

🗓️ Aug 12, 2026⏱️ 12 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

What exactly is the DV Lottery 2027?

The DV Lottery 2027 is the US Diversity Visa programme for fiscal year 2027 — the annual draw that gives out up to 55,000 permanent resident visas (green cards) to people from countries with historically low immigration to the United States. It is completely random: roughly 10 million people enter each cycle and only about 55,000 are selected, which makes it a genuine lottery and the one US immigration route that most applicants can try without an employer, a family sponsor or an investment. This guide covers the DV-2027 eligibility rules, the new passport and $1 fee requirements, how to enter, what happens if you win, and how to avoid the scams that surround the programme.

When does the DV Lottery 2027 registration window open?

DV-2027 covers the US fiscal year running from October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027. As of this guide's publication the registration window had not yet opened — the State Department delayed it while introducing the new entry rules — so the single most important piece of advice is to monitor the official portal and prepare your documents now. Because passports, police certificates and photographs all take time, starting your checklist months early is the difference between entering on day one and missing the cycle entirely.

Key takeaways

  • DV-2027 awards up to 55,000 green cards; entry is free to file aside from a new $1 fee, and registration happens only at dvprogram.state.gov.
  • New for DV-2027 (effective April 10, 2026): you need a valid, unexpired passport at entry and must upload a scan of the biographic and signature pages.
  • Eligibility depends on your country of birth, plus either a high-school education or two years of qualifying work experience in the last five years.
  • You may submit only one entry; multiple entries disqualify you.
  • Results appear only on the Entrant Status Check page; the State Department never emails, mails or phones winners.
  • If selected, the visa must be issued before September 30, 2027, with no extensions.

What is the DV Lottery?

Who created the Diversity Visa programme and why does it exist?

Created by the Immigration Act of 1990, the Diversity Visa programme exists to broaden the mix of nationalities among US immigrants by issuing visas to people from countries that sent few immigrants to the United States in the previous five years. It is a statutory allocation system, not a skills programme: nobody assesses your qualifications, your income or your English, because the draw itself is the selection mechanism.

Which countries can enter the DV Lottery 2027?

Eligibility is decided by country of birth, and the list shifts each cycle. "High-admission" countries whose natives cannot enter include India, China (mainland), Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Brazil, Canada, the Philippines, South Korea, Vietnam and the UK (mainland-born), among others — although individual countries are added and removed as immigration data changes. Because the source list is recalculated annually, always confirm your status against the official DV-2027 instructions on travel.state.gov before you invest time in an entry.

Eligibility for DV-2027

What are the two basic eligibility tests?

You qualify for DV-2027 if you were born in an eligible country and meet one of two standards: either a high-school education or its equivalent (a 12-year course of schooling), or two years of work experience gained within the five years before you apply in an occupation that requires at least two years of training or experience. You only need one of the two — the education route or the work route — and you are free to claim whichever is easier to document.

Can I claim eligibility through a spouse or parent?

Yes. Two family rules expand the eligible pool. If your spouse or your parent was born in an eligible country, you may derive eligibility from that person. Separately, under cross-chargeability, a married couple can usually elect to use the eligible spouse's country of birth, which frequently rescues an application that would otherwise be barred by the high-admission rule.

The new DV-2027 entry rules

Do I need a valid passport to enter the DV Lottery 2027?

Yes. A final rule effective April 10, 2026 changed how entries work, and the headline change is that every entrant must hold a valid, unexpired passport at the moment of entry and upload a JPEG scan of the biographic and signature pages, with a maximum file size of 5 MB. Limited exemptions exist for stateless persons and for nationals of Communist-controlled countries who are unable to obtain a passport.

What does the $1 entry fee cover and how is it paid?

Every DV-2027 entry now carries a non-refundable $1 fee, paid through Pay.gov, with no waivers available for any applicant. Entries submitted without payment are not considered, so have a card ready before you start the form rather than discovering the payment step at the end.

What else changed on the DV-2027 entry form?

The form now asks for your date of birth and sex rather than your age and gender, which removes a common source of form errors because you can no longer miscalculate an age at a future date. The State Department also deliberately delayed the registration window so that applicants have time to obtain passports under the new requirement.

How to enter, step by step

  1. Get a valid passport now and scan the biographic and signature pages as a JPEG under 5 MB.
  2. Wait for the official registration window to open, announced on travel.state.gov and dvprogram.state.gov — do not enter through any third-party site.
  3. Complete the entry form (DS-5501) in one sitting; there is no save-and-return option, so budget about 90 minutes.
  4. Submit exactly one entry per person and keep your confirmation page and unique confirmation number.
  5. After the window closes, check results only on the Entrant Status Check page using your confirmation number, last name and date of birth.

How long should I set aside for the DV-2027 form?

Because there is no save function, allow roughly 90 uninterrupted minutes. Close your email, put your phone on silent, have your passport and photograph open on your desk, and fill in education and work history from documents rather than memory. Every field you can verify against a source document is a field that will not contradict your DS-260 six months later.

If you are selected

Does being selected guarantee me a green card?

No. Selection is not a visa; it is an invitation to apply. You must complete the DS-260 immigrant visa application, attend a consular interview and pay the required fees before a green card is issued, and every one of those steps must be finished before September 30, 2027 or the opportunity expires with no extension.

What will the DV Lottery 2027 visa cost in total?

The core cost is a non-refundable diversity visa fee of $330 per person, payable by the principal applicant and by each family member, plus a separate USCIS immigrant fee before your green card is mailed. Higher case numbers may only become current in the Visa Bulletin near the very end of the fiscal year, so a complete file early is your best protection against the deadline catching you out.

Avoiding DV-2027 scams

Will the State Department email, call or post to me if I win DV-2027?

Never. Winners are never notified by email, post or phone under any circumstances. No one can increase your selection odds, and any communication that claims to be an official notification is false. If you receive such a message, delete it and check the Entrant Status Check page yourself at the official address.

Can I pay an agent to enter the DV Lottery 2027 for me?

No. Any site charging you to "guarantee" entry, or an agent offering to submit on your behalf for a fee, is operating a scam, because the State Department states plainly that entrants should complete the form themselves. You would also be handing over exactly the personal data that identity thieves want.

Which websites are the genuine DV-2027 ones?

There are only two, and both end in .gov. They are dvprogram.state.gov (entry and status) and travel.state.gov (instructions and news). For the wider picture on US immigration and study, see our immigrate to the USA guide and the study in the USA guide.

Who Is Eligible for DV Lottery 2027

Eligibility for the DV Lottery is determined by two factors: your country of birth, and your education or work experience. The programme is designed to diversify the US immigrant population by favouring applicants from countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States.

DV lottery 2027 eligibility: who qualifies?

If your country of birth is excluded, there is one important exception: if your spouse was born in an eligible country, you can claim your spouse's country of birth on your application. This is one of the few ways to work around the country-of-birth restriction, and it requires that you and your spouse are married at the time of entry and that you intend to immigrate together.

Am I eligible for DV lottery 2027?

The education or work requirement is straightforward: you need either a high school diploma (or its equivalent) or two years of work experience within the past five years in an occupation that requires at least two years of training or experience. The US Department of State maintains a published list of qualifying occupations, which spans roles from accountants and engineers to nurses and mechanics. Meeting either the education or the work requirement is sufficient — you never need both.

Can I enter the DV lottery 2027 from inside the US?

A common point of confusion is the belief that the DV Lottery is open only to people living outside the United States. This is not true: you can enter from anywhere in the world, including from within the US on a valid visa. However, if you are selected and are already in the US, you may be able to adjust status to permanent residency without leaving the country, which is a significant advantage.

What documents do you need for DV lottery 2027?

For Pakistani applicants, Pakistan is an eligible country for the DV Lottery, which means Pakistani nationals can enter directly. The selection rate for Pakistani applicants is competitive, and the process is identical to that for applicants from any other eligible country, with the same passport requirement, entry fee and document checklist.

The New DV-2027 Entry Rules and Passport Requirement

DV-2027 introduced two changes to the entry process that every applicant must understand, and both of them reward early preparation.

Why is the passport requirement a big change?

All applicants must now hold a valid, unexpired passport at the time of entry. This was not previously required, and it represents a significant shift, because it means you cannot enter the lottery without first obtaining a passport. If your passport is expired or you simply do not have one, you must apply for one before the entry period opens — which is why the State Department delayed the window in the first place.

How much is the DV-2027 entry fee?

The second change is the $1 non-refundable entry fee, which must be paid by credit or debit card at the time of submission. Previously the DV Lottery was entirely free, and the introduction of a fee — even a nominal one — is designed to reduce the number of fraudulent or duplicate entries. Entries without payment are not considered, so treat the fee as a mandatory step rather than an optional extra.

How long must my DV-2027 passport stay valid?

Both changes apply to all applicants regardless of country of birth. The passport must be valid for at least six months beyond the intended date of entry into the United States, and it must be an ordinary passport — not a diplomatic, official or emergency travel document. If you are entering with your spouse, both you and your spouse must hold valid passports.

What are the DV lottery 2027 requirements?

The entry period for DV-2027 typically opens in early October and closes in early November. The exact dates are announced by the Department of State a few weeks before the entry period begins, and all entries must be submitted inside that window. No late entries are accepted, and there is no way to confirm that your entry was received until the results are announced the following spring.

When should I start preparing for the DV-2027 entry window?

Preparation must begin well before the entry period opens. Applying for a passport can take several weeks, particularly if you need to renew an expired one or apply for your first. Starting this process in the summer, ahead of the October window, ensures you are ready to enter the moment the portal opens rather than watching the deadline pass while you wait for paperwork.

Where do I submit my DV-2027 entry?

The entry is submitted online through the official Department of State website at dvprogram.state.gov. The process is straightforward but demands careful attention to detail, because errors in your entry can result in disqualification even if you are selected.

What documents must I gather before entering?

Start by gathering: a valid passport for yourself and any derivative family members, a recent photograph that meets the specific requirements (digital, 600 by 600 pixels, white background, taken within the last six months), and your education or work experience information. The photograph is a recurring source of rejection — it must be in colour, show your full face, and be free of glasses and head coverings except for religious purposes, while meeting the exact pixel and file-size specifications.

What personal information does the DV-2027 form ask for?

The online entry form collects your full name exactly as it appears on your passport, your date of birth, your country of birth and your country of residence. If you are married and your spouse is eligible, include their information as well, because both of you can immigrate if selected. If you have unmarried children under 21, include them too, because they can accompany you as derivative beneficiaries.

Which DV lottery 2027 tips actually work?

Upload your photograph and pay the $1 fee, then you will receive a unique confirmation number. Save that number immediately and in multiple locations: it is the only way to check your entry status when results are announced. Without a confirmation number you cannot verify whether you were selected, and there is no way to retrieve a lost one.

Should my spouse enter separately?

Yes, if it makes sense for your family. Do not submit more than one entry per person — duplicate entries cause automatic disqualification of all of that person's entries. But if you are married, each spouse may submit a separate entry, and if one is selected the other can immigrate as a derivative. This doubles your family's chances without violating any rule.

How do I avoid photo and technical rejections?

Common technical issues during submission include photograph rejections caused by incorrect dimensions, background colour or resolution. The online tool checks your photo before you submit, but it is worth testing your image with a free online resizer well before the entry period begins so that no last-minute technical problem costs you the cycle.

If You Are Selected: What Happens Next

What is the DV Lottery 2027 deadline after selection?

Selection does not guarantee a green card. It means you are now eligible to apply for one, and you must complete the process within the fiscal year specified in your selection notice. For DV-2027, every step must be finished between October 1, 2026 and September 30, 2027.

How do I complete the DS-260?

The first step after selection is the DS-260 Immigrant Visa application, filed online through the Consular Electronic Application Center. It collects detailed information about your background, education, work history, family relationships and any criminal or security concerns. Completing it accurately and completely is critical, because inconsistencies between your DS-260 and your supporting documents are one of the most common reasons for visa denial.

What supporting documents will I need?

You will need your passport, birth certificate, police certificates from every country where you have lived for more than twelve months since the age of sixteen, educational credentials, work experience documentation and medical examination results. The medical must be conducted by an embassy-approved physician, and the results go directly to the embassy rather than through you.

What DV lottery 2027 interview questions should you prepare for?

After your documents are reviewed you will be scheduled for an interview at the US embassy or consulate in your country of residence. The interview is typically brief and focused on verifying that your information is accurate and that you are not inadmissible for any reason. Common grounds for inadmissibility include criminal history, previous immigration violations and certain medical conditions, though most applicants never encounter these issues.

What happens once my visa is approved?

If your visa is approved, you receive an immigrant visa stamped in your passport, which allows you to enter the United States as a permanent resident. You must enter before the visa expires, typically within six months of issuance. Once you have entered, your green card is mailed to your US address within a few weeks.

Common DV Lottery Scams and How to Avoid Them

What does a DV Lottery selection scam look like?

The DV Lottery attracts a significant number of scams, and the most common is a phishing email or website claiming you have been selected and asking for payment or personal information to "process" your visa. The Department of State never contacts DV Lottery entrants by email, and the only way to check your status is the official entrant status check at dvprogram.state.gov using your confirmation number.

Is it safe to pay someone to enter the lottery on my behalf?

No. These services are unnecessary and sometimes fraudulent. The entry process is simple enough to complete yourself, and the Department of State neither endorses nor partners with any private company for DV Lottery entries. If you pay a service to enter for you, you have no guarantee the entry was actually submitted — and you may have handed sensitive personal information to a dishonest operator.

How do selected-applicant fee scams work?

Some scams specifically target people who have already been selected, sending messages that appear to come from the Department of State and request additional fees, bank account details or copies of personal documents. The State Department does not request payment by email, and it never asks for bank account information or document copies through the entrant status check website. All official communication comes through the US embassy or consulate in your country of residence.

What is the best protection against DV-2027 scams?

Use only the official DV Lottery website, never share your confirmation number with anyone other than your immediate family and immigration attorney, and treat any unsolicited communication claiming to be from the Department of State as suspect. If something feels wrong, navigate to the official website directly rather than clicking links in emails or messages.

Should I trust social media and WhatsApp DV Lottery groups?

For Pakistani applicants in particular, be cautious of social media groups and WhatsApp chains claiming inside information about DV Lottery results or processes. The Department of State does not release results through third parties, so any claim of insider knowledge is false. Always verify information through the official website or the US Embassy in Islamabad.

DV Lottery for Pakistani Applicants: What to Know

Is Pakistan an eligible country for DV-2027?

Pakistan is an eligible country for the DV Lottery, and Pakistani applicants make up a significant share of entries each year. The selection rate for Pakistani applicants is competitive, and the process is identical to that for applicants from any other eligible country, with the same passport requirement, entry fee and document checklist.

What country-specific documents do Pakistani applicants need?

Be aware of a few country-specific considerations. Police certificates are required from every country where you have lived for more than twelve months since the age of sixteen. For most Pakistani applicants this means obtaining a character certificate from the local police station and potentially from the Federal Investigation Agency if you have lived abroad. The process varies, so starting early prevents delays.

Where is the Pakistani DV-2027 medical exam done?

Medical examinations for US immigrant visas must be conducted by an embassy-approved panel physician in Pakistan. The list of approved physicians is maintained by the US Embassy in Islamabad and the Consulate in Karachi, and appointments should be scheduled well in advance, particularly during peak periods.

Where is the DV Lottery 2027 interview held in Pakistan?

Your interview will be conducted at the US Embassy in Islamabad or the Consulate General in Karachi. Both locations process immigrant visa applications, and you will be scheduled at whichever is nearest your residence. The interview itself is typically straightforward for DV Lottery applicants, because the selection process has already established your eligibility. The consular officer verifies documents, confirms that your information is accurate and asks a few clarifying questions.

What will a Pakistani applicant pay after selection?

After visa approval you will need to pay the USCIS Immigrant Fee of $235 per person before your green card is produced, and it is paid online. Planning for this cost, along with travel expenses and initial settlement costs in the US, ensures you are financially prepared for the transition. Many Pakistani applicants also hire an immigration attorney to guide them through the post-selection process, which typically costs between $1,000 and $3,000 but provides valuable reassurance that all documents are in order.

Preparing Your Documents Before the Entry Period Opens

How long is the DV-2027 entry window?

The DV Lottery entry period is brief, typically lasting about thirty-five days from early October to early November, which means you cannot afford to spend that window gathering documents. Everything you need must be ready before the portal opens so that you can complete your entry quickly and accurately.

How do I get a Pakistani passport in time?

The passport is the most time-consuming document to prepare, so if you do not have a valid one, applying should be your first priority. Pakistani citizens apply through the Directorate General of Immigration and Passports, and standard processing typically takes two to four weeks. The passport must be valid for at least six months beyond your intended date of entry into the United States, which for DV-2027 means it should be valid through at least March 2028.

What are the exact DV Lottery 2027 photo requirements?

The photo requirements are specific and unforgiving: 600 by 600 pixels, in colour, with a plain white background, taken within the last six months, with your full face visible and no glasses, hats or head coverings except for religious purposes. Many otherwise eligible applicants are disqualified purely because their photo does not meet these specifications. Taking a compliant photo before the entry period begins — and testing it with the official photo checker tool on the DV Lottery website — eliminates this risk entirely.

How do you apply for DV lottery 2027?

Gather your education and work experience documents in advance. If you are qualifying through education you need your high school diploma or equivalent; if you are qualifying through work experience you need documentation showing that you have worked in a qualifying occupation for at least two years within the past five years. Having these on hand ensures you can complete the entry form accurately without searching for information at the last minute.

What documents do I need for my spouse?

If you are entering with a spouse, you need all of the above for them as well. The passport, photograph and supporting documents for your spouse must be ready before the entry period, because the form requires your spouse's information and a separate photograph. Coordinating this preparation in advance prevents one person's missing document from delaying the entire family's entry.

What should I do if I am not selected for DV-2027?

First, understand that not being selected is the expected outcome, not a reflection of your qualifications or your chances in future years. The DV Lottery is a random draw and there is no way to improve your odds within a single cycle beyond submitting an accurate, complete entry. The most important strategy for unsuccessful entrants is simply to enter again in the next cycle. The DV-2028 entry period will open in the autumn of 2027, and entering every year is the only way to accumulate the statistical probability of eventual selection.

How good are the odds over a decade of annual entries?

Over a ten-year period of annual entries, your cumulative probability of being selected at least once rises to approximately thirty to forty percent, depending on your country's selection rate. That is a meaningful return on a one-dollar annual investment and a few minutes of your time, and it is why experienced applicants treat the DV Lottery as a long game rather than a single shot.

What other immigration pathways should I explore in parallel?

While you wait for future lottery results, you can explore other routes to the United States, including employer-sponsored visas, family-based immigration, the EB-1 and EB-2 green card categories for individuals with extraordinary ability or advanced degrees, and the E-2 treaty investor visa for entrepreneurs from treaty countries. Each pathway has its own eligibility requirements, costs and timelines, and consulting an immigration attorney can help you decide which options are worth pursuing alongside the lottery.

How can I build a backup path through the H-1B visa?

For Pakistani students specifically, the H-1B specialty occupation visa is another route, though it is employer-sponsored and subject to an annual cap and its own lottery. Building your career in a field that qualifies for H-1B sponsorship — such as technology, engineering or healthcare — creates a parallel path to US residency that does not depend on the DV Lottery at all.

Life After the Green Card: Adjusting to Permanent Residency

What are the first practical steps after arriving on a green card?

Winning the DV Lottery and receiving your green card is the beginning of a new chapter, not the end of the process. The first step is obtaining a Social Security number, which you can apply for at a Social Security Administration office after you enter the US.

Do I need a US bank account and a credit history?

Your Social Security number is essential for opening bank accounts, filing taxes, applying for credit and many other daily activities, and the second step is opening a US bank account, which you can usually do with your passport and green card at most major banks. A US account lets you receive direct deposits, pay bills electronically and build the US credit history you will need if you ever want to rent an apartment, buy a car or obtain a credit card.

What are my US tax obligations as a permanent resident?

The third step is understanding your taxes. As a permanent resident you are required to file a US federal tax return every year, reporting your worldwide income, regardless of where you live or work. Failing to file can result in penalties and may jeopardise your permanent residency status, so build the habit from your first tax season in the US.

Can I travel outside the US with a DV green card?

As a permanent resident you can travel outside the US freely, but extended absences of more than six months can raise questions about whether you intend to maintain your US residency, and absences of more than one year without a re-entry permit can result in your green card being considered abandoned. Planning your travel carefully and applying for a re-entry permit for longer trips protects your status.

How long until I can apply for US citizenship?

Permanent residency is a stepping stone to citizenship. After five years of continuous permanent residency you can apply for naturalisation, which grants you full US citizenship and the right to vote in federal elections.

Understanding the Odds: DV Lottery Statistics and Reality

What are the real DV Lottery 2027 selection odds?

The DV Lottery is one of the most misunderstood immigration programmes, and the actual statistics help you set realistic expectations. Each year approximately 10 to 12 million people enter, and approximately 55,000 are selected, so the overall selection rate is roughly 0.5 percent — about one in every 200 entrants.

Do selection rates differ by country?

The overall rate masks significant variation: countries with fewer eligible applicants tend to have higher selection rates, while countries with large eligible populations have lower ones. For Pakistan the selection rate is generally in line with the overall average, though it fluctuates year to year based on entry numbers and the country-specific allocation. It is also important to remember that being selected is not the same as receiving a green card.

Why does the State Department select more people than there are visas?

Of the 55,000 people selected, only a fraction ultimately complete the immigration process, because many are found ineligible at the visa interview, fail to meet the education or work requirement, or simply do not finish within the fiscal year. The State Department deliberately selects roughly twice as many people as there are available visas to account for this attrition, which is why even a selection notice is not a guarantee of a visa.

How should I approach the DV Lottery strategically?

Understanding these statistics helps you treat the DV Lottery as one strategy among many rather than a guaranteed path. Entering every year, preparing your documents in advance and pursuing alternative immigration pathways in parallel all reflect a realistic understanding of the odds and a proactive approach to your immigration goals.

What is the final DV-2027 pre-submission checklist?

Before the DV Lottery 2027 entry period opens, working through a final checklist ensures that your entry is complete, accurate and eligible.

  • Passport check: verify that you and every derivative family member entering with you hold a valid, unexpired passport that meets the eligibility requirements. If any passport needs renewal, complete that well before the entry period opens.
  • Photograph check: confirm that your photo and your family members' photos meet the specifications for dimensions, background, lighting and recency, and test them with the photo checker tool on the DV Lottery website.
  • Fee check: ensure you have a credit or debit card available to pay the one-dollar entry fee for each entry you submit.
  • Information check: verify that your name matches your passport exactly, that your date and country of birth are correct, and that your education or work experience qualifications are accurate and documented. If entering with a spouse, confirm all family information is complete.
  • Timing check: mark the entry period dates, which typically run from early October to early November, and plan to submit on the first day the portal opens, because early submission gives you the whole window to resolve technical issues.
  • Confirmation number check: save your confirmation number in at least two places, including a password manager or a secure file, because it is the only way to check your status when results are announced the following spring.
