Immigrate to Turkey: The 2026 Work Permit & Citizenship Guide

Turkey has two very different doors: a work-permit system for professionals (role-based salary thresholds, an employer-ratio rule, 8 years to a permanent permit) and a citizenship-by-investment program (US$400K property, ~3–6 months, no residence required). This guide covers both, plus the long-term residence route.

The Turkish System in One Paragraph

Turkey's immigration law (Law 6458) separates residence from work. To work, you need a permit (çalışma izni) from the Ministry of Labour — which then doubles as your residence permit. To settle long-term without working, you hold a residence permit (ikamet). After 8 years of continuous legal residence you can apply for the indefinite long-term permit, and citizenship-by-investment bypasses residency entirely for those with US$400K+.

Reality check: the work-permit route is employer-gated and scrutinised — the 2025–26 tightening (income checks, district closures, tourist-permit rejection rates of ~30–40% in big cities) means route choices matter. Property-based permits and the investment route are currently the most reliable for non-employees.

Work Permit (Çalışma İzni) — The Employee Route

Application goes through the Ministry of Labour's e-İzin system, initiated either from a Turkish consulate abroad or (if you hold a residence permit with 6+ months validity) from inside Turkey.

Residence Permit (İkamet) — Staying Without Work

Anyone staying over 90 days per 180 needs a residence permit, applied for online at e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr. Types include short-term (tourist), student, family and property-based permits. The 2026 card fee is TRY 964 plus a nationality- and duration-based permit fee. Mandatory Turkish health insurance applies (minimum coverage raised in April 2025).

2025–26 tightening: tourist-permit renewals are now frequently rejected in big cities (~30–40%), and property-based permits are seeing ~10–15% rejections. Overstaying costs ~US$54/day plus potential entry bans. Renewals need genuine purpose, income evidence and long-stay intent.

Citizenship by Investment — The Golden Passport

Regulation Art. 20 of Citizenship Law 5901 offers Turkish citizenship through investment. No residence requirement, no stay needed — spouse and children under 18 are included at no extra investment. Dual citizenship is fully permitted. Processing runs ~3–6 months via Presidential decree.

Other Routes to Settlement & Citizenship

🃏 Turquoise Card

Turkey's "green card" for highly qualified professionals — indefinite work authorisation on a points-based assessment, no employer sponsorship required.

🌱 Exceptional citizenship

Presidential decree for extraordinary service in science, technology, economics, sport, culture or the arts — no minimum residence period.

📅 Naturalisation (5 yrs)

5 years of continuous legal residence + intention to settle + Turkish language + sufficient income + good conduct. Discretionary.

💍 By marriage

3 years of marriage + continuous residence on the general naturalisation route.

Long-term residence permit (8-year route): after 8 years of continuous legal residence on valid permits, apply for the indefinite long-term (uzun dönem) permit — provided you've had no social-assistance receipts in 3 years, have income, insurance and no public-order issues. Note: student-permit time does not count toward this or toward citizenship residence.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to Turkey?+

For the work-permit route, yes — a Turkish employer must sponsor you and meet the capital/ratio/salary conditions. But you can live in Turkey on a residence permit without working, or obtain citizenship directly through the US$400K investment route with no job and no residence requirement.

Is Turkey's citizenship by investment worth it in 2026?+

It's one of the cheapest citizenship-by-investment programs in the world (US$400K property, ~3–6 months) and includes your family. But sales are cooling as the program is debated in 2025, due diligence has tightened, and the passport's visa-free reach (~107–120 countries) is far below a European passport. Research thoroughly before committing.

Can I convert a tourist permit to something permanent?+

Tourism-based renewals are increasingly rejected in major cities (2025–26). For a genuine long-term plan, apply for a purpose-appropriate permit (work, student, family, property-based) rather than chaining tourist permits — and note that you cannot "convert" a tourist e-visa into a student permit either.

What's the fastest route to Turkish citizenship?+

The investment route (~3–6 months) and the exceptional-service route are the fastest; the residence-based routes take 5–8 years. The Turquoise Card is the fastest route to work rights for highly qualified professionals.

Does studying in Turkey count toward citizenship?+

No — time on a student permit does not count toward the 5-year naturalisation residence requirement nor the 8-year long-term permit. Work-permit time does count toward the 8-year permanent-work-permit route.
