Germany Opportunity Card 2026: Points, Cost & Steps

🗓️ Sep 29, 2026⏱️ 25 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team✅ Verified against official sources

If you are planning a move to Germany for work, the germany opportunity card is the permit that makes it possible without a job offer lined up first. This guide explains the points system, the money you must show, and the real application steps for 2026.

The germany opportunity card, called the Chancenkarte in German, is the official German residence permit for job seekers. It has been available since 1 June 2024, it lasts up to 12 months, and it is now the standard route for people applying from outside Germany.

Two numbers matter most in 2026. You need at least 6 points to be eligible, and you need EUR 1,091 of money for every month of your stay. That works out to EUR 13,092 for a full 12-month period.

This article covers the eligibility rules, a complete points table, the financial proof, the application process, and a 2026 court development that could change timelines for graduates. If you have not studied yet, read our study in Germany from Pakistan 2026 guide first, because studying in Germany creates a stronger profile than applying from abroad with only a home degree.

What is the germany opportunity card?

The germany opportunity card is a residence permit, not a work permit. It allows a qualified person from outside the European Economic Area to enter Germany, look for a job, and then switch to a normal work permit once they have an offer.

The permit is created by Section 20a of the German Residence Act, known in German as the Aufenthaltsgesetz. The rules took effect on 1 June 2024.

Before that date, applicants living outside Germany could only request a job seeker visa if a German employer had already confirmed a position in writing. The new permit removed that employer requirement entirely.

This is the single biggest change for international applicants. You can now enter Germany with no job offer, no sponsor, and no employer letter, as long as you score enough points and prove you can support yourself for the full period.

The official German government overview of the permit is published on the federal portal at Make it in Germany, opportunity card job search. That page is the authoritative source if any figure here conflicts with a third-party summary.

One practical warning. The permit is issued on the basis of a genuine job search. If the authorities conclude that your real intention is tourism, study, or settlement, the application can be refused even when your points score is high.

Do I need a job offer to get the germany opportunity card?

No. A job offer is not required for either route. The whole purpose of the permit is to let you search from inside Germany, where employers can meet you in person and where interviews happen far more often than they do from Islamabad or Karachi.

What you do need is a credible plan. Your documents should show a clear professional direction, and any employment history you list must be real and documented.

A further point about the germany job seeker visa 2026 rules settles a common confusion. The older job seeker visa still exists, but it only applies to people who are already inside Germany, usually graduates finishing a degree. It is not available to applicants living abroad.

So if you are researching a germany work visa from pakistan that requires a confirmed job offer, you have found the wrong permit. The search route asks for points and funds, not for an employer letter.

Who is eligible for the germany opportunity card?

Eligibility runs through two separate routes. Which route you qualify for decides whether points are even calculated for you, and it changes the evidence you must submit with the file.

Route one: your qualification is already recognised in Germany

If you hold a qualification that Germany already recognises as a skilled worker qualification, you can receive the germany opportunity card without going through the points route. No score is calculated in this situation.

This applies when your vocational or academic qualification has already been formally recognised by a German authority, or when it falls under an automatic recognition rule for your country of training.

Pakistani applicants usually fall into the second route instead, because most Pakistani degrees have not yet been assessed for German equivalence at the time of application.

Route two: the 6-point system

Everyone else applies through the points route. You must score at least 6 points out of a theoretical maximum of 16, and you must also clear three basic conditions that cannot be traded for points.

Those basic conditions are the part most applicants overlook. You need a formal qualification, proof of language skills, and proof of sufficient funds. Points are added on top of these, never instead of them.

The minimum language requirement

Everyone on the points route needs at least A1 level German, which is basic German. The alternative is B2 level English, which is a considerably higher standard than A1 German.

In practice, most successful applicants from Pakistan hold B1 or B2 German certificates from the Goethe-Institut. A certificate that is more than two years old is often rejected, so plan your test date carefully.

What counts as a formal qualification

A formal qualification is a completed degree, diploma, or vocational training that leads to a recognised profession. Short certificate courses, bootcamps, and informal training programmes do not qualify.

If you studied in Pakistan, your degree must be verifiable in the German academic database called Anabin. The confirmation you obtain from your own university speeds up verification and prevents delays at the embassy.

Applicants with a strong academic record should also review our guide to how to study in Germany for free. Studying in Germany itself often creates the recognised-qualification route, which is stronger than points alone.

Who cannot use this permit

You cannot apply if you have travel insurance but no funds, if you cannot meet the minimum language level, or if your qualification is not a formal academic or vocational qualification.

People already living in Germany on a residence permit should not apply for a new germany opportunity card from inside the country. For them the correct route is usually an extension of the existing permit, which we cover in our article on the 18-month post-study work permit.

How the germany opportunity card points system works in 2026

The opportunity card points system is a single table of criteria. Each row gives you a fixed number of points for one clear condition, and points from different categories are added together. You only need 6.

There is no interview-based scoring and no discretionary weighting. That makes the scheme unusually predictable for an immigration route, and it means you can calculate your own score before you spend money on applications or certificates.

Qualification points

Qualification is the highest-value category. A degree already recognised as equivalent to a German degree gives 4 points, which clears the 6-point threshold on its own once you add language points.

A degree recognised in your own country but not yet assessed for German equivalence gives 3 points. This is the most common position for Pakistani university graduates who apply before the recognition process has finished.

A two-year vocational qualification recognised in Germany gives 2 points. This route suits applicants who completed a technical diploma rather than a four-year degree.

Shortage occupation points

An additional 1 point is available when your qualification belongs to a German shortage occupation. The official list is maintained by the federal employment agency and covers areas such as nursing, engineering, information technology, crafts, and healthcare.

This point is awarded on top of your qualification points. It is one of the easiest points to claim if your field is listed, so check the current shortage occupation list before finalising your file.

Professional experience points

You receive 2 points for 2 years or more of relevant work experience gained within the last 5 years. You receive 3 points for 5 years or more of relevant experience within the last 7 years.

Experience must relate to your qualification and must be documented. Employer letters, signed contracts, and payslips are the documents that work; undocumented informal work is almost never accepted.

German and English language points

German produces the most language points. A2 gives 1 point, B1 gives 2 points, and B2 or higher gives 3 points. Native speakers cannot collect more than those 3 German points.

One extra point is available for C1 level English or for native English. It can be claimed at the same time as German points, so a bilingual applicant can reach 4 language points in total.

Age points

Applicants under 35 receive 2 points. Applicants aged 35 to 40 receive 1 point. Applicants over 40 receive no age points, although they can still qualify through the other categories.

Previous stay in Germany points

One point is awarded if you previously held legal residence in Germany for at least 6 months within the last 5 years. Tourist entries and visa-waiver stays do not count toward this point.

This criterion rewards applicants who already understand the German system from the inside, which lowers the risk of a first file failing on administrative details.

Partner points

One point is available if your spouse or registered partner also independently meets the permit requirements. You cannot claim it when your partner is travelling only as a dependent with no eligibility of their own.

How many points do I need for the germany opportunity card?

Six points is the legal minimum for the points route. In practice, most successful applicants score between 8 and 12 points, because the threshold by itself does not protect you from weak documentation.

A worked example helps. A 27-year-old with a recognised bachelor degree, 2 years of relevant experience, and B1 German scores 9 points, which clears the requirement with room to spare.

A second example shows the minimum realistic profile. A 38-year-old with a two-year vocational qualification and only A1 German scores 3 points, and the file would be refused. A low language level cannot carry an application on its own.

If your score is 5 or lower, your realistic options are to raise your German level, add documented experience, or wait for your qualification to be recognised and apply under the first route instead.

Score yourself before you commit money. If the total is below 9, treat the language certificate and the recognition process as the two priorities to fix first.

What funds do you need for the germany opportunity card?

The financial requirement for 2026 is EUR 1,091 net per month. Across a full 12-month stay that adds up to EUR 13,092, and you must show the full amount before the visa is issued.

The requirement exists because the permit is built for genuine job seekers. The authorities want to see that you can live in Germany without relying on welfare or undeclared work.

How to prove your money

Two methods are accepted. The standard method is a blocked bank account, known in German as a Sperrkonto or blocked account, held at a German bank. The alternative is a formal declaration of commitment from a person legally resident in Germany who takes financial responsibility for you.

A blocked account is opened with a German bank. You transfer in the full sum, the bank issues a confirmation letter, and that letter goes into your visa file. The money remains locked for the whole 12-month period.

Because the funds stay locked, you plan your monthly withdrawal carefully. You can take out a fixed monthly amount, but the account must still hold enough to cover the remaining period at any point in time.

Why a declaration of commitment is harder

A declaration of commitment is signed by a German resident who is financially strong and willing to cover your costs. Many embassies find it difficult to verify, so it is used far less often than a blocked account.

If a family member is sponsoring you, check the requirements for proof of funds for study abroad from Pakistan in 2026. The documents that satisfy a university are not always the documents an embassy accepts.

What you can do on the germany opportunity card

The permit gives you real working rights rather than a purely theoretical status. You may work up to 20 hours per week across the whole week, including evenings and weekends, as long as the employment is genuine and declared.

You may also take trial work. Employers can hire you for a short trial period known as Probearbeiten, which may last up to two weeks per employer. This is the single most useful provision for graduates, because it lets you prove your skills before either side signs anything long-term.

Trial work is limited to two weeks per employer, but you can repeat it with different companies. Many candidates convert a trial into a contract, and employers who already know your work are far more likely to sponsor you.

Job-search habits that work inside Germany

The single biggest advantage of holding a search permit is access. In-person interviews, networking events, and informal visits to small companies often lead to job offers that never appeared as online listings.

Register with the German federal employment agency within days of arrival. Their placement service is free, and many employers only publish vacancies through it, so an account is effectively mandatory for a serious search.

Keep a written record of every application and interview. When your permit is reviewed later, evidence of genuine effort supports your case for a switch to a work permit or a further residence option.

Build a German CV early. German employers expect a specific two-page format, and a CV written in Pakistani style is frequently rejected before it reaches a human reader.

Do not wait until month eleven to start looking. Interviews, notice periods, and contract review add weeks, and an offer that arrives in the final weeks may be too late to use.

What the permit does not cover

The permit does not let you take a full-time position or work unlimited overtime. Going beyond the 20-hour weekly limit without a proper permit is a legal problem, and it can end your job search early.

You also cannot use the permit as a base for long-term unemployment. If 12 months pass without a suitable job, you must leave Germany unless another route applies.

Can the permit be extended

No. The germany opportunity card cannot be extended beyond 12 months, and there is no renewal inside Germany. This is a firm limit built into Section 20a of the Residence Act, not a matter of discretion.

However, the permit is designed to be temporary and transitional. The moment you secure a qualifying job, you switch to a different permit, which is where the real value of the 12 months lies.

Can you switch to another residence permit

Yes, and you can switch at any time once you have a qualifying job. The common destinations are a skilled worker permit or, if your salary and degree meet the thresholds, an EU Blue Card.

A switch made from inside Germany is usually faster and cheaper than applying from abroad, because your German residence history already supports the application. See the official work visa routes on Make it in Germany for the current Blue Card salary thresholds.

How to apply for the germany opportunity card from Pakistan

Applications from Pakistan are handled by the German Embassy in Islamabad, which covers the whole country for visa purposes. You apply for the national visa first, then collect the residence permit card after arrival in Germany.

This two-stage process is normal for all German visas. The embassy decides whether you may enter the country, and the local foreigners authority in your German city issues the physical residence card once you register an address.

The documents you need

A complete file usually contains a valid passport with at least two blank pages, a detailed CV covering your education and employment, and your qualification certificates with any recognition documentation.

Add proof of your language level, such as a Goethe-Zertifikat or a recognised English certificate. Submit your blocked account confirmation letter or your declaration of commitment, together with health insurance that covers you in Germany for the full stay.

Finally, include a motivation letter that explains why you are looking for work in Germany and what kind of role you want. It should connect your qualification and experience to the German labour market, not repeat your CV.

Where and how to submit

Book an appointment through the German Embassy in Islamabad and submit the file in person or through the embassy appointment system as instructed. Keep printed copies of everything, because originals are often requested at the interview.

The visa fee is approximately EUR 75. The fee is separate from your blocked account, and it is not refunded if the application is refused.

Processing normally takes 4 to 8 weeks from a complete submission. Peak periods around the start of the German academic year can extend this, so apply well before you plan to travel.

Supporting checks

Health insurance must be valid in Germany. Some applicants buy travel insurance by mistake, which is not accepted, because the requirement is a proper health insurance policy covering the entire stay.

German authorities may also request a criminal background check, depending on your profession and the local rules at the time of application. Vocational and healthcare applicants are most often asked for one.

If your file is refused

A refusal always carries a reason. Common causes are a points score below 6, missing language evidence, an incomplete blocked account letter, or documents that do not match the motivation letter.

Using the germany opportunity card after a post-study work permit

The opportunity card for international students is a common misunderstanding, so this section is important if you studied in Germany yourself. The post-study permit and the opportunity card are two different permits with different rules.

After you finish a German degree, you normally receive an 18-month residence permit to look for work. That permit applies only to people who are already inside Germany, and it cannot be combined with a visa that has to be applied for from outside the country.

The correct sequence for work in germany after graduation is therefore fixed. Study first, use the 18-month permit, and only consider the opportunity card if you are outside Germany when you are ready to job-hunt.

What the 2026 court ruling changed

A 2026 court ruling contained an obiter dictum, which is a remark made by a judge that is not part of the binding decision. In that remark, the court indicated that an opportunity card may be granted after the 18-month post-study permit has ended.

If that interpretation is applied broadly, a graduate who used the full 18 months inside Germany could in principle extend the overall job search to roughly 30 months by adding a 12-month opportunity card afterwards.

You must treat this as an emerging legal interpretation, not as settled policy. No formal legislative change has followed it, and each local foreigners authority still applies its own judgement on whether an individual case qualifies.

What to do about it

If you are already inside Germany on a post-study permit, do not assume the ruling guarantees an extension. Ask your local foreigners authority in writing, and keep a copy of the reply.

Time your application so it does not expire while you wait. A short extension request filed before your current permit ends is safer than a fresh application filed afterwards.

What does the germany opportunity card cost in practice?

The official fees are small, but the real expense is the money you must hold in Germany for a year. Understanding the difference between the two prevents a lot of confusion when applicants budget for the move.

The visa itself costs about EUR 75. The blocked account requires EUR 13,092, and that amount stays locked while you are in Germany, so it is money you cannot invest at home.

Common additional expenses

A German language test at Goethe-Institut level costs roughly EUR 150 to EUR 200 per module, and many applicants sit two modules to reach B1. A B2 certificate usually requires an earlier A2 or B1 result, so plan for multiple sittings.

Professional recognition of a qualification through anabin or a recognition body can involve separate fees. Translations and apostilles of Pakistani documents add a further cost that applicants frequently forget.

Health insurance for 12 months must be paid before arrival and is not included in the blocked account figure. Some applicants also budget for a first month of accommodation costs in their target city.

Can you combine the opportunity card with study or part-time work?

No. The permit is designed for a job search, not for study. Using it to begin a German degree would be treated as an abuse of the permit, and the visa can be cancelled at any point.

Working part-time is a different matter. Because the card explicitly allows 20 hours per week, it is compatible with flexible work, and many applicants in this category already balance a job search with delivery, warehouse, or hospitality shifts.

If your plan involves earning while you study, read our guide to part-time jobs for international students abroad. The rules there describe student permits, which differ from the opportunity card.

How does the germany opportunity card compare with a work permit?

A work permit is tied to a specific job and employer. If you lose that job, your permit is usually at risk within a short period, so it suits people who already have a confirmed contract.

The opportunity card is not tied to any employer. That flexibility is its main advantage, and it is why most applicants use it as a first step rather than a final destination.

The flexibility matters if you plan to work in germany after graduation and have not yet received an offer. A search permit lets you attend interviews, take trials, and change direction without restarting the visa process each time.

How to get the germany opportunity card: a realistic timeline

The full process usually takes 3 to 5 months from the day you decide to apply. Most of that time is spent preparing documents rather than waiting for a decision.

Score your points before anything else. If you are below 6, book a German language exam instead of an appointment, because the certificate changes your eligibility.

Open the blocked account next, and remember that bank confirmations can take several working days to arrive by post. Meanwhile, request your Anabin confirmation from your university's examination office.

Submit the file once the language certificate and bank letter are both in hand. Incomplete files are the most common cause of delay, so it is better to wait a week and submit everything together.

After approval, book your flight and travel prepared. Bring your admission letter, blocked account confirmation, health insurance policy, and enough cash for the first weeks before your first income starts.

If you want the full picture of how to get opportunity card germany approval, the sequence is always the same: confirm the points, open the blocked account, gather the certified documents, and submit one complete file. Applicants who follow that order rarely receive a request for additional papers.

Score yourself against the opportunity card requirements 2026 checklist one more time before you book the appointment. If any single line is missing, fix it first rather than hoping the officer will overlook it.

Register your address within the deadline given on arrival, then apply for the residence permit card at your local foreigners authority. Keep every document safe, because you will need them again for tax registration and banking.

What to do if you are refused

A refusal is not the end of the route. Read the stated reason carefully, because most refusals relate to a single missing or expired document rather than to eligibility itself.

If the problem was the points score, you can improve it and reapply, but there is no limit on how many times you may apply. If the problem was the language evidence, a new certificate is usually enough to fix the file.

Frequently asked questions about the germany opportunity card

How many points do I need for the Germany Opportunity Card?

You need at least 6 points on the points system, and 16 is the maximum possible score. Points are added across qualification, shortage occupation, experience, language, age, previous stay, and partner. Most successful applicants score between 8 and 12, because a higher score does not repair incomplete documents.

Can I work on the Germany Opportunity Card?

Yes, but only within limits. You may work up to 20 hours per week and take short trial jobs of up to two weeks per employer. You cannot take a full-time position, and exceeding the weekly limit without a proper work permit is a legal violation that can end your job search early.

How much money do I need for the Opportunity Card in 2026?

You need EUR 1,091 net per month, which is EUR 13,092 for a full 12-month stay. This must be shown through a blocked bank account at a German bank or a formal declaration of commitment. Travel insurance and a Pakistani bank statement do not satisfy the requirement.

How long is the Germany Opportunity Card valid?

The permit is valid for up to 12 months and cannot be extended. If you secure a qualifying job, you switch to a skilled worker permit or an EU Blue Card instead. If you do not find a job in time, you must leave Germany when the period ends.

Can I convert my Opportunity Card to a Blue Card?

Yes, at any time once you hold a qualifying job offer that meets the Blue Card salary and qualification thresholds. The switch is made from inside Germany, which is faster than applying from abroad, and your time on the search permit supports the new application.

The germany opportunity card is one of the most accessible work entry routes Germany has ever offered. It asks for points, proof of funds, and a real plan, and it gives you 12 months inside the country to convert all three into a job.
