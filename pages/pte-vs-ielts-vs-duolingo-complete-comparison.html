PTE vs IELTS vs Duolingo: Complete Comparison 2026

🗓️ Aug 17, 2026⏱️ 14 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

Choosing the wrong English language test can cost you months of preparation time, hundreds of dollars, and in some cases your visa application. Every year, students waste money sitting for a test that their target university does not accept, or that their visa authority will not recognise. The honest comparison nobody gives you is this: which test suits which type of student, which countries actually accept each, and what the real costs are beyond the test fee itself.

In 2026, four major English proficiency tests compete for international students: IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, and the Duolingo English Test. Each has different formats, scoring systems, costs, acceptance levels, and practical implications for your study abroad plans. This guide compares all four in detail so you can make the right choice the first time.

Key takeaways

  • PTE Academic: fastest results (2–5 days), fully computer-marked, accepted by 3,000+ institutions, strong in UK/Canada/Australia
  • IELTS: most widely accepted globally, UKVI version required for UK student visas, face-to-face speaking component
  • Duolingo: cheapest option at USD 65, takes 45 minutes at home, growing university acceptance but NOT accepted for UK student visas
  • TOEFL iBT: accepted by virtually every university worldwide, 2-hour format, strong US recognition
  • For UK student visas: only IELTS for UKVI, PTE Academic UKVI, TOEFL iBT from approved test centres, or Cambridge C1 Advanced are accepted — Duolingo is excluded
  • For USA: all four tests are widely accepted by universities; for visa purposes, check SEVP-approved test centres
  • For Canada: IELTS and PTE Academic are the standard; Duolingo accepted by some institutions but not universally for visa purposes

IELTS Academic / IELTS for UKVI

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) remains the most widely recognised English proficiency test in the world, accepted by over 12,000 organisations across 140 countries. It is the default choice for students applying to the UK, and it is the safest option if you are applying to multiple countries simultaneously.

Duration: 2 hours 45 minutes total. Listening: 30 minutes (plus 10 minutes transfer time for paper-based). Reading: 60 minutes. Writing: 60 minutes. Speaking: 11–14 minutes, conducted face-to-face with an examiner.

Scoring: 0–9 band scale in half-band increments. Most universities require 6.0 to 7.5 depending on the programme. UK visa minimum is typically UKVI B2 level, which corresponds to IELTS 5.5–6.5 depending on the institution.

Results: 3–5 days for computer-delivered test, 13 days for paper-based. Scores are valid for 2 years.

Cost: USD 235–270 globally. In Pakistan: PKR 53,000–62,000. In India: INR 17,000–18,500. In the UK: GBP 215–235. The IELTS for UKVI (required for UK visa applications) costs slightly more at approximately GBP 200–250.

Versions: IELTS Academic (for university admission), IELTS General Training (for immigration/work), IELTS for UKVI (required for UK student visa applications). If you are applying for a UK student visa, you must take IELTS for UKVI at a UKVI-approved test centre — the regular IELTS Academic is not accepted for visa purposes.

Key advantage — face-to-face speaking: Many students perform better in a real conversation with a human examiner than speaking into a computer microphone. The IELTS speaking test feels more natural for students who are comfortable with oral communication and who benefit from interaction and clarification.

Key limitation: IELTS results take longer than PTE or Duolingo. If you are on a tight deadline, the 3–13 day wait can create pressure. The writing section is also the component where most students lose marks, and it requires practice with timed essay and task completion.

PTE Academic / PTE Academic UKVI

PTE Academic (Pearson Test of English) has grown rapidly as an alternative to IELTS, particularly for students applying to the UK, Canada, and Australia. It is entirely computer-marked, which eliminates the subjectivity concern some students have with IELTS human examiners.

Duration: 2 hours. Speaking & Writing: 54–67 minutes. Reading: 29–30 minutes. Listening: 30–43 minutes. The entire test is completed in a single sitting on a computer.

Scoring: 10–90 scale. PTE does not use half-bands like IELTS. Most universities require scores in the 50–76 range depending on the programme. The computer scoring means your results are consistent and predictable — there is no human variability in the assessment.

Results: 2–5 business days, typically within 48 hours for most test-takers. This is significantly faster than IELTS and makes PTE attractive for students on tight application deadlines.

Cost: USD 180–220 globally. PTE Academic UKVI (required for UK visa applications) costs approximately the same or slightly more. The cost is generally lower than IELTS in most markets.

Versions: PTE Academic (for university admission), PTE Academic UKVI (for UK student visa applications). Both versions test the same skills; the UKVI version is administered at UKVI-approved test centres and meets the UK government's security requirements.

Key advantage — fully computer-scored: There is no human examiner involved in scoring any section, including speaking and writing. Your responses are evaluated by Pearson's AI scoring system, which means results are objective and consistent. Many students find PTE scoring more predictable because it removes the variability of individual examiners.

Key limitation: The speaking section requires you to speak into a microphone in a room with other test-takers, which some students find uncomfortable. The fast-paced format also means you need to be comfortable with computer-based testing and quick task transitions.

Duolingo English Test

The Duolingo English Test is the newest major entrant in the English proficiency testing market. It is significantly cheaper and faster than IELTS and PTE, and it can be taken from home, which makes it accessible to students in remote areas or those who cannot easily reach a test centre.

Duration: 45 minutes. The test is adaptive (questions get harder or easier based on your performance), followed by a short video interview and a written sample. The entire test is completed online in a single sitting.

Scoring: 10–160 scale. Most universities require scores in the 100–120 range. The adaptive format means the test can precisely measure your level in a shorter time than traditional tests.

Results: 48 hours. This is the fastest of any major English test, making it ideal for students with very tight timelines.

Cost: USD 65. This is dramatically cheaper than IELTS (USD 235–270) or PTE (USD 180–220). The low cost makes Duolingo an excellent option for students who need to take the test multiple times to improve their score.

Key advantage — take at home: You do not need to travel to a test centre. You can take the test on your own computer at home, which eliminates travel costs, test centre anxiety, and scheduling constraints. This accessibility is a major benefit for students in countries with limited test centre availability.

Key limitations: Acceptance is growing but still more limited than IELTS or PTE. As of 2026, Duolingo is accepted by over 5,000 institutions worldwide, but many universities, particularly in the UK, Europe, and parts of Asia, still do not accept it. Most critically, Duolingo is NOT accepted for UK student visa applications. If you need a UK student visa, you must take IELTS for UKVI, PTE Academic UKVI, or another UKVI-approved test. Duolingo is also not accepted for Australian student visas.

TOEFL iBT

TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) is the oldest and most established English proficiency test, particularly dominant in the United States. It is accepted by virtually every university in the US and by thousands of institutions worldwide.

Duration: 2 hours. Reading: 35 minutes. Listening: 36 minutes. Speaking: 16 minutes (recorded responses). Writing: 29 minutes. The test is completed on a computer at a test centre or at home (TOEFL iBT Home Edition).

Scoring: 0–120 scale. Each section scores 0–30. Most universities require total scores between 60 and 100 depending on programme competitiveness. TOEFL scores are accepted by over 12,000 institutions in 160 countries.

Results: 4–8 days. Faster than IELTS but slower than PTE or Duolingo.

Cost: USD 200–250. The TOEFL iBT Home Edition costs the same as the test centre version and is accepted by most institutions that accept TOEFL.

Key advantage — universal acceptance: TOEFL is accepted by virtually every English-medium university in the world. If you are applying to the US, TOEFL is the safest choice and is sometimes preferred over other tests by US admissions offices.

Key limitation: Slightly longer than PTE and more expensive than Duolingo. The 2-hour format requires sustained concentration, and the home edition, while convenient, requires a reliable computer, internet connection, and quiet environment.

Acceptance by country

The most important factor in choosing your test is whether the country and institutions you are applying to actually accept it. Here is the reality in 2026:

United Kingdom: IELTS (Academic or for UKVI), PTE Academic (including UKVI), TOEFL iBT, and Cambridge C1 Advanced are accepted for university admission. For the student visa, only IELTS for UKVI, PTE Academic UKVI, TOEFL iBT (from approved test centres), and Cambridge C1 Advanced are accepted. Duolingo is NOT accepted for UK student visas.

United States: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, and Duolingo are all widely accepted by universities. For visa purposes, SEVP-approved test centres are required. All four tests are strong options in the US market.

Canada: IELTS and PTE Academic are the standard. TOEFL is widely accepted. Duolingo is accepted by some Canadian universities for admission, but check with the specific institution. For immigration purposes (Study Permit), IRCC accepts IELTS, PTE, and TOEFL.

Australia: IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, and Cambridge C1 Advanced are accepted for both university admission and student visa purposes. Duolingo has limited acceptance and is not universally accepted for visa applications.

Germany: IELTS and TOEFL are the most widely accepted. PTE Academic is accepted by a growing number of German universities. Duolingo acceptance is increasing but check with individual universities.

New Zealand: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, and Cambridge C1 Advanced are all accepted for university admission and visa purposes. Duolingo is accepted by some institutions.

Which test should you choose? Decision framework

Choose PTE Academic if: you are comfortable with computers, want the fastest possible results, and are applying to the UK, Canada, or Australia. The fully computer-scored format provides consistent, predictable results, and the lower cost and faster turnaround make it a strong all-around choice. If you are applying for a UK visa, PTE Academic UKVI is an excellent alternative to IELTS for UKVI.

Choose IELTS if: you prefer face-to-face speaking, need the UKVI version for a UK student visa, or are applying to institutions that strongly prefer or only accept IELTS. IELTS remains the most universally accepted test, and the face-to-face speaking component suits students who communicate more naturally in conversation. See our IELTS guidance for detailed preparation advice and our 6-week study plan for a structured approach to achieving a strong score.

Choose Duolingo if: budget is tight, you need results within 48 hours, you cannot easily reach a test centre, and your target universities accept Duolingo. At USD 65, it is the most affordable option and the ability to take it from home eliminates logistical barriers. However, verify acceptance with both your universities and your visa authority before committing. If you are exploring alternatives to standard tests, see our guide on studying abroad without IELTS.

Choose TOEFL if: you are primarily targeting the United States and want maximum acceptance. TOEFL is the most established test in the US market and is accepted by virtually every American university. The Home Edition provides flexibility for students who cannot easily reach a test centre.

Can I take more than one test?

Yes, and this is a legitimate strategy. Some students take IELTS and PTE to compare their scores and then submit the stronger result. Others take Duolingo as a practice run before sitting the official IELTS or PTE. There is no restriction on taking multiple tests, and you are free to submit whichever score best represents your ability. However, check whether your target universities accept multiple test types — most do, but some may have a preference.

Score validity and timing

All four test scores are valid for 2 years from the test date. Most universities and visa authorities require scores that are no more than 2 years old at the time of application. Some institutions accept scores within 12–24 months, so check the specific requirement. Plan your test date to ensure your scores are valid when you apply — taking the test too early can mean your scores expire before you need them, while taking it too late can delay your application.

Final recommendation

There is no single "best" English test — only the best test for your specific situation. The right choice depends on your target country, your budget, your timeline, your comfort with different test formats, and the specific requirements of your target universities and visa authority. Use the decision framework above, verify acceptance with your specific institutions, and prepare thoroughly for whichever test you choose. Frequently asked questions

Which English test is easier: PTE, IELTS or Duolingo?+

It depends on your strengths. PTE suits tech-savvy students comfortable with computers and faster-paced tasks. IELTS suits students who prefer paper-based writing and face-to-face speaking. Duolingo is shorter and more affordable but accepted by fewer institutions. Many students find PTE scoring more predictable because it is entirely computer-marked.

Which test do UK universities accept?+

UK universities accept IELTS (Academic or for UKVI), PTE Academic (including UKVI), TOEFL iBT, and Cambridge C1 Advanced for admission. For the student visa itself, the UKVI only accepts IELTS for UKVI, PTE Academic UKVI, TOEFL iBT Home Edition (from approved test centres), and Cambridge C1 Advanced. Duolingo is not on the UKVI list.

Is Duolingo accepted for student visas?+

Duolingo is accepted for university admission in many countries (USA, Canada, Australia) but is not accepted for student visas in the UK, and acceptance varies by country. Always check with the specific visa authority before relying on Duolingo. For UK visas, it is not accepted at all.

How much does each test cost in 2026?+

IELTS: USD 235-270 (GBP 215-235 in UK, INR 17,000-18,500 in India, PKR 53,000-62,000 in Pakistan). PTE Academic: USD 180-220. Duolingo: USD 65. TOEFL iBT: USD 200-250. Duolingo is by far the cheapest, and results arrive within 48 hours.

How long are test scores valid?+

IELTS, PTE, and TOEFL scores are valid for 2 years from the test date. Duolingo scores are valid for 2 years for most institutions. Some universities and visa authorities may accept results within 12-24 months, so always check the specific requirement.
