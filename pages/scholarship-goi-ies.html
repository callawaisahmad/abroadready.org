Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship (GOI-IES) eligibility criteria

  • Be a national from outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland, and the UK
  • Study at NFQ Level 9 or 10 (Master's, postgraduate diploma, or PhD)
  • Hold a conditional or final offer from an eligible Irish institution at application
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic achievement
  • Not a previous GOI-IES recipient
  • Russian and Belarusian nationals are ineligible

Benefits and coverage

  • €10,000 stipend for one year of study
  • Full tuition fee waiver for the scholarship year
  • Awarded to study at an eligible Irish institution
  • Prestigious government-backed award
  • 60 scholarships available annually

Required documents

  • Two references uploaded via the online portal
  • Evidence of conditional or final admission offer
  • Academic records/transcripts
  • Personal statement / rationale for studying in Ireland
  • Evidence of extracurricular involvement

How to apply for the Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship (GOI-IES)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Annual deadline is typically 5pm Irish time in mid-March; outcomes announced early June. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
