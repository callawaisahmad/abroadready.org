About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend (KRW 1,000,000/month), round-trip airfare, settlement allowance, Korean language training, and health insurance for Masters/PhD in South Korea. Masters 2 years + 1 year Korean language; PhD 3 years + 1 year Korean language

Korean Government Scholarship Program (GKS) eligibility criteria

  • Citizenship of a GKS partner country (173 countries including Pakistan)
  • Under 40 years of age
  • Bachelor's degree for Masters; Master's for PhD
  • GPA above 80% (or 2.64/4.0)
  • Not a Korean citizen
  • Healthy enough to pursue studies
  • Previously not enrolled at a Korean university

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Monthly stipend KRW 1,000,000 (approx. USD $750)
  • Round-trip economy airfare
  • Settlement allowance KRW 200,000
  • Korean language training (1 year)
  • Health insurance (Korean National Health Insurance)
  • Research support fees
  • Completion grant

Required documents

  • GKS application form (NIIED)
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Statement of purpose
  • Research proposal (PhD)
  • Korean/English proficiency test scores
  • Copy of passport
  • Health certificate
  • GKS university acceptance letter (if applying via university track)

How to apply for the Korean Government Scholarship Program (GKS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Embassy track: typically February-March. University track: varies by university (usually February-April). Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
