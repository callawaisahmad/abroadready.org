About this scholarship

Fully funded government scholarship covering tuition, a monthly stipend, accommodation, health insurance, a Turkish language course and flights. 1 year language course + Bachelors (4-6 yr) / Masters (2 yr) / PhD (~4 yr)

Turkiye Burslari (Turkiye Scholarships) eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of any country except Turkiye (and not a former Turkish citizen)
  • Not already enrolled in a Turkish university at the level applied for
  • Age under 21 (Bachelor's), under 30 (Master's), under 35 (PhD)
  • Minimum academic success: 70% (Bachelor's), 75% (graduate), 90% (health sciences)
  • Graduated or in the final year of current program
  • Required exam/language scores if the chosen program demands them

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees for the whole program
  • Monthly living stipend
  • Free accommodation in state/university dormitories
  • Health insurance
  • One-year Turkish language course
  • Once-off round-trip flight ticket (arrival and departure)
  • Academic and cultural support activities

Required documents

  • Valid passport or national ID
  • Recent photograph
  • Diploma or provisional graduation certificate
  • Academic transcript
  • National/international exam results if applicable (SAT/GRE/GMAT)
  • Language certificates if required (TOEFL/DELF)
  • Research proposal and writing sample (PhD only)

How to apply for the Turkiye Burslari (Turkiye Scholarships)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Application window typically opens in January and closes in late February; verify the annual application calendar. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
