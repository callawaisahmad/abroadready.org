Immigrate to the UK: The 2026 Work Visa & ILR Guide

The UK is a points-adjacent system with no lottery and no per-country caps: find a licensed sponsor, meet the salary bar, and you're in. The path to settlement (ILR) runs through 5 years on a work route. This guide covers every visa, the real thresholds, and how study sets you up for it.

The UK System in One Paragraph

The UK doesn't use a points lottery like the US, nor a transparent ranking like Canada. Instead: a job offer from a licensed sponsor + a qualifying salary + English = a visa. Settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain) comes after 5 continuous years on qualifying routes, then citizenship after 12 months more. There are no annual caps on the main skilled routes — the bottleneck is finding a licensed-sponsor employer.

Biggest leverage for graduates: the Graduate Route (2 years, no sponsor) gives you time to find a Skilled Worker sponsor — and for many roles, your UK degree plus English may satisfy the salary/points requirement through the "new entrant" discount.

Other Routes Worth Knowing

🎓 Graduate Route

2 years after a UK degree (18 months from Jan 2027 for bachelor's/master's; PhD stays 3). No sponsor, no salary requirement, any job. The bridge to Skilled Worker. See Study in the UK.

⭐ Global Talent

For recognised leaders or emerging leaders in academia, tech, arts, medicine — endorsed by a Home Office body. No salary requirement, no cap, leads to ILR faster (3 years). Best for researchers and senior tech talent.

🎯 High Potential Individual (HPI)

For graduates of the top non-UK universities on the Home Office list. 2 years (3 for PhD), no job offer needed — but the Graduate Route requires a UK degree, HPI doesn't.

💍 Family & Ancestry

Spouse of a UK citizen/ILR holder (5-year route), British national by descent, or UK Ancestry (Commonwealth citizens with a British grandparent — 5-year route).

From Student to Settlement: The 5-Year Plan

  1. Study (1–3 yrs): UK degree on a Student visa
  2. Graduate Route (2 yrs): work freely, no sponsor — find a sponsor role paying the going rate
  3. Skilled Worker (3 yrs): switch to a sponsored role; use the new-entrant discount if needed
  4. ILR (at 5 yrs on qualifying routes): apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain — no immigration time limit
  5. Citizenship (+12 months): apply after 12 months of ILR (pass the Life in the UK test + B1 English)

Total: roughly 6–8 years from first day of study to passport — comparable to Canada, faster than the US. Time on the Student visa itself counts toward the residence requirement for citizenship but not toward the 5-year ILR clock.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to the UK?+

For the Skilled Worker route, yes — a sponsored job is mandatory. But the Graduate Route (UK degree), High Potential Individual (top global university) and Global Talent (endorsement) all allow entry without a job offer, buying you time to find sponsorship.

Can I get ILR without a job?+

ILR requires 5 years on qualifying routes; if that route is Skilled Worker you must remain employed (with allowances for unemployment gaps). Routes like family settlement or UK Ancestry let you reach ILR without sponsorship. The Global Talent route reaches ILR in just 3 years.

Is the UK harder than Canada or Australia?+

Different: the UK has no points ranking, so it's not harder, just different — it rewards finding a sponsor more than raw points. Canada/Australia reward points first. The UK's salary threshold (£41,700) is a real hurdle for junior roles; Canada's points system favours younger candidates. Compare all in our immigration hub.

What's the fastest route to UK settlement?+

Global Talent: ILR after 3 years with an endorsement (researchers, exceptional tech talent). Otherwise the standard path is 5 years (Skilled Worker/HPI/family), plus 12 months for citizenship. Recent White Paper proposals aim to introduce "earned settlement" rules — keep an eye on changes.

Does studying in the UK count toward ILR?+

Not toward the 5-year ILR clock (only work-route years count). But Student visa time counts toward the total residence requirement for citizenship (5 of the 6 years, 12-month absence rule). The Graduate Route's 2 years do count toward ILR only if you then hold a qualifying work visa for the remaining 3.
