Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree (EMJMD) - European Union eligibility criteria

  • Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in a relevant field. English proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ / TOEFL 90+). Not previously awarded an Erasmus Mundus scholarship. Meet specific programme requirements.

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees covered
  • Monthly allowance €1,400 (contribution to living costs)
  • Travel cost allowance
  • Installation costs allowance
  • Health insurance for the entire duration
  • Study at 2-3 European universities
  • Networking across EU countries

Required documents

  • Online application via the EMJMD website
  • Bachelor's degree and transcripts
  • English proficiency certificate
  • Two recommendation letters
  • CV/Resume
  • Motivation letter
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree (EMJMD) - European Union

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Most programmes have deadlines between October and January. Check individual programme pages on eacea.ec.europa.eu. Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
