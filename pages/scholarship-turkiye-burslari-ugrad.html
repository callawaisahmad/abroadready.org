About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend ($700-$950), round-trip airfare, accommodation, health insurance, and 1 year Turkish language training for all degree levels. Bachelors 4 years; Masters 2 years; PhD 3-4 years (plus 1 year Turkish language)

Türkiye Burslari (Turkish Government Scholarship) eligibility criteria

  • Non-Turkish citizen
  • Not already studying in Turkey
  • Under 21 for Bachelors, under 30 for Masters, under 35 for PhD
  • Minimum GPA 75% (for Bachelors 75%, Masters/PhD 80%)
  • Healthy enough to study
  • Not previously received a Türkiye Burslari scholarship

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Monthly stipend: Bachelors $700, Masters $850, PhD $1,200
  • Round-trip economy airfare
  • Free accommodation (dormitory)
  • Health insurance
  • 1 year Turkish language training
  • Settlement allowance

Required documents

  • Online application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Passport copy
  • Photo (biometric)
  • English/Turkish proficiency certificate
  • Recommendation letters (Masters/PhD)
  • Statement of purpose
  • Medical report

How to apply for the Türkiye Burslari (Turkish Government Scholarship)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications open in January and close in February 20. Results announced in July-August. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
