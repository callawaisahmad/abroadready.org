Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA) eligibility criteria

  • Non-Singaporean citizen
  • Strong academic record (upper second class honours or better)
  • Bachelor's or Master's in STEM field
  • Good English proficiency
  • Not already holding a doctoral degree
  • Research experience preferred

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Monthly stipend SGD $2,200-$2,700 (increases over PhD years)
  • One-time return airfare (up to SGD $1,500)
  • Settlement allowance SGD $1,000
  • Visa application fees
  • Health insurance
  • Conference travel support

Required documents

  • SINGA online application
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Statement of research interests
  • CV/Resume
  • English proficiency test scores
  • Copy of passport
  • Research proposal (optional but recommended)

How to apply for the Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Two main rounds: January 1 deadline for August intake, June 1 deadline for January intake. Submit a complete application before the June deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
