Study in Australia: The Complete 2026 Guide

All eight Group of Eight universities sit in the world's top 100, Australia's post-study visa (485) gives 2–4 years of unlimited work, and the points system genuinely rewards Australian degrees (extra 5–10 points toward PR). The cost: top-tier tuition (AUD $34K–58K/yr) and a visa regime that has tightened hard since 2024.

Why Study in Australia?

Australia pairs a genuinely world-class university sector with the most migration-friendly post-study system of the English-speaking majors. Study there and you gain: 2–4 years of unlimited work on the 485 visa, up to 5–10 extra PR points (Australian degree, regional study, STEM), and professional-year/community-language bonuses most competitors can't offer. It's expensive, but it's a rare country where education is a direct pipeline to permanent residence.

Heads-up: costs have surged — the student visa fee jumped from $1,600 to $2,500 by July 2026, the living-cost benchmark is $29,710/yr, and Genuine Student scrutiny is tough for high-risk nationalities. The 485 no longer extends freely (regional only) and its max age is now 35.

Affordable & Easy-Entry Universities

Skip the Go8 and you can save ~$8–15K/year without giving up the same 485 work rights and PR points. Regional universities — Charles Sturt, USQ, UNE, Federation — charge AUD 25–31K/year, accept ATARs in the 60s–70s, and their campuses strengthen your regional-migration eligibility. Most run scholarships worth 10–15% of tuition.

Degrees, Intakes & Admission

🎓 Bachelor's — 3 years

4 for Honours/some engineering. Entry: Year 12/IB/A-levels (IB ~30–38 typical at Go8); no SAT/ACT generally. IELTS 6.5 (6.0 bands) typical.

📘 Master's — 1.5–2 years

Coursework master's 1.5–2 yrs; research 2. Entry: recognised bachelor's (~65–70% for Go8), transcripts, CV, references, English. MBA needs GRE/GMAT + experience.

🔬 PhD — 3–4 years

Research degrees get unlimited work rights; scholarships (RTP, university stipends) cover tuition + living. Strong research infrastructure.

🗓️ Intakes

Main intakes February & July; UNSW runs trimesters (Feb, Jun, Sep). Apply 6–12 months ahead (offers are rolling). Planning level 2026–27: 295,000 new overseas students.

After Graduation: The 485 Visa & PR Points

The Temporary Graduate visa (485) is your bridge to skilled PR — and a key reason students pick Australia.

Post-Vocational EducationUp to 18 monthsAssociate degree/diploma/trade + skills assessment

  • Unlimited work rights; fee from $5,750 (July 2026); apply within 6 months of student visa expiry; age ≤35
  • Time on 485 doesn't count toward 189/190 PR directly — but the experience and sponsor bridge do
  • Australian degree = +5 points; regional study +5; STEM master's/PhD +10; Professional Year +5; NAATI language +5
Reality: since July 2024 the 485 is a shorter bridge, not an open-ended holding visa (lodgements fell ~30% in 2024–25). Your real PR target is the Skilled Independent (189), Skilled Nominated (190), or employer-sponsored (482→186) routes. See Immigrate to Australia.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do I need for an Australian student visa?+

You must show first-year tuition + AUD $29,710 living costs + travel + OSHC for a single student (partner +$10,394, each child +$4,449). Funds need to be genuinely available — 3 months of bank history is safest, and unexplained lump sums raise Genuine Student flags.

Can I work while studying in Australia?+

Yes — up to 48 hours per fortnight while your course is in session, unlimited during scheduled breaks, and unlimited anytime for masters-by-research and PhD students. The 485 visa afterwards removes all work limits entirely.

Is Australia cheaper than the UK or US?+

Tuition is comparable to the US/UK top tier (AUD $34K–58K/yr at Go8), and living costs in Sydney/Melbourne are high. The value is different: Australia's post-study (485) and PR-point advantages are unmatched, which can make the total cost worthwhile if settlement is your goal.

Can I get PR after studying in Australia?+

Yes — this is Australia's biggest draw. Your degree earns PR points (5 for an Australian degree, +5 regional, +10 STEM master's/PhD), the 485 gives you work experience to top up, and occupations on the skilled lists (nurses, teachers, engineers, IT) regularly get invited. Realistic 2026 invite scores are ~85–95 for most professionals.

When should I apply for an Australian student visa?+

Apply 6–16 weeks before your course start — but for EL3 nationalities (India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan) budget longer for manual verification. Get your CoE from a CRICOS-registered provider first; it's the backbone of your application.
