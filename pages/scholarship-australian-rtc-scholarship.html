About this scholarship

Full tuition offset, living stipend (AUD $32,000-$40,000/year), and health insurance for research-based Masters and PhD in Australia. Masters 1-2 years; PhD 3-4 years

Australian Research Training Program (RTP) eligibility criteria

  • International student (non-Australian citizen/permanent resident)
  • Strong academic record (Honors 1 or equivalent for PhD; Honors 2A for Masters)
  • Meet the entry requirements of the chosen university and research degree
  • Have a supervisory agreement with a potential supervisor
  • Not already holding an equivalent research degree

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fee offset
  • Living stipend AUD $32,000-$40,000 per year (tax-free)
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)
  • Relocation allowance (in some cases)
  • Research training support fund
  • Conference travel support

Required documents

  • University application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Research proposal
  • Supervisory agreement letter
  • Two academic references
  • English proficiency test scores (IELTS 6.5+)
  • CV/Resume
  • Publications list (if any)

How to apply for the Australian Research Training Program (RTP)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by university; most round 1 deadlines are around September-October for the following year. Some universities have rolling admissions. Submit a complete application before the September deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
