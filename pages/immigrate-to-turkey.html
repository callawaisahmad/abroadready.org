Immigrate to Turkey: The 2026 Work Permit & Citizenship Guide

Turkey has two very different doors: a work-permit system for professionals (role-based salary thresholds, an employer-ratio rule, 8 years to a permanent permit) and a citizenship-by-investment program (US$400K property, ~3–6 months, no residence required). This guide covers both, plus the long-term residence route.

The Turkish System in One Paragraph

Turkey's immigration law (Law 6458) separates residence from work. To work, you need a permit (çalışma izni) from the Ministry of Labour — which then doubles as your residence permit. To settle long-term without working, you hold a residence permit (ikamet). After 8 years of continuous legal residence you can apply for the indefinite long-term permit, and citizenship-by-investment bypasses residency entirely for those with US$400K+.

Reality check: the work-permit route is employer-gated and scrutinised — the 2025–26 tightening (income checks, district closures, tourist-permit rejection rates of ~30–40% in big cities) means route choices matter. Property-based permits and the investment route are currently the most reliable for non-employees.

Work Permit (Çalışma İzni) — The Employee Route

Application goes through the Ministry of Labour's e-İzin system, initiated either from a Turkish consulate abroad or (if you hold a residence permit with 6+ months validity) from inside Turkey.

Residence Permit (İkamet) — Staying Without Work

Anyone staying over 90 days per 180 needs a residence permit, applied for online at e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr. Types include short-term (tourist), student, family and property-based permits. The 2026 card fee is TRY 964 plus a nationality- and duration-based permit fee. Mandatory Turkish health insurance applies (minimum coverage raised in April 2025).

2025–26 tightening: tourist-permit renewals are now frequently rejected in big cities (~30–40%), and property-based permits are seeing ~10–15% rejections. Overstaying costs ~US$54/day plus potential entry bans. Renewals need genuine purpose, income evidence and long-stay intent.

Citizenship by Investment — The Golden Passport

Regulation Art. 20 of Citizenship Law 5901 offers Turkish citizenship through investment. No residence requirement, no stay needed — spouse and children under 18 are included at no extra investment. Dual citizenship is fully permitted. Processing runs ~3–6 months via Presidential decree.

Other Routes to Settlement & Citizenship

🃏 Turquoise Card

Turkey's "green card" for highly qualified professionals — indefinite work authorisation on a points-based assessment, no employer sponsorship required.

🌱 Exceptional citizenship

Presidential decree for extraordinary service in science, technology, economics, sport, culture or the arts — no minimum residence period.

📅 Naturalisation (5 yrs)

5 years of continuous legal residence + intention to settle + Turkish language + sufficient income + good conduct. Discretionary.

💍 By marriage

3 years of marriage + continuous residence on the general naturalisation route.

Long-term residence permit (8-year route): after 8 years of continuous legal residence on valid permits, apply for the indefinite long-term (uzun dönem) permit — provided you've had no social-assistance receipts in 3 years, have income, insurance and no public-order issues. Note: student-permit time does not count toward this or toward citizenship residence.

Tourist & Visitor Visa: E-Visa vs Visa-Free Entry

Turkey splits short visitors into three groups: visa-free nationalities, e-visa nationalities and consular-visa nationalities. Most travellers fall into the first two.

Business Visa (Short Business Trips)

Short business trips — meetings, conferences, negotiations and trade fairs — can use the e-visa for business purposes (same fee and 180-day validity) or a sticker visa where your nationality isn't on the e-visa list.

📋 Requirements E-visa / sticker

E-visa: form + payment only, no invitation letter needed. Sticker visa: invitation letter from the Turkish company, employer's letter, bank statements, travel insurance.

⏱ Duration 90/180

Same as tourism — up to 90 days in any 180-day period (30 days per entry for some nationalities), with the visa valid for 180 days from issue.

🚫 No local work Restriction

Business visits permit meetings and negotiations only — no Turkish employment or paid work. Real assignments need a work permit (çalışma izni) via your Turkish employer.

💼 Longer assignments Work permit

Working in Turkey for weeks or months requires the employer-sponsored work permit — see the work-permit section above.

Work & Stay Routes Beyond the Standard Permit

Beyond the classic employer work permit, Turkey offers several specialist statuses — some of which beat the standard route outright.

🃏 Turquoise Card Indefinite

Turkey's "green card" for highly qualified professionals — points-based, no employer sponsorship, indefinite work authorisation plus the holder's family. The fastest route to independent work rights (see "Other Routes" above).

💼 Self-employment permits Company route

Foreigners who establish or partner in a Turkish company can apply for a work permit as owner/manager — capital, job-creation and turnover conditions apply, and the 5:1 foreign-worker ratio is eased for substantial investments.

💻 Digital Nomad Visa (2024) Remote work

For remote workers aged 21–55 with a university degree, foreign-source income of at least US$3,000/month (US$36,000/yr) and no Turkish clients. Get a Digital Nomad Identification Certificate on the GoTürkiye portal, then a visa at the consulate; longer stays convert to a renewable digital-nomad residence permit.

🔵 Blue Card (Mavi Kart) Re-integration

For former Turkish citizens who renounced citizenship: restores most citizen rights — work, residence, inheritance, property — without needing permits, while they keep their foreign passport.

🛡 Remote-work exemption No permit needed

Working remotely for a foreign employer with no Turkish activity does not require a Turkish work permit — that's exactly what the digital-nomad route formalises.

💍 Work permit via marriage Spouse route

Spouses of Turkish citizens can apply for work permits under a simplified procedure; marriage of 3+ years with residence also feeds the naturalisation clock (see "Other Routes").

Investor & Entrepreneur Residence

Beyond citizenship-by-investment (above), investors can hold residence first — and build toward long-term status without applying for citizenship.

🏠 Property-based residence US$200K+

Buying real estate (title deed in your name, government-licensed valuation) opens a short-term residence permit — minimum property value US$200,000 since Oct 2023. Issued up to 2 years, renewable.

🏢 Investor short-term permit 5 years

Large investments attested by the Council of Ministers (e.g. a US$500,000 private pension-fund deposit) can earn the investor and family a 5-year short-term permit — and feed citizenship/long-term residence.

🏗 Entrepreneur work permits Company

Founders and partners of Turkish companies apply for their own work permits (eased ratio rules for substantial investment) — see "Work & Stay Routes" above.

🛂 Citizenship by investment US$400K

The fast track: US$400,000 property (or US$500,000 deposit/bonds/funds, or 50 hires) → citizenship in ~3–6 months with no residence requirement. Covered in full above.

Special Categories: Retirees, Medical Stays & Humanitarian

👴 Retired residence permit No dedicated visa

Turkey has no separate "retirement visa" — retirees use the short-term residence permit with proof of a regular pension/income (commonly ~€500–600/month, or ~1× the net minimum wage), health insurance and an address. Issued 1–2 years, renewable indefinitely.

🏥 Medical treatment stays Short-term ikamet

Medical tourism (a major reason people choose Turkey) is covered by a short-term residence permit for treatment — usually up to 1 year, renewable with a doctor's report and proof of funds.

🕊 Humanitarian & trafficking Protection

Victims of human trafficking receive a humanitarian residence permit (renewable, with support) under Law 6458, and those with protection needs can access the temporary/international protection framework.

🕐 Long-term residence (8 yrs) Indefinite

After 8 years of continuous legal residence (no student time, no long absences), the long-term permit is indefinite and allows residence without renewal — see "Other Routes" above.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to Turkey?+

For the work-permit route, yes — a Turkish employer must sponsor you and meet the capital/ratio/salary conditions. But you can live in Turkey on a residence permit without working, or obtain citizenship directly through the US$400K investment route with no job and no residence requirement.

Is Turkey's citizenship by investment worth it in 2026?+

It's one of the cheapest citizenship-by-investment programs in the world (US$400K property, ~3–6 months) and includes your family. But sales are cooling as the program is debated in 2025, due diligence has tightened, and the passport's visa-free reach (~107–120 countries) is far below a European passport. Research thoroughly before committing.

Can I convert a tourist permit to something permanent?+

Tourism-based renewals are increasingly rejected in major cities (2025–26). For a genuine long-term plan, apply for a purpose-appropriate permit (work, student, family, property-based) rather than chaining tourist permits — and note that you cannot "convert" a tourist e-visa into a student permit either.

What's the fastest route to Turkish citizenship?+

The investment route (~3–6 months) and the exceptional-service route are the fastest; the residence-based routes take 5–8 years. The Turquoise Card is the fastest route to work rights for highly qualified professionals.

Does studying in Turkey count toward citizenship?+

No — time on a student permit does not count toward the 5-year naturalisation residence requirement nor the 8-year long-term permit. Work-permit time does count toward the 8-year permanent-work-permit route.

Can I switch from a tourist visa to work or residence in Turkey?+

A tourist e-visa cannot be "converted" into a work or student permit — the purpose of stay must match the permit. For residence you can apply for a purpose-appropriate ikamet (short-term, family, property-based) within your legal stay; for work, your Turkish employer initiates the e-İzin application. Remote workers can get the Digital Nomad Identification Certificate and then a residence permit while in Turkey.

Do I need a separate visa for business meetings in Turkey?+

No. The e-visa covers tourism and business purposes, and visa-free nationals attend meetings visa-free. A consular sticker visa is only needed for nationalities outside the e-visa and visa-free lists. Actual work still requires a work permit.

Is there a retirement or digital-nomad route for Turkey?+

Yes to the digital-nomad route (since 2024): ages 21–55, a university degree and foreign-source income of at least US$3,000/month, with the certificate-first process on the GoTürkiye portal. Retirees have no dedicated visa — they use the renewable short-term residence permit with proof of a regular pension.
