Fulbright Foreign Student Program eligibility criteria

  • Citizen/resident of a participating country, residing there at time of application
  • Not a U.S. citizen or permanent resident (dual U.S. citizens ineligible)
  • Hold the equivalent of a U.S. bachelor's degree with a strong academic record
  • Demonstrated English proficiency (e.g., TOEFL iBT 79-80+ or IELTS 6.5+)
  • Apply through home-country Fulbright Commission/Foundation or U.S. Embassy
  • Cannot pursue clinical training programs (medicine, dentistry, nursing, etc.)

Benefits and coverage

  • Tuition and academic fees funding
  • Living/maintenance stipend
  • Round-trip international travel
  • Health benefit plan (accident & sickness coverage)
  • J-1 exchange visitor visa sponsorship
  • Enrichment activities and seminars

Required documents

  • Completed Fulbright application form
  • Academic transcripts/records
  • English proficiency test scores (TOEFL/IELTS)
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Statement of purpose / study objective
  • Standardized test scores as required by target universities (e.g., GRE/GMAT)

How to apply for the Fulbright Foreign Student Program

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by country and are set by your home-country Fulbright Commission/Foundation or U.S. Embassy (typically spring-fall of the year before intake). Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
