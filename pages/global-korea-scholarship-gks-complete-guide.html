Global Korea Scholarship (GKS): The Complete Guide

🗓️ May 26, 2026⏱️ 12 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

What is the Global Korea Scholarship?

The Global Korea Scholarship, often abbreviated as GKS and formerly known as the Korean Government Scholarship Program, is South Korea's flagship fully funded scholarship for international students. Administered by the National Institute for International Education, it supports undergraduate and graduate study at universities across Korea.

What does GKS make possible?

GKS can cover the major financial barriers to studying in Korea, including tuition, living costs, airfare, medical insurance, and a year of Korean language training. For students who want a Korean degree without the full financial burden, it is one of the most important programmes to understand. It is generous, well organised, and open to applicants from many countries.

Key takeaways

What are the most important GKS facts?

  • The Global Korea Scholarship is a fully funded Korean government scholarship for undergraduate and graduate study.
  • It covers tuition, a monthly living stipend, airfare, medical insurance, and a year of Korean language training.
  • You apply through one of two tracks: the Embassy track or the University track.
  • You can choose only one track and, in most cases, apply to a limited number of universities.
  • A settlement allowance and a degree completion grant are also part of the package.

What the Global Korea Scholarship covers

How comprehensive is the scholarship package?

GKS is one of the most complete scholarship packages available to international students. Its standard package generally includes full tuition, a monthly living allowance, a settlement allowance on arrival, medical insurance, and round-trip airfare.

Does GKS include language study and completion support?

Yes. Recipients receive one year of Korean language training at a designated institution before beginning their degree, plus a degree completion grant when they finish. These benefits make the Global Korea Scholarship more than a tuition waiver.

Do graduate scholars receive any extra benefits?

Graduate students may receive a research allowance. Scholars who achieve a high level of Korean proficiency may also qualify for an additional language proficiency grant, depending on the year's official conditions.

Are the benefit amounts the same every year?

No. Exact figures are reviewed and may change, so always confirm the current benefits in the official application guidelines for your application year and study level.

Undergraduate and graduate tracks

What degree levels does GKS support?

The Global Korea Scholarship is divided into two main degree levels—undergraduate and graduate—each with its own eligibility guidance, application procedures, and timeline. Your academic background and intended next step should determine which level fits you.

What does Undergraduate GKS fund?

The undergraduate track funds a full bachelor's degree in Korea and is normally preceded by one year of Korean language study. It is intended for students who have completed or will complete secondary education and want to pursue their entire undergraduate degree in Korea.

What does Graduate GKS fund?

The graduate track funds master's and doctoral degrees and, in some cases, research programmes, generally with one year of Korean language study beforehand. It is designed for applicants who hold or are completing a relevant prior degree and want to continue their studies in Korea.

The two application tracks

What are the two GKS application routes?

The two main routes are the Embassy track and the University track. Choosing between them is one of the most important decisions in your Global Korea Scholarship application, and you may apply through only one route in a given cycle.

How does the Embassy track work?

In the Embassy track, you apply through the Korean embassy in your home country. The embassy conducts the first round of selection, and successful candidates then apply to a limited number of universities. This route is often chosen by applicants who want flexibility and are comfortable with a strong, country-level competition.

How does the University track work?

In the University track, you apply directly to a participating Korean university. The university conducts its selection process and recommends you to the programme. It can suit applicants with a particular university or programme in mind, especially graduate students seeking a specific department or supervisor.

Can I apply through both tracks?

No. Applying through both the Embassy and University tracks is not permitted. You must select one route, follow its rules, and meet its deadlines rather than combining the two processes.

How to choose your track

Which GKS track is better?

There is no universally better route. The Embassy track offers national competition and, for some applicants, clearer procedures, while the University track lets you target a particular institution and, at graduate level, build a relationship with a department.

When is the Embassy track the better choice?

The Embassy track may suit you if you value flexibility across universities and are confident in a broad selection process. You will be competing for places within your home country's allocation, so a polished, competitive application is essential.

When is the University track the better choice?

The University track may fit better when a particular university, professor, or graduate department is central to your plans. Universities set their own internal requirements and deadlines within the overall programme framework, so you must research those details carefully.

How should I make the final decision?

Compare your priorities, academic fit, target universities, and the level of competition you understand. Read the official guidelines for your chosen track because documents and deadlines differ. You can explore participating universities and related awards on our scholarship results page.

Eligibility essentials

Who is generally eligible for GKS?

Detailed criteria are published each year, but applicants generally must hold the nationality of an eligible country, must not hold Korean citizenship, and must meet the age guidelines for their chosen degree level. They also normally need a minimum academic result and good health.

What academic performance is required?

The Global Korea Scholarship commonly expresses minimum academic performance as a grade point average or percentile threshold. The precise standard can vary by nationality, degree level, and selection route, so use the current official checklist rather than an old threshold.

Does meeting the minimum requirements guarantee selection?

No. Meeting the baseline only makes you eligible to be considered. Because selection is competitive, assessors evaluate the complete profile, including your academic record, motivation, chosen field, documents, and overall fit.

When should I confirm my eligibility?

Confirm nationality, age, degree level, academic standing, health, and any prior Korean government scholarship history before investing heavily in the application. Catching an ineligibility issue early gives you time to choose another funding route.

Building a strong GKS application

Why does preparation matter for GKS?

The Global Korea Scholarship attracts applicants from many countries, so a thoughtful and complete application is essential. Strong academics open the door, but your statements, recommendations, document choices, and programme fit help determine whether you stand out.

How should I write my personal statement?

Your personal statement or statement of purpose should explain your motivation for studying in Korea, your academic and career goals, and why the selected programme is a genuine fit. Be specific and authentic rather than generic. Our statement of purpose builder can help you organise a clear, persuasive narrative.

What should a study or research plan show?

A study or research plan should demonstrate focus, feasibility, and a clear connection between your preparation and your intended work. Graduate applicants should align it with the strengths of the target programme and, where relevant, a potential supervisor.

Who should write my recommendation letters?

Choose referees who know your work well and can speak specifically about your abilities, character, and potential. Give them enough time, the correct guidance, and relevant details so they can write a detailed letter rather than a vague endorsement.

How should I handle required documents?

GKS applications may require forms, transcripts, certificates, statements, recommendation letters, and proof of language ability. Some records must be apostilled or notarised, which can take time. Follow the checklist for your selected track exactly and start early.

Does Korean or English ability strengthen an application?

You do not need Korean fluency to apply because GKS includes a year of language training. However, Korean or recognised English proficiency can demonstrate readiness, and a qualifying Korean level may unlock additional benefits after enrolment.

A realistic timeline

How often is the GKS application offered?

The Global Korea Scholarship runs on an annual cycle. The Embassy track usually opens earlier in the year, while University track timing varies by institution. Broadly, applications open in the first half of the year, selection follows, and language training or enrolment begins later that year or the following spring.

How early should I begin?

Begin months before the application window because transcripts, translations, legalisation, recommendations, and medical paperwork all take time. Researching universities and programmes early also gives your written application enough time to become specific.

What if I am unsure which route or degree fits?

Compare the official criteria before committing, and seek a grounded second opinion rather than choosing by assumption. Our AI advisor can help you weigh the options while you continue verifying every final detail with official sources.

Understanding the quota system

How do national quotas shape selection?

GKS operates through quota allocations, so competition depends partly on where you apply. Each country receives a number of Embassy-track places, while participating universities receive separate allocations under the University track.

Who competes in the Embassy track?

In the Embassy track, you compete with other applicants from your own country for that country's places. This makes the national shortlist and the number of available scholarships important when judging your chances.

Who competes in the University track?

In the University track, you compete within a participating university's own applicant pool. A profile that strongly matches that institution's programme, faculty, or research priorities may stand out there.

Should the quota system determine my choice?

Not by itself. Some applicants from highly competitive countries prefer the University track because they can target a stronger institutional fit, while others value the nationally administered Embassy route. Base the decision on your background, target programme, and realistic competitiveness.

The Korean language requirement in practice

Is the Korean language year mandatory?

Yes. Every GKS scholar normally completes one year of Korean language training before starting the degree, and scholars must meet the applicable proficiency benchmark. Arriving with some Korean can make this year less pressured and more productive.

How is Korean proficiency measured?

Proficiency is generally measured through the Test of Proficiency in Korean, known as TOPIK. Reaching the required level is a real milestone because language ability supports both daily life and success in the degree that follows.

Can an English-taught graduate programme offer flexibility?

Some English-taught graduate programmes may allow flexibility under the applicable rules. Achieving a higher TOPIK level can also create additional financial benefits, so check both your academic requirements and current scholarship regulations.

How should I approach the language year?

Treat it as serious academic preparation rather than a formality. Attend classes, practise consistently, and use the year to build the communication ability that will underpin your degree, even if you already understand some Hangul or basic Korean.

Choosing your universities and programs

Can I choose any university in Korea?

No. You must select from universities participating in the relevant GKS route for your application year. Each institution and programme has its own academic conditions, so verify eligibility before investing in a full list.

How many universities can I list?

The Global Korea Scholarship often limits how many universities you may choose and which institutional combinations are allowed. Read your track's current guidelines carefully before finalising the list.

How do university groups affect my choices?

Participating universities may be arranged in groups that determine which institutions can be selected together. These rules can change, so a combination that appeared in an earlier cycle may not be allowed now.

What makes a strong programme choice?

Choose programmes that genuinely match your background and goals. For graduate study, compare departmental strengths, supervisors, facilities, and research interests with your own. A focused, justified list is stronger than a random selection and will support a more rewarding experience after arrival.

The health check and other requirements

What health requirement applies to GKS?

Applicants complete a personal medical assessment during the application process and a fuller examination at the required later stage. Serious health conditions that would prevent study may affect eligibility, so do not leave this requirement until the deadline.

When should I arrange the medical assessment?

Arrange it as soon as the official instructions and approved physician list are available. For Pakistani applicants, booking early with an approved panel physician is especially important because appointments and result processing can take time.

Which document formalities can cause rejection?

Notarisation, apostille, translation, seals, signatures, file rules, and prescribed formats can all matter. Build enough time into your schedule to correct a failed scan, an unattested transcript, or an essay that exceeds the required format.

Life in Korea and what the stipend covers

What does the monthly living allowance cover?

The stipend is intended to help with everyday living costs. Together with tuition support, the settlement allowance, medical insurance, and other benefits, it can make a Korean degree genuinely affordable.

Will the stipend feel different in Seoul?

Living costs vary between Seoul and smaller cities, so the same allowance generally stretches further outside the capital. Before arrival, estimate accommodation, food, transport, communication, and initial setup costs for your assigned location.

How should I manage the GKS payment plan?

Follow NIIED and university instructions about when and where funds are paid, and keep records of essential receipts where appropriate. Budget carefully during your first months, especially if you must cover temporary purchases before a regular payment arrives.

Common mistakes to avoid

What application errors should GKS applicants avoid?

  • Applying through both tracks, which is not allowed, instead of committing to one.
  • Treating required documents casually and missing notarisation or formatting rules.
  • Writing generic statements that do not connect to Korea or the chosen programme.
  • Underestimating the Korean language benchmark you must reach during the training year.
  • Choosing universities at random rather of matching programmes to your goals.

Alternatives to GKS

What alternatives can fund study in Korea?

The Global Korea Scholarship is the flagship option, but it is not the only way to finance Korean study. University awards, local government schemes, foundations, department funding, and English-taught programme packages can provide alternatives.

Can a Korean university scholarship be fully funded?

Many Korean universities offer their own international-student scholarships, ranging from partial awards to full tuition waivers, and some are granted automatically based on academic grades. They can be a strong backup or even a first choice if you have a specific university in mind.

Where else can funding come from?

Local governments, foundations, and individual academic departments also provide support. English-taught graduate programmes sometimes include funding designed to attract international talent, particularly where the field matches a funder's priorities.

What should I do if GKS is unsuccessful?

Research university scholarships, assistantships, government programmes, and relevant foundation awards before you arrive. A focused alternative package can still make a Korean degree affordable, and you may be able to apply for GKS again in a later cycle if the rules allow it.

After you arrive: obligations and opportunities

Does GKS impose strict post-study conditions?

The Global Korea Scholarship is designed primarily to support your academic success and does not generally bind scholars to a rigid post-study placement condition. You must, however, complete the language year and degree while maintaining the academic standards required to keep the award.

What must you do to keep the scholarship?

Satisfy attendance, study-progress, language, and grade requirements throughout the programme. Report serious problems early and seek approval before making changes that could affect your enrolment or funding status.

How can Korean proficiency help after arrival?

Achieving a strong Korean level can bring financial benefits, make daily life easier, and deepen your engagement with university life. It can also become a lasting professional skill rather than merely a condition of the award.

What long-term value does GKS offer?

Beyond funding, GKS connects scholars to a large alumni network, Korean universities, and Korean culture. Treat the programme as an opportunity to build lasting skills and relationships, not only a discounted degree, and participate in the community around you.

Staying organised through the application cycle

Why is organisation essential for GKS?

The application spans months, multiple assessments, and many documents. Strong candidates can lose opportunities through administrative slips rather than weak academic profiles, so a deliberate system is a practical advantage.

What should a GKS checklist include?

Create a checklist for your exact track, record every official deadline, and track the status of each required file. Include transcripts, certificates, statements, recommendations, language evidence, medical steps, and any legalisation or translation work.

When should I request recommendations and documents?

Ask for records and recommendation letters well before the final deadline. Universities and employers need time to respond, and you need time to resolve an incorrect name, missing seal, or incomplete transcript without rushing at submission.

How should I manage the final week?

Upload early, check every scan for legibility, compare your submission with the official checklist, and retain confirmation records. Do not rely on the deadline minute; technical problems or last-minute portal changes can otherwise ruin an otherwise strong file.

Tips for standout application essays

Why do GKS essays matter so much?

Written elements carry enormous weight because they reveal how you think, what motivates you, and whether your proposed study makes sense. They give selectors evidence that cannot be shown by a grade list alone.

How should a personal statement tell your story?

Explain who you are, what shaped your goals, and why Korea is the right place to pursue them. Connect achievements to a clear purpose instead of listing credentials without meaning or repeating generic praise of Korea.

What makes a study plan specific?

Name the field, explain its relevance to your background, and set out a realistic course or research direction. Graduate applicants should indicate the intended research shape and explain how the target department or supervisor can support it.

Should I write what selectors want to hear?

No. Be authentic and support your choices with evidence. A sincere account grounded in your real experience is more convincing than a polished answer built from guessed preferences.

How should you revise your drafts?

Revise several times for structure, clarity, and specificity, ideally with feedback from someone whose judgement you trust. Remove repetition, prove broad claims, and ensure every section answers the question actually asked.

Final thoughts

Why is GKS worth serious consideration?

The Global Korea Scholarship is one of the most complete fully funded opportunities in international education. It can support tuition, living costs, airfare, insurance, and Korean language training for both undergraduate and graduate students.

What determines a strong GKS application?

Your chances depend on choosing the right track, meeting the current eligibility criteria, selecting a credible university fit, and preparing strong statements and documents well ahead of the deadline. Organised, tailored work matters throughout the process.

When should you start?

Start early, research official rules for your cycle, and tailor every part of the application to the selected track and programme. With careful preparation, studying in Korea through a fully funded award can become a realistic goal.

Global Korea Scholarship application checklist for Pakistani students

What makes the Pakistani GKS process different?

Pakistani applicants need a checklist that accounts for local university documentation, embassy procedures, approved medical screening, and Korean visa preparation. Identifying requirements early prevents small errors that can derail an otherwise strong Global Korea Scholarship application.

What academic eligibility should I check?

Begin by confirming your degree-level eligibility. Graduate applicants typically need a bachelor's degree with a competitive GPA, and an applicant generally must not hold Korean citizenship or have previously received a Korean government scholarship at the same degree level. Verify the current rules for your exact track.

How do you apply for the Global Korea Scholarship?

Gather academic transcripts early because Pakistani universities may take several weeks to issue attested copies, and incomplete documentation will not be accepted. You will also need a valid passport, completed forms, a study plan or research proposal where required, and detailed recommendation letters from professors or employers who know your work.

How does the application process work?

After submitting through the applicable Embassy or University route, you complete the prescribed assessment, provide any additional documents, and proceed through national or university selection. Successful candidates complete post-selection steps before receiving the instructions needed for study and visa processing.

Should I study Korean before applying?

Basic Hangul and conversational Korean can make the funded language year more productive and demonstrate genuine commitment to Korean culture. Fluency is not normally required at the application stage, but early preparation is still useful.

Is the medical examination mandatory?

Yes. Book the examination with an approved panel physician in Pakistan well in advance so the results can be submitted within the official window. The assessment commonly includes a chest X-ray, blood work, and a general physical evaluation.

What should you write in your GKS essays?

Connect your academic background, Korean study motivation, programme choice, and future goals in clear English. Avoid generic claims, explain specifically why Korea fits your field, and ask a mentor or English-speaking colleague to review the drafts before submission.

What is the final Pakistani applicant checklist?

  • Secure a valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity
  • Book a medical examination with an approved panel physician

Visa process and pre-departure steps for GKS scholars

When does the visa process begin?

Your visa and pre-departure work begins promptly after you receive the Global Korea Scholarship award letter. NIIED and the Korean Embassy in Islamabad will issue the specific instructions, but understanding the general sequence helps you prepare instead of scrambling.

What should you do after receiving the award letter?

Confirm your acceptance and provide any remaining NIIED documents. These may include a final degree certificate, updated transcripts, or a criminal background check. NIIED then processes the confirmation and provides the visa issuance information you need.

How do you apply for the D-4 student visa?

Use the confirmation or visa issuance number to apply for a D-4 student visa through the Korean Embassy. The usual file includes your passport, visa confirmation, passport-sized photographs, scholarship evidence of financial support, and medical examination results.

How long should visa processing take?

Processing times vary, and peak-season demand can increase waiting periods. Pakistani applicants have reported waits of roughly two to four weeks, so apply as soon as your confirmation number arrives rather than planning around a guaranteed date.

When should you arrive in Korea?

Arrange your flight to reach Seoul at least two or three days before mandatory orientation. Orientation covers university registration, bank-account setup, residence registration, and practical guidance for daily life. Missing it can create administrative problems that take months to resolve.

How should you pack for Korea?

Korea has four distinct seasons, and Seoul winters can be much colder than many parts of Pakistan. Bring warm winter clothing and a few professional outfits for academic presentations. Everyday goods are easy to find, but a few familiar personal items can ease your adjustment.

What communication arrangements should you make?

Tell your bank in Pakistan about the extended absence, arrange international roaming or plan to obtain a Korean SIM card, and establish a reliable way to contact family. Settling these details before departure lets you focus on orientation and language training.

Understanding the GKS grading and renewal requirements

Is GKS funding a one-time award?

No. Your funding is renewed while you continue meeting programme requirements. Academic progress, language study, attendance, and administrative compliance therefore remain important throughout the scholarship, not only at the initial selection stage.

What happens during the language year?

You are expected to make satisfactory progress in Korean. NIIED and your institution may monitor attendance and grades, and inadequate effort can lead to funding consequences. Treat language classes with the same discipline you would apply to a degree course.

What GPA must you maintain?

Degree scholars generally need to maintain a minimum GPA each semester, often around 2.0 on the Korean grading scale or its equivalent under the applicable rules. Falling below the threshold can trigger a warning, and continued underperformance may lead to suspension or termination.

What should you do if your grades fall?

Contact your academic adviser and the relevant NIIED or university office early rather than waiting for a serious problem to grow. Ask what improvement plan is available, document the advice you receive, and follow the official process exactly.

Does attendance affect renewal?

Yes. Korean universities track attendance closely, and excessive absence can damage both your grades and scholarship status. Even when a course is not central to your research, consistent attendance is a non-negotiable part of maintaining the award.

Must you remain in Korea during the programme?

You are generally expected to remain in Korea for the duration of your studies. Extended leave or long returns to Pakistan without approval can jeopardise funding. If an emergency requires travel, contact NIIED before leaving and obtain written permission when required.

Career outcomes and alumni benefits after GKS

What career advantage does GKS provide?

The Global Korea Scholarship offers a long-term professional advantage during and after your time in Korea. Scholars gain a globally recognised credential, practical international experience, language ability, and access to a professional network valued by employers in Pakistan and elsewhere.

What networking opportunities exist during study?

NIIED and partner universities may arrange networking events, industry visits, and cultural exchanges involving Korean businesses, government agencies, and international scholars. Treat these as opportunities to build relationships that may lead to internships, collaborative research, or employment.

What is the Global Korea Scholarship alumni network?

Former scholars work across many industries and countries, and the shared experience creates a strong professional community. NIIED organises alumni activities and maintains resources that can help graduates connect with one another.

Which careers can GKS alumni pursue?

Many scholars use their Korean education and language skills in international trade, technology, diplomacy, education, translation, and intercultural work. Korea's strong economic ties with Pakistan create particular value in technology, infrastructure, consumer goods, and organisations with Korean connections.

Can graduates remain in Korea after GKS?

Graduates may be eligible for Korean post-study or job-seeking visa categories, subject to current immigration rules. The job-seeking route can allow qualifying graduates time to search for employment, and GKS alumni often transition into Korean organisations seeking international talent.

What can GKS graduates do in Pakistan?

You can return to Pakistan and apply your skills to Korean-funded development projects, language teaching, translation, interpretation, or Korean companies operating locally. A Korean degree, language proficiency, and professional relationships can give you a distinct advantage in either country.

How the Global Korea Scholarship differs from other Korean funding

Why compare GKS with other Korean scholarships?

South Korea offers several funding routes for international students, and the Global Korea Scholarship differs in scale, structure, and selection. A clear comparison helps you decide which opportunity best matches your academic priorities and financial needs.

How do university-specific scholarships compare?

University scholarships are the most common alternative. Korean universities may offer partial or full tuition waivers and, in some cases, a modest stipend. Competition may be smaller, but these awards rarely cover airfare, settlement costs, insurance, and a dedicated Korean language year in the same comprehensive package as GKS.

What do KOICA and Korea Foundation scholarships offer?

Government-linked support from organisations such as the Korea Foundation and KOICA may focus on international development, public policy, governance, or other defined fields. These programmes can be strong fits when your interests match, although their scope and eligibility differ from the Global Korea Scholarship.

How does an embassy-administered scholarship differ?

Some embassies offer scholarship routes administered in partner countries. Depending on the programme, they may cover tuition and living costs without providing the same language training, settlement support, or NIIED administration associated with GKS, so compare the full package rather than the tuition line alone.

What makes GKS stand out?

The Global Korea Scholarship combines comprehensive financial support with a structured programme. It generally brings together a language year, tuition support, monthly stipend, airfare, settlement allowance, health insurance, and support during study. The trade-off is strong competition because the total value attracts many applicants.

What should Pakistani students target?

Use GKS as a primary target while researching university-specific scholarships and assistantships as alternatives. A university award may still make study in Korea possible if GKS is not offered, and you can consider a later GKS application if the current rules permit reapplication.

Preparing for Korean academic culture as a GKS student

What is the biggest cultural adjustment for GKS scholars?

Adjusting to Korean academic culture is both a challenge and a reward. Universities may differ from Pakistani institutions in classroom participation, faculty relationships, collaboration, and expectations, so learning those patterns before arrival helps you adapt.

How formal are relationships with professors?

Korean academic culture often places considerable emphasis on hierarchy and respect for faculty. Use appropriate honorifics, maintain a respectful tone in email, and clarify expectations for office hours and academic meetings. These details affect how faculty perceive your professionalism.

How important is group work?

Group projects are a major part of Korean university education, often involving Korean and international students. Reliability, punctuality, preparation, and active contribution build respect and improve group outcomes, so do not wait silently for others to organise the work.

What research resources are available?

Graduate students may access well-funded laboratories, extensive libraries, and research infrastructure that exceeds what is available at many Pakistani institutions. Use initiative: attend seminars, ask professors about opportunities, and investigate internal grants available to graduate scholars.

How should you manage academic pressure?

The pace can be demanding, especially during examinations and while you adjust to a new culture and language. Build a structured weekly schedule from the first semester, protect study and recovery time, and address academic difficulties early to avoid burnout.

Why should you join university social life?

Academic life in Korea extends beyond classes into shared meals, study groups, and clubs. Participation creates friendships and a support network that can make difficult periods less isolating while giving you a deeper understanding of campus culture.

Which GKS preparation tips have the greatest value?

The most useful tips are practical: learn basic Korean, respect university hierarchy, prepare for collaboration, use research resources, attend consistently, and communicate proactively. Combine them with a realistic academic plan and active engagement in both Korean and international student communities.

Frequently asked questions about the Global Korea Scholarship

Can I apply if I already have a degree from Korea?

Generally, you cannot previously have received a Korean government scholarship at the same degree level. If you completed a bachelor's degree in Korea, you may still qualify for a different level, but you must verify the current rule on the NIIED website before applying.

Is the Korean language training year mandatory?

Yes. GKS scholars generally complete approximately one year of Korean instruction before their degree, and the funded training is an integral part of the programme. A shorter arrangement may be considered for applicants with high-level Korean, but only NIIED can decide an individual case.

Can my spouse or children accompany me?

The Global Korea Scholarship does not cover dependant costs. Family members may be able to enter on a dependent visa, but you must show sufficient personal funds to support them. Korean family living costs are substantial, so seek advice from alumni who have handled this situation.

What happens if I fail a course?

One failed course does not automatically terminate the award, but it can lower your cumulative GPA. Falling below the required minimum may lead to a warning or probation, and continued underperformance can result in termination. Contact your academic adviser or international student office immediately.

Can I work part-time on GKS?

The stipend is intended to support your living expenses, and NIIED expects you to prioritise study and language training. Work is restricted during the language year. After starting your degree, limited employment may be possible with approval, so check the current rules before accepting any job.

When should I start my application?

Begin at least six to eight months before the expected deadline. This gives you time to collect transcripts, draft and revise essays, request recommendations, complete medical requirements, and research universities and programmes without last-minute stress.

Can a GKS application be strengthened after a previous rejection?

Yes. Review feedback, identify academic or documentary gaps, and make measurable improvements before the next permitted cycle. Build on relevant experience, strengthen language readiness, and seek a better programme fit rather than resubmitting the same application with minor edits.

What is the GKS timeline?

Applications generally open during the first half of the year, followed by national or university selection and later confirmation of the cohort. Language training and degree enrolment may begin later in the same year or the following spring, so plan the entire cycle rather than focusing only on the submission date.

Should I apply through the Embassy or University track?

Choose the route that matches your strongest institutional fit and the competition you understand. The Embassy track offers a national selection and university options afterward, while the University track lets you apply directly to a target institution. Verify current rules and make only one choice.

What is the most important preparation advice?

Treat GKS as a long-term commitment rather than a single competition. Strengthen your academic record, extracurricular profile, Korean and English ability, programme knowledge, and community engagement well before applications open. A complete, error-free file depends on preparation spread across many months.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Global Korea Scholarship cover?+

GKS generally covers full tuition, a monthly living stipend, a settlement allowance, medical insurance, round-trip airfare, one year of Korean language training, and a degree completion grant. Exact amounts are set each year, so check the current guidelines.

What is the difference between the embassy and university tracks?+

In the Embassy track you apply through the Korean embassy in your country, which handles first-round selection. In the University track you apply directly to a participating Korean university that selects and recommends you. You may only use one track per cycle.

Do I need to speak Korean to apply for GKS?+

No. GKS includes a year of Korean language training, and you can apply without fluency. However, demonstrating Korean or English proficiency can strengthen your application, and a high Korean level can unlock extra benefits later.

Is GKS available for both undergraduate and graduate students?+

Yes. GKS has separate tracks for undergraduate degrees and for master's and doctoral study, each with its own guidelines and timeline, and both include a year of Korean language study before the degree begins.

When should I start preparing my GKS application?+
