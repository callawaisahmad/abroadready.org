Knight-Hennessy Scholars eligibility criteria

  • Citizens and residents of all countries may apply
  • First bachelor's degree earned in January 2020 or later (for 2027 cohort; earlier with military service)
  • Must apply to and enroll full-time in an eligible Stanford graduate program the same year
  • Eligible programs include DMA, JD, MA, MBA, MD, MFA, MPP, MS, and PhD
  • No age restrictions
  • Prior graduate degrees permitted if first bachelor's meets recency requirement

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition and associated fees (up to 3 years)
  • Living and academic expenses stipend
  • Annual travel stipend (one round-trip to/from Stanford)
  • Multidisciplinary leadership development program
  • Access to a global scholar community and Denning House
  • KHeystone projects, workshops, and guest-speaker events

Required documents

  • Online KHS application (short answers & essay)
  • Resume/CV
  • Academic transcripts
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Test scores (only if required by the chosen degree program)
  • Video statement (by invitation)

How to apply for the Knight-Hennessy Scholars

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Early October deadline; you must also meet your Stanford degree program's separate deadline. Submit a complete application before the October deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
