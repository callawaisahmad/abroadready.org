MIT Scholarships for International Students eligibility criteria

  • International student of any nationality
  • Admitted to MIT as a first-year undergraduate
  • Demonstrated financial need (MIT is need-blind for international students)
  • Exceptional academic record in STEM and all subjects
  • Strong standardized test scores (SAT/ACT required)
  • Must demonstrate MIT's fit through essays, activities, and recommendations

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • On-campus housing and meals
  • Personal expenses allowance
  • Health insurance
  • Book and supply allowance
  • Travel allowance for international students
  • Summer earnings expectation replaced by grant for international students

Required documents

  • MIT application (MyMIT or Common App)
  • Secondary school transcripts and school report
  • Two teacher recommendations (math/science and humanities)
  • SAT or ACT scores (required for all applicants)
  • TOEFL scores (recommended for non-native speakers, 90+ iBT)
  • CSS Profile and MIT International Student Financial Aid Application
  • Portfolio or research abstract (optional but recommended)

How to apply for the MIT Scholarships for International Students

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Early Action November 1; Regular Action January 6. Financial aid materials same deadlines. Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
