What We Handle For You

🌍 University shortlisting

We assess your grades, budget, language skills and career goals to recommend 3–4 countries and a realistic list of reach, match and safe universities — with tuition, deadlines and scholarship fit for each.

📄 Document preparation

We guide every document: statement of purpose, motivation letter, CV, recommendation letters and transcript formatting — structured, proofed and ready to submit.

🗓️ Application submission

We fill and submit your university applications through the correct portals (UCAS, Common App, Uni-Assist, Universitaly and others), double-checking every field before it goes out.

📮 Application tracking

We monitor deadlines, chase references, respond to universities, manage interview invites and follow up on offers — so nothing slips through the cracks.

🏆 Scholarship alignment

We match you to scholarships you're genuinely eligible for and plan a funding stack — merit aid, external awards and assistantships — before you commit to any university.

💬 One point of contact

One dedicated advisor handles your entire file and answers your questions through the full journey — no call centres, no hand-offs, no disappearing agents.

Answers to Common Questions

Do you really handle the whole application?+

Yes. Once you share your profile, we build the shortlist and document checklist with you, prepare and proof every document, submit your applications through the correct portals and track them until you receive decisions. You approve everything before it's submitted.

How soon will I get a reply?+

We respond within 48 hours, usually much faster. For the fastest help, be specific about your grades, budget and target country in your message.

Do you guarantee admission?+

No one can honestly guarantee admission — and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. We guarantee honest, high-quality work: realistic shortlists, correct documents, and applications submitted on time. That is what maximises your chances.

Can you help me apply for scholarships too?+

We help you find scholarships you're eligible for, plan your application timeline, and give feedback on your SOP. Start by browsing our scholarship database.
