Orange Tulip Scholarship (OTS) eligibility criteria

  • National of an eligible Neso country (e.g. Brazil, China, India, Indonesia, Mexico, South Africa, South Korea, Vietnam)
  • Do not hold a degree from a Dutch educational institution
  • Meet the admission requirements of the chosen Dutch programme
  • Have applied or been admitted to a participating Dutch institution/OTS package
  • Demonstrate academic excellence and talent

Benefits and coverage

  • Tuition fee reduction ranging from partial up to full (100%) depending on the package
  • Wide choice of participating Dutch institutions and programmes
  • Scholarship packages tailored by each individual university
  • Open to Bachelor's and Master's applicants
  • Aimed at talented students from selected Neso countries
  • Administered by Nuffic through its Neso country offices

Required documents

  • Proof of admission or application to a participating Dutch programme
  • Academic transcripts and diplomas
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Motivation letter
  • Proof of nationality (passport)
  • English language test results (IELTS/TOEFL)

How to apply for the Orange Tulip Scholarship (OTS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by country programme and university, typically February–May. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
