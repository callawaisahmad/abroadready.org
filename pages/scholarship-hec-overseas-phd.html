HEC Overseas PhD Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Pakistani citizen with valid CNIC
  • Master's degree with minimum CGPA 3.0/4.0
  • Valid IELTS (6.5+) or TOEFL (80+) score
  • Age limit: 40 years on application deadline
  • Not already enrolled in a PhD program abroad
  • HEC attested degrees
  • GRE/GAT subject score where applicable

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees at the host university
  • Monthly living allowance $1,500-$2,000 depending on country
  • Return economy airfare
  • Health insurance
  • HEC degree attestation support
  • Conference travel grant

Required documents

  • HEC online application form
  • CNIC copy
  • Master's degree and transcripts (HEC attested)
  • IELTS/TOEFL score report
  • GRE/GAT subject score
  • Two academic reference letters
  • Research proposal outline
  • No objection certificate from employer (if applicable)

How to apply for the HEC Overseas PhD Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open January and close end of March. Check hec.gov.pk. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
