Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SISGP) eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of one of the eligible countries on the SI list (approx. 40 countries)
  • Minimum 3,000 hours of demonstrated work experience
  • Demonstrated leadership experience and engagement in societal/sustainability issues
  • Must first apply to an eligible master's programme via universityadmissions.se
  • Must not have lived in Sweden for two years or more, nor hold a Swedish residence permit
  • May submit only one scholarship application and hold only one SI scholarship

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees paid directly to the university
  • Monthly living allowance of SEK 12,000
  • One-time travel grant of SEK 15,000
  • Insurance against illness and accident via Kammarkollegiet
  • Membership in the SI Network for Global Professionals (NFGP)
  • Lifetime membership of the SI alumni network
  • Leadership and professional development activities

Required documents

  • CV
  • Motivation letter / application answers
  • Proof of work experience (employer certificates or references)
  • Letter(s) of reference
  • Copy of passport
  • Proof of application to an eligible master's programme (universityadmissions.se application number)

How to apply for the Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SISGP)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Scholarship call opens in February, after the universityadmissions.se master's application (mid-January). Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
