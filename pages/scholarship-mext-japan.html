MEXT Japanese Government Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Nationality of a country with diplomatic relations with Japan
  • Meet age limits by category (e.g. Research applicants under ~35, Undergraduate under 25)
  • Completed required years of prior education (bachelor's/16 years for Research track)
  • Willingness to learn Japanese
  • Meet health requirements
  • Dual nationals must relinquish Japanese citizenship by arrival

Benefits and coverage

  • Full waiver of tuition, entrance exam and matriculation fees
  • Monthly stipend (approx. ¥117,000 undergraduate; ¥143,000-¥145,000 research/graduate)
  • Round-trip economy-class international airfare to and from Japan
  • Japanese language preparatory training (about 6-12 months)
  • Supplemental regional allowance in designated areas

Required documents

  • Application form and placement preference form
  • Academic transcripts and degree/graduation certificates
  • Certificate of Health completed by a physician
  • Letter of recommendation
  • Field of study and research plan (Research track)
  • Language proficiency certificates where applicable

How to apply for the MEXT Japanese Government Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Embassy recommendation call opens mid-April; each Japanese Embassy/Consulate sets its own country deadline (typically April-May). Submit a complete application before the May deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
