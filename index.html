💡 Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions students ask most about scholarships and studying abroad

What is AbroadReady?+

AbroadReady is a free platform that helps students find and apply for fully funded and partial international scholarships. It combines a live scholarship database with real deadlines, an AI-powered matching quiz, step-by-step application guides, an SOP builder and an AI advisor — all in one place.

Is AbroadReady really free for students?+

Yes. Every tool on AbroadReady is free and there are no hidden fees. The platform is supported by advertising, so students never pay to search scholarships, take the quiz, read the guides or build their SOP.

Which scholarships can I find on AbroadReady?+

AbroadReady covers 60+ fully funded and partial scholarships, including Chevening, Fulbright, DAAD, Commonwealth, Erasmus Mundus and Australia Awards, alongside country guides, internships and study-abroad resources.

How does the scholarship matching quiz work?+

You answer eight quick questions about your education, interests and goals. The AI then ranks scholarships by how well they fit your profile and shows you the best matches — usually in about two minutes.

What makes AbroadReady different from other scholarship websites?+

Instead of a static list, AbroadReady gives you a personalised shortlist from an AI quiz, live deadline tracking, a step-by-step journey map for every scholarship, and free tools like the SOP builder and AI advisor to strengthen your application.

Can students from Pakistan, India and other countries use AbroadReady?+

Yes. AbroadReady is designed for students worldwide. Its guides cover fully funded programmes open to many nationalities, such as Chevening, Fulbright, DAAD, Commonwealth and Australia Awards, with country-specific requirements for each.
