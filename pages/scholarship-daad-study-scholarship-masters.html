Study Scholarships – Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines eligibility criteria

  • Completed first (bachelor's) degree, normally no more than 6 years old at the deadline
  • Open to foreign graduates of all academic disciplines
  • Not resided in Germany more than 15 months at the application deadline
  • Aiming for / admitted to a full Master's programme at a German university
  • Strong academic record
  • Language skills meeting the chosen programme's requirements

Benefits and coverage

  • Monthly scholarship stipend (graduate rate, approx. €934)
  • Health, accident and personal liability insurance
  • Travel allowance
  • One-time study/research allowance
  • Possible monthly rent subsidy
  • Possible family allowance for accompanying children
  • Funding for a preparatory German language course

Required documents

  • DAAD portal application form
  • Tabular CV (max 3 pages)
  • Motivation letter
  • Letter of admission / proof from the German university (where required)
  • Transcript of records
  • Degree certificate with grades
  • Language proficiency certificate
  • One signed academic letter of reference

How to apply for the Study Scholarships – Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Varies by country Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
