Immigrate to the Netherlands: The 2026 HSM & Settlement Guide

The Netherlands' main skilled route is the Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant) permit — a job offer from an IND-recognised sponsor, a salary threshold, no labour-market test, and the 30% tax ruling for qualifying expats. Graduates get an unfair advantage: the Orientation Year plus a reduced HSM threshold of just €3,122/month.

The Dutch System in One Paragraph

The Netherlands is a sponsor-based system: to work, you need a job from an employer that's a recognised sponsor with the IND (the Dutch immigration service). Meet the salary threshold (indexed every January), and the permit follows — no labour-market test, no points. Settlement needs 5 continuous years of lawful residence, then permanent residence or naturalisation (with an A2 integration exam).

Graduate edge: if you studied in the Netherlands (or at a foreign top-200 university), the Orientation Year gives you 1–2 years of free job-search, and switching to HSM afterwards uses a reduced threshold (€3,122/month) until you hit 30. The Netherlands is one of the easiest EU countries for a fresh graduate to stay in.

Orientation Year — The Graduate Bridge

Full labour-market access for 1 year, renewable once (max 2 years).

  • Apply within 3 years of completing an NL bachelor's or master's (10+ months), a foreign top-200 university master's/PhD, or an Erasmus Mundus master's
  • Not available for MBO-level study only
  • No job offer or salary requirement during the year itself
  • After it: switch to HSM using the reduced €3,122 threshold, or another route
  • Official form: IND form 7523

Self-Employment & Other Routes

🧑‍💼 Self-employed residence permit

For freelancers whose activity serves an "essential interest" of the Dutch economy; requires KvK registration and a solid business plan. Harder than HSM — discretionary.

🚀 Startup

A recognised facilitator/mentor organisation sponsors your startup; no salary threshold. Common route for founders.

💍 Family reunification

Spouse/partner of a permit holder — partners get work rights; 21+ age requirement and income rules apply.

🎓 EU Blue Card

Higher threshold (€5,942) but faster track to long-term EU residence; reduced rate €4,754 for graduates.

More Work Permits: GVVA, ICT, Research & Seasonal

The HSM route needs an IND-recognised sponsor. If your employer isn't a sponsor — or you're being transferred, doing research or taking seasonal work — these permits fill the gap.

📋 GVVA — labour-market permit Employer

The tewerkstellingsvergunning (GVVA) for employers who are not recognised sponsors hiring mid- or lower-skilled staff. Requires a labour-market test — UWV must confirm the job can't be filled from the EU/EEA/Switzerland. Tied to the employer and limited in duration; not a route to residence.

🔄 Intra-corporate transferee (ICT) EU Directive

For managers, specialists and trainees transferred within a multinational. Requires 3+ months' prior employment with the sending company; no labour-market test. Up to 3 years (managers/specialists) or 1 year (trainees); family members may accompany.

🔬 Researcher / scientific Research

Hosting agreement with a research organisation that's a recognised sponsor. No personal salary threshold applies — relevant for PhD and postdoc researchers employed by Dutch universities and institutes.

🌷 Seasonal work Temporary

Horticulture and agriculture employers use the GVVA for seasonal hires — maximum 24 weeks (about 6 months) per calendar year at Dutch minimum wage. No route to permanent residence.

30% tax ruling: expats recruited from abroad can get up to 30% of gross salary paid tax-free for up to 5 years — subject to a salary minimum (indexed annually). It applies mainly to HSM/EU Blue Card employees; your employer's HR applies to the Dutch tax authority (Belastingdienst).

Tourist & Short-Stay — the Schengen C Visa

For visits of up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the whole Schengen Area — holiday, family visits and short business trips. This is a visitor status, not a residence permit.

Visa-free nationals will need an ETIAS travel authorisation once the scheme goes live (still pending at the time of writing) — ETIAS is not a visa, but will become mandatory for entry.

There's no separate "business visa" — short business trips run on the same Schengen C short-stay visa.

Study → Post-Study Work → Settlement

The Dutch study-to-work bridge is one of the strongest in Europe. Full guidance lives on the Study in the Netherlands page.

🎓 Student residence permit

For a place at a recognised Dutch university or HBO. Non-EU students may work up to 16 hrs/week during term (or full-time June–August) with a work permit. Student time does NOT count toward the PR clock.

🎉 Orientation Year (zoekjaar)

1 year, renewable once (max 2) of full job-search freedom after an NL degree or a top-200 foreign master's/PhD. No job offer or salary required during it. See the section above.

💼 Switch to HSM

After the Orientation Year, take the reduced HSM threshold of €3,122/month (until age 30) with any IND-recognised sponsor. This is the standard student-to-settlement pipeline.

🛂 Residence clock

Only non-student years count toward the 5-year PR/naturalisation requirement — the Orientation Year and HSM years do. Plan the switch early so your years accumulate.

Investor & Entrepreneur Routes

🚀 Startup visa Founders

For founders with a scalable business idea backed by a recognised facilitator/mentor organisation. No salary threshold, but you must show living capital of €2,900/month. Valid 1 year, renewable once; then switch to HSM or self-employment.

🧑‍💼 Self-employment (zelfstandig) Freelancers

For freelancers/entrepreneurs whose activity serves an "essential Dutch interest". Requires KvK registration, a solid business plan and proof of income. Discretionary — harder than HSM. See the section above.

💶 Foreign-investor permit Abolished

The old €1.25M investment route was abolished on 17 April 2024 — only renewals of existing permits survive. Investment alone no longer buys residence; the practical investor routes are the startup visa and self-employment.

🎓 EU Blue Card Highly paid

For employees earning €5,942/month with a recognised degree — faster EU long-term residence and easy family entry. Reduced rate €4,754 for recent graduates.

Special & Other Categories

💻 Digital nomad No visa

There is no dedicated Dutch digital-nomad visa. Working remotely for a foreign employer while on a Schengen short-stay is a legal grey area — for anything long-term you need an employer-based permit (HSM/Blue Card/GVVA) or the self-employment route.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Au pair Youth

Ages 18–30, live with a host family for up to 1 year (non-extendable). Light childcare and housework, pocket money of ~€340/month (recently raised), plus a language course. Not a route to residence.

👨‍👩‍👧 Dependants of HSM holders Work rights

Partners and children of Highly Skilled Migrants accompany with no income test, and partners receive full work rights — no separate work permit needed.

🎓 Orientation Year Talent

Covered above — but note it also reaches graduates of foreign top-200 universities, making it a genuine international talent-retention tool. Official form IND 7523.

Permanent Residence & Citizenship

RouteRequirement
Permanent residence5 continuous years of lawful residence on a valid non-temporary permit + integration requirement
Naturalisation5 years legal residence + civic integration exam (A2) + no major criminal record + willingness to renounce other nationality (with exceptions)
Dual citizenship: the Netherlands generally requires you to give up your previous nationality on naturalisation (exceptions include EU/EEA or Swiss nationality, being married to a Dutch citizen, and refugees). Permanent residence does not require renunciation — which is why many expats stop at PR.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to the Netherlands?+

For the HSM route, yes — the employer must be an IND-recognised sponsor. The exceptions are graduates on the Orientation Year (no offer needed, 1–2 years) and the self-employment/startup routes. Unlike Canada or Australia there's no points-based skilled route without a job.

How hard is the HSM salary threshold to meet?+

€4,357/month (under 30) is roughly a €62K annual salary — very attainable in tech, engineering, finance and consulting, less so in junior humanities roles. Graduates using the Orientation Year-to-HSM switch get €3,122/month, which helps a lot. Thresholds index every January.

What's the 30% tax ruling?+

For expats recruited from abroad, 30% of gross salary is paid tax-free for up to 5 years — a huge effective pay rise. Eligibility rules tightened in recent years (salary minimum applies). Your employer's HR handles the application to the tax authority.

How long to permanent residence in the Netherlands?+

5 continuous years of lawful residence on non-temporary permits, with an integration requirement. Citizenship also runs at 5 years but adds an A2 civic exam and (for most) renunciation of your previous nationality — check your exemptions.

Does studying in the Netherlands count toward PR?+

Time on a student permit generally does not count toward the 5-year PR residence clock — but the Orientation Year and HSM years after it do. That's why the study → Orientation Year → HSM pipeline is the standard Dutch settlement path.

Can I switch from a tourist Schengen visa to a Dutch work permit?+

No. The Schengen C visa is short-stay only and cannot be converted into a residence permit while in the Netherlands. You must leave, secure a job with an IND-recognised sponsor, and apply for an MVV entry visa from your home country.

Can I visit the Netherlands on business without a work permit?+

Yes — short business trips (meetings, conferences, negotiations) are covered by the Schengen C visa or visa-free entry for waiver nationals. You may not take up employment with a Dutch employer, receive a Dutch salary, or work as a local. Anything longer needs a work/residence permit before arrival.

Is there a digital nomad visa for the Netherlands?+

No dedicated visa exists. The pragmatic routes for remote workers are the self-employment permit (if you pass the "essential interest" test) or employment through an IND-recognised sponsor. Long-term remote work on a tourist stay is a grey area and won't lead to residence.
