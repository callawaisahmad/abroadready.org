About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend (CNY 7,000/month), accommodation, and travel support for PhD study at CAS institutes for developing country students. 3-4 years PhD

CAS-TWAS President's Fellowship (PhD in China) eligibility criteria

  • Citizenship of a TWAS member developing country (including Pakistan)
  • Under 35 years of age
  • Master's degree in a relevant field
  • Strong academic and research record
  • English proficiency
  • Not a Chinese citizen

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition waiver
  • Monthly stipend CNY 7,000 (approx. USD $960)
  • On-campus accommodation
  • Health insurance
  • International airfare (round trip)
  • One-time settlement allowance
  • Conference travel support

Required documents

  • CAS-TWAS online application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Research proposal
  • Two recommendation letters (from senior scientists)
  • English proficiency certificate
  • CV/Resume
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the CAS-TWAS President's Fellowship (PhD in China)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications open in October and close in mid-February. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
