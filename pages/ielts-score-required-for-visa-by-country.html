IELTS Score Required for a Visa: by Country 2026

🗓️ Aug 12, 2026⏱️ 12 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

Your English test can make or break a visa application, so the exact IELTS score required for a visa is one of the first things you should research, not the last. The score you need depends entirely on the country, the visa type and the institution, and the differences are bigger than most applicants expect: the UK will accept band 4.0 for a skilled worker visa while the same country's universities want 6.5 or higher. This guide sets out the 2026 requirements for student, work and permanent-residence visas across 14 countries, plus which IELTS test type you actually need.

Every requirement below is a minimum for the visa route. University courses almost always set their own higher bar, so always check the specific program before you book a test date.

Key takeaways

  • Choose the right test: IELTS Academic for study, IELTS General Training for most work/PR routes, IELTS for UKVI for UK visas, and IELTS Life Skills for UK family routes.
  • Canada: IELTS 6.0 overall (no band below 5.5) for the former SDS, and CLB 7–9 (IELTS 6.0+ per band) for Express Entry points.
  • UK: 5.5 in each band (B2) for the Student route; 4.0 in each band (B1) for Skilled Worker; 4.0 (Life Skills B1) for settlement.
  • Australia: 6.0 overall (5.0 minimum per band) for a student visa; 6.5+ for skilled migration points.
  • New Zealand: 5.5–6.5 for study depending on level; 6.5 overall for Skilled Migrant.
  • Your IELTS result is valid for two years, and computer-delivered results arrive in three to five days.

Which IELTS test type do you need?

Choosing the wrong test is one of the most common, and most expensive, mistakes applicants make.

  • IELTS Academic — university study, scholarships, professional registration.
  • IELTS General Training — work visas, immigration and PR (Canada Express Entry, Australia skilled visas, NZ Skilled Migrant, UK Skilled Worker).
  • IELTS for UKVI (Academic or General) — UK visa applications, taken at a UKVI-approved centre as a SELT.
  • IELTS Life Skills (A1, A2, B1) — UK family and settlement visas, speaking and listening only.

For Canadian immigration always use IELTS General Training. For UK visas use IELTS for UKVI. For university admissions, IELTS Academic is almost always required.

Required IELTS scores by country & visa (2026)

Canada

Student: 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5 was the SDS benchmark; most universities ask 6.0–6.5. PR: Express Entry maps IELTS to CLB levels, with CLB 7 (IELTS 6.0 per band) as the minimum and CLB 9 for maximum points. IELTS General Training is the right version.

United States

The F-1 student visa has no federal IELTS minimum; each university sets its own bar, typically 6.0–7.5. Work (H-1B) and green card routes do not require IELTS.

United Kingdom

Student route: 5.5 in each band (B2). Skilled Worker: 4.0 in each band (B1). Settlement/ILR: 4.0 via IELTS Life Skills B1. The Graduate visa needs no test.

Australia

Student visa (500): 6.0 overall with a 5.0 minimum per band. Graduate visa (485): 6.5 overall. Skilled visas: 6.0 per band earns points, and higher bands earn more.

New Zealand

Study: 5.5–6.5 depending on qualification level. Skilled Migrant Category: 6.5 overall is the typical bar, and higher scores improve your profile.

Germany, Netherlands, France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey

None of these countries requires IELTS for the visa itself, but English-taught programs at their universities set their own minimums: typically 6.0–6.5 for most of Europe, 6.5–7.0 for top Swiss schools like ETH Zurich and EPFL, and 5.5–6.5 for Turkish universities. "Not required" for a visa means the English bar is met through other evidence, never that no English is needed.

IELTS test fees and validity (2026)

Fees are set locally and differ between computer-delivered and paper tests. Typical 2026 ranges: India INR 17,000–18,500; Pakistan PKR 53,000–62,000; UK £215–235; US$235–270; Canada CAD 319–340; Australia AUD 485–525. Results: computer-delivered in three to five days, paper in 13 days. Your Test Report Form is valid for two years for visas and university applications, and with One Skill Retake you can resit a single module within 60 days.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for a student visa in 2026?+

Most universities and student visa routes require IELTS 6.0 to 6.5 overall. The UK Student route accepts 5.5 in each band (B2), Australia requires 6.0 overall with a 5.0 minimum per band, and Canada's benchmark was 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5.

Is IELTS required for permanent residence?+

In points-based systems, yes: Canada's Express Entry, Australia's skilled migration and New Zealand's Skilled Migrant Category all award points for IELTS bands (Canada maps IELTS to CLB, with CLB 9 giving maximum points). Most European PR routes do not ask for IELTS.

What is the difference between IELTS Academic and General Training?+

Academic is for university study and professional registration; General Training is for work, immigration and PR. Listening and Speaking are identical; Reading and Writing differ, with Academic using academic texts and data-description tasks and General Training using everyday texts and a letter-writing task.

How long is an IELTS score valid?+

Two years from the test date for universities and visa authorities. Visa applications almost always require a result issued within the last two years.

How much does IELTS cost in 2026?+

Typical ranges: India INR 17,000–18,500, Pakistan PKR 53,000–62,000, UK £215–235, USA US$235–270, Canada CAD 319–340, Australia AUD 485–525. Computer tests sometimes cost a little more; One Skill Retake is around 70–80% of the full fee.
