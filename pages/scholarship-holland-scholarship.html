Holland Scholarship (NL Scholarship) eligibility criteria

  • Non-EEA (non-EU/EEA) nationality
  • Applying to or enrolling in a participating bachelor's or master's programme
  • No prior degree or enrolment at a Dutch education institution
  • Meet the specific requirements of the chosen institution
  • Enrolling as a full-time student
  • Not eligible for statutory (lower) Dutch tuition fees

Benefits and coverage

  • One-off grant of €5,000 in the first year
  • Helps offset tuition or living costs
  • Open to a wide range of bachelor's and master's programmes
  • Awarded across many participating Dutch institutions
  • No repayment required
  • Supports non-EEA international students studying in the Netherlands

Required documents

  • Programme admission/application materials
  • Valid non-EEA passport
  • Statement of financial resources
  • Motivation or scholarship application form
  • Academic transcripts/records
  • Proof of English language proficiency

How to apply for the Holland Scholarship (NL Scholarship)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by institution; commonly 1 February (some set 1 May). Check the chosen institution. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
