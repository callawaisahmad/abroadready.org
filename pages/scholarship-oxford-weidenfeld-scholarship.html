Oxford-Weidenfeld and Hoffmann Scholarship eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of a developing country (including Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, etc.)
  • Applying for an eligible one-year Master's course at Oxford
  • Demonstrated academic excellence (first-class or strong upper-second-class degree)
  • Commitment to public service, leadership, and contributing to your home country's development
  • Clear career goal linked to the development of your home country
  • Must apply for admission to Oxford and separately for the scholarship

Benefits and coverage

  • Full University of Oxford tuition and college fees
  • Living costs grant (sufficient for a single student in Oxford)
  • Leadership development and mentoring programme
  • Access to Weidenfeld-Hoffmann alumni network
  • Invitation to exclusive events and seminars
  • Pre-arrival support and orientation

Required documents

  • Oxford graduate application form
  • Weidenfeld-Hoffmann scholarship application (online form)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Three academic references
  • Statement of purpose / personal statement
  • CV/Resume
  • Proof of English language proficiency (IELTS 7.0+ required for most courses)

How to apply for the Oxford-Weidenfeld and Hoffmann Scholarship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Scholarship deadline is early January; Oxford course deadlines are typically December or January. Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
