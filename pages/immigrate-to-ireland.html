Immigrate to Ireland: The 2026 Permits & Settlement Guide

Ireland's skilled route is the Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP): a role on the critical-skills list paying €40,904+ (or any role paying €68,911+), no labour-market test, immediate family reunification with work rights — and Stamp 4 settlement after just 2 years. Graduates get a turbo-start via the Stamp 1G scheme and recent-graduate thresholds.

The Irish System in One Paragraph

Ireland runs on employment permits, not points. The Critical Skills Employment Permit is the flagship: for occupations on the Critical Skills Occupations List, or any non-ineligible role paying €68,911+, with no Labour Market Needs Test. After 2 years you're released from the permit system entirely onto Stamp 4 (free access to the labour market). Everything else flows from that: PR, citizenship after 5 reckonable years.

Graduate advantage: Irish graduates move onto the Stamp 1G (12–24 months, any employer, no permit), then convert to a CSEP at the discounted recent-graduate threshold (€36,848) — and the CSEP clock to Stamp 4 starts immediately.

Stamp 1G & the Graduate Route

Non-EEA graduates of recognised Irish institutions hold current Stamp 2 and apply within 6 months of their award: Level 8 → 12 months; Level 9/10 → 24 months (12 + 12 renewal while job-seeking). Full-time work with any employer, no employment permit. Must respect the 7-year (or 8-year for Level 9+) overall student limit.

Transition: after Stamp 1G, join the CSEP/GEP at recent-graduate rates or a research hosting agreement. The CSEP's 2-year-to-Stamp-4 clock then runs from your permit grant.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to Ireland?+

Yes — Ireland's employment-permit system is entirely job-offer driven. There's no points-based independent route. The good news: CSEP needs no labour-market test, so employers face less red tape, and graduates buy time on the Stamp 1G to find the right role.

What's the fastest route to Irish permanent residence?+

A Critical Skills permit delivers Stamp 4 (free labour-market access) after just 2 years — the fastest settlement trigger in Europe. Citizenship needs 5 years of reckonable residence (the "5-in-9" rule) with one continuous year before application. Spouses of Irish citizens qualify in 3 years.

Can my family come with me on an Irish work permit?+

Yes — CSEP holders get immediate family reunification and their spouse receives Stamp 1/1G work rights (no separate permit). GEP holders can reunify after 1 year. Children attend school free.

Is Ireland a good post-study country?+

Excellent. The Stamp 1G gives 12–24 months of unrestricted work, recent-graduate CSEP/GEP thresholds are lower (€36,848/€34,009), and the tech economy (Dublin is Europe's EU tech hub) absorbs graduates readily. One catch: study time doesn't count toward the citizenship clock, so plan the work phase carefully.

How expensive is it to apply?+

CSEP €1,000 (up to 24 months), GEP €500–1,000, Stamp 1G registration €300. Permits refund 90% of the fee if refused. There's no separate PR application fee path in the same way as some countries — settlement happens via the Stamp system at registration.
