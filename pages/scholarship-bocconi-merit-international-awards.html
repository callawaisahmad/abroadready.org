Bocconi Merit Award / Bocconi International Awards eligibility criteria

  • Admitted first-year Bachelor or Master of Science student for a.y. 2026-27
  • Academic profile ranked among the top admitted applicants
  • Assessed on the merit of the submitted admission dossier
  • Open to international, Italian and Bocconi students
  • No separate award application needed — considered automatically at admission

Benefits and coverage

  • 100% waiver on Bocconi ordinary academic tuition and fees
  • Awarded automatically on the strength of the admission dossier
  • No separate scholarship application required
  • Assignment communicated together with admission results
  • Open to international, Italian and Bocconi applicants
  • Renewable for the full duration of the degree if merit is maintained

Required documents

  • Complete admission application dossier
  • Academic transcripts / prior qualifications
  • Required admission test scores (per program)
  • English language certification (if required)
  • Updated CV (to be uploaded upon acceptance)

How to apply for the Bocconi Merit Award / Bocconi International Awards

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. No separate award deadline; assessed automatically at admission, so apply in an early admission round. Submit a complete application before the official deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
