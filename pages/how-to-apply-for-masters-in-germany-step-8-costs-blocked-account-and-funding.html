What does Step 8 actually deliver?

What do I need before I can open a blocked account?

A confirmed admission letter. A blocked account exists to fund a specific enrolment, so no provider will open one without a Zulassung. By the time you work through apply for masters in germany step 8 costs, that PDF should be in your inbox alongside your APS certificate and a clear start date. Winter intake begins in October, summer in April, and every visa date is scheduled backwards from the start date rather than forwards from today.

Three artefacts come out of Step 8: a funded restricted account in a German bank in your name, the provider's confirmation that the mission accepts as proof of financing, and a first-year budget you can defend to the person funding you. That third artefact is the one students skip, and it is why so many families are quietly short in month four. The blocked account is a floor, not a plan.

How long does Step 8 take?

Three to six weeks for the mechanics, considerably longer for the money. Online signup with an ID check takes 30 to 90 minutes spread over a few days, because verification fails more often than providers admit. Paper confirmation adds one to three weeks. Moving EUR 11,904 out of Pakistan is the slow part: wires run five to ten business days, and the funds must clear before anything else starts. Begin the moment your offer arrives.

Tuition, semester fees and what Germany does not charge

Does Germany really charge no tuition fees?

For most international students, yes. Around 500 public universities charge no tuition to anyone including non-EU nationals. That structural fact is the entire reason Germany became the destination it is, and why Step 8 is about living costs rather than course fees.

Three exceptions are worth knowing precisely. First, Baden-Württemberg, where public universities charge students from outside the EU/EEA around EUR 1,500 per semester — if your list includes Heidelberg, Stuttgart, Karlsruhe, Freiburg, Tübingen or Ulm, that one rule can add more to your budget than everything else here combined. Second, a small number of English-language or business programmes at public universities charge fees, particularly in medicine and some MBA tracks. Third, private universities charge what they like. Verify on the official university page; the German government's official portal on studying in Germany is the safest starting point.

What is the semester contribution?

The Semesterbeitrag is not tuition. It funds student services, the library, sports, the student union and, in many states, a transport pass. It is set per university, so the number genuinely differs at every institution, and it is normally non-refundable. Most charge EUR 150 to EUR 250; larger universities and cities with a student transport pass sit between EUR 300 and EUR 400. Amortise it over six months when planning: a EUR 350 fee is roughly EUR 58 a month, charged twice a year in lumps that land in October and April, exactly when students with a drained account get caught.

Is EUR 11,904 enough for a year?

Officially that is the point of the number, and practically it is the wrong question. EUR 11,904 is a floor designed to prevent starvation, not a comfortable budget, and understanding that gap is the most useful thing you learn in apply for masters in germany step 8 costs. Visa fee, flight, deposit, furniture and a reserve sit outside it, so a realistic first-year figure is EUR 15,000 to EUR 23,000.

Why does rent vary so much between cities?

Because the German housing market is local, national and extremely tight in the big cities. A shared room in a smaller eastern or mid-sized western university town can be EUR 280 to EUR 400 including utilities. In Munich it can be EUR 700 to EUR 950; in Berlin, EUR 550 to EUR 850. The gap between a Munich budget and a Kaiserslautern budget is roughly EUR 400 a month.

So city choice is a financial decision as much as an academic one, and EUR 992 assumes average rent. Utilities are usually bundled into an advertised Warmmiete but not always, so read the Mietvertrag.

How much does health insurance cost a student?

Public statutory insurance is the cheapest route for most students, running roughly EUR 128 to EUR 140 a month for the student tariff in 2026, sometimes matched by your university. You must be insured to enrol, so the practical order is: register a German address, sort insurance, then enrol. Private tariffs sometimes work out marginally cheaper, occasionally not, and some will not accept a student without a German address. Many universities also charge a separate Studentschaftsbeitrag, so check what yours bills. The arrival step, residence registration and the first 90 days, covers the sequence.

How much should I keep outside the monthly release?

Two things, minimum. An emergency reserve of at least EUR 500 to EUR 1,000 that is not part of the blocked account, ideally in a separate savings account, because releases are monthly and offer no flexibility if a repair bill lands late in the month. And enough to cover the semester contribution, your room deposit and your flight, because the release is monthly while those land in lumps. The most common budgeting mistake among Pakistani students in Germany is treating the monthly release as the only balance. It is not: a regulated stream is not a buffer.

The EUR 11,904 blocked account, explained

What exactly is a Sperrkonto?

A Sperrkonto is a German account opened in your name with your access to the funds restricted. You are the legal owner and you can see the balance, but you cannot withdraw from it. Instead the provider releases a fixed monthly amount to a second, fully accessible account, and the total plus the original amount defines how long you can live. It exists because the state wants certainty that a student arriving from outside the EU will not become a burden on the social system, so rather than assessing your intentions in an interview and hoping, it asks for money to be present and restricted.

Why exactly EUR 11,904?

It is a simple multiplication. The Federal Foreign Office publishes a monthly living cost figure for students, and 2026's figure is EUR 992. Twelve months at EUR 992 is EUR 11,904. It is recalculated periodically rather than annually, so any article quoting EUR 9,147, EUR 10,392 or EUR 11,208 is quoting a previous year. Use EUR 11,904 for a 2026 application, and note the balance must be exact: EUR 11,000 in an EUR 11,904 account is a refusal, not a near-miss.

Can I get the money in one go?

No, and the provider says so at signup. The monthly release is the product: you choose an amount per month within the provider's range, and the account closes when the balance is exhausted. Worth internalising because it shapes your arrival plan — you land with one month's money already released, not a year. Flight, deposit, first furniture purchase and the first week of groceries all come out of release one, so land with more than the minimum in hand.

Do I need the account for university, or only for the visa?

Only for the visa. University admission, APS verification and most scholarship applications do not require a funded Sperrkonto, though some universities ask for proof you can support yourself. Do not let it slow Steps 1 to 7. Choosing a blocked account provider

Which providers do Pakistani students use most?

Two dominate by a wide margin: Expatrio and Fintiba. Both are purpose-built blocked account providers, both are accepted by German missions, and both can be opened entirely online from abroad — the feature that matters most when you are sitting in Lahore or Karachi. Traditional banks such as Deutsche Bank, comdirect, Sparkasse or your local Volksbank offer legitimate German accounts, but the process is built around someone already in Germany walking into a branch with a passport. How much does the setup cost in total?

Between EUR 50 and EUR 150 one-time, sitting on top of the EUR 11,904 that must actually be credited. Students routinely budget EUR 11,904 plus EUR 89 and then fall short on the wire fee, so send EUR 11,904 plus the setup fee plus a EUR 50 to EUR 100 cushion for correspondent bank charges. The pattern is consistent across providers: the base account is cheap, the convenience is not. For a two-year master's, paying EUR 100 once to avoid recurring fees is usually the better deal.

How do I verify my identity from Pakistan?

Online, with your passport. Both major providers run a digital identity check combining your passport biographical page, a photo of you holding it open, and sometimes short video confirmation. This is where applications fail, and for a small number of predictable reasons:

  • Expired or damaged passport. One expiring in under six months is rejected. Check validity dates before you start.
  • Name mismatch. Your name on the admission letter, passport, APS certificate and account application must match character for character, including the order of your names. Middle names, spacing and transliteration variants are the number one cause of failed verification.
  • Poor photography. Glare, shadow, low resolution and a cropped page edge all fail. Use a flat, evenly lit surface.
  • Wrong document type. A national identity card or driving licence is not accepted. It must be a passport.
  • Inconsistent address. Your address in the application, on the admission letter and in university records should be consistent enough that a human reviewer finds no contradiction.

Budget two or three attempts. Failing an automated check and retrying properly is not a black mark. Submitting a mismatched name and having it discovered at the visa interview is.

What else goes wrong with blocked account applications?

Some missions maintain their own accepted-provider lists that differ from the provider's claim of approval, so confirm on the consular services portal first.

Funding EUR 11,904 from Pakistan

What are my realistic transfer options?

Three, in descending order of cost. A normal SWIFT wire from a Pakistani bank to the provider's German partner bank is the standard documented route, but it is slow, involves intermediary banks and carries significant fees. Sending via Wise or a similar multi-currency service into the provider's EUR account is usually faster and cheaper, with a clearly visible conversion rate. Payoneer works if you already hold a balance, though the FX markup is typically worse.

You will also see cash courier transfers and informal exchange houses suggested online. Do not. Money moved outside the banking system cannot produce a bank statement, and the provider needs a traceable credit from a legitimate source — the paper trail is the product. Our guide to study loans available to Pakistani students in 2026 is worth reading first, because a structured education loan is often cheaper and better documented.

How long does a transfer actually take?

Plan for five to ten business days for a SWIFT wire, and one to three days for a Wise-style transfer once you have verified your identity there. Add two to three days for the receiving bank to clear, and note that the provider issues the confirmation letter only after the credit has cleared, not when you press send. The realistic timeline from "I started Step 8" to "I hold a confirmation letter" is two to four weeks, a month in summer. That is why the funding half of apply for masters in germany step 8 costs is a scheduling exercise, not a banking one.

What exchange rate should I assume for 2026?

Plan around PKR 300 to PKR 330 per euro and stress-test at the pessimistic end. At PKR 300, EUR 11,904 is roughly PKR 3.57 million. At PKR 320, roughly PKR 3.81 million. At PKR 330, roughly PKR 3.93 million. That is a PKR 360,000 swing on the base amount alone, before fees, deposit and travel, so an 8% rupee move over your first year is a real erosion of your standard of living.

Convert a meaningful share of savings before departure rather than converting everything at the worst moment, and remember you will also send money during the year, so budget a rate rather than a rate plus optimism. Our broader study abroad cost and budget guide covers the currency and contingency framework in more detail.

What if the money is in my parents' account?

The blocked account must be in your name because it funds your life. The money funding it can come from anywhere: a parents' account, a joint account, a family business account, a scholarship disbursement. What matters is that the source is traceable. Expect the provider to ask for evidence of origin on larger transfers, and expect the mission to ask about the sponsor in the interview, so have a clear explanation of who funded what. There is no legal limit on a family remittance and no requirement that funds come from a source "typical" of your background.

If you cannot block EUR 11,904 today

What proof of funding do consulates accept instead?

It depends on the mission, which is why reading your consulate's checklist beats reading generic advice. Generally they accept one of four things: a blocked account confirmation; a scholarship or funding commitment letter specific enough to verify; an employment or salary contract for a family sponsor, sometimes with a sponsor bank statement; or an account in the applicant's name meeting the requirement with a hold on it. The scholarship route is the strongest alternative, because a verified scholarship letter is both funding and a signal of merit. Our guide to DAAD scholarships explained for study in Germany details the amounts.

What makes a scholarship letter valid?

One that names you, names the programme and university, states the amount and duration, and comes on official letterhead or an official institutional email domain. A vague PDF titled "scholarship confirmation" from a random site fails. So does a letter covering only part of the requirement, unless the consulate accepts top-up funding — which most do when the top-up is a documented blocked account. Check the currency, and check whether the award is paid monthly, in instalments or as a lump sum.

Does a Pakistani bank statement work as evidence?

Sometimes. A large, recent, certified statement can support a family-sponsored application, particularly where the sponsor's income and employment are also documented. It is much less reliable as a standalone answer, because the question is about money available in Germany on arrival, so treat it as supporting evidence rather than a substitute. Worst of all: unattested screenshots, statements from informal accounts, and documents showing a large balance with no visible source of funds. Misrepresenting your financial position is not a grey area.

Scholarships that fund Step 8

Is DAAD fully funded?

For many DAAD programmes, effectively yes. DAAD scholarships typically cover tuition where it exists, travel costs, insurance and a monthly living stipend. Because most public German universities charge no tuition anyway, the practical value to a Pakistani student is the stipend, the travel allowance and the credibility of the award in a visa file. DAAD also runs development-related and In-Country/In-Region schemes designed to support students from countries where studying abroad is financially difficult.

The application calendar is the hard part. DAAD cycles run with autumn and spring windows, individual programme calls open on their own schedules, and rounds close well before German application deadlines. Scholarship work is a Step 5 activity, not a Step 8 one. If you are reading this at Step 8 with nothing submitted, the realistic options have closed, and the complete scholarships list for Pakistani students in 2026 will help you salvage the current cycle.

What is the Deutschlandstipendium worth?

It pays EUR 300 per month for twelve months, EUR 3,600 a year, awarded on academic merit and personal commitment rather than financial need alone. It is not a full ride: EUR 300 a month is meaningful but not sufficient alone, so it typically combines with a blocked account or partial family funding. Eligibility depends on the university and often on having reached a particular academic stage at home. The guide to writing a scholarship motivation letter applies directly.

Which smaller awards do Pakistani students actually win?

Three categories. University-specific scholarships and travel grants, often small but plentiful with far lower competition than national schemes — ask the international office of each shortlisted university directly. Private and charitable German foundations funding students from developing countries, many of which nobody applies to because they are not advertised. And Pakistani institutional schemes such as HEC-supported mobility funding, which occasionally cover a research stay in Germany. The scholarships step of this series maps the full calendar.

Earning while you study

How many hours can I legally work?

During the lecture period, international students may work up to 20 hours a week. Outside it, the rule is expressed in days: up to 120 full working days, or 240 half days, in any 12-month period. Those are the limits that matter for a two-year master's, and they are what your residence permit conditions actually say. One carve-out matters enormously: working as a student assistant, or HiWi, at your own university does not count against those limits. HiWi roles pay above minimum wage, need no visa variation, and are recruited internally. Our guide to part-time jobs for international students abroad covers job types and contracts.

What can I realistically earn?

The statutory minimum wage in Germany in 2026 is EUR 13.90 per hour. At 20 hours a week across roughly 18 working weeks that is about EUR 5,000 gross per semester, and roughly EUR 10,000 across a full year if you work the permitted days consistently. Against a EUR 992 monthly requirement, a steady schedule is effectively a second income stream. Two cautions: gross is not net, because students fall in income tax class I and net lands closer to EUR 11 to EUR 12; and the limit is legal, not a scheduling preference.

What jobs do students actually get?

Four categories in descending order of pay: university HiWi and research assistant roles; teaching assistant work in the department; retail, hospitality and event work, where German matters enormously; and private tutoring, which needs at least conversational German. The honest assessment for an English-taught master's student in month one is that wage-earning takes time, because German proficiency gates most well-paid work. Our guide to studying in Germany on a student budget sets out what is achievable before income starts: the blocked account should fund your first semester unaided.

Directly and immediately. At PKR 300 the medium scenario is roughly PKR 5.49 million. At PKR 320, the same EUR 18,299 is roughly PKR 5.86 million. At PKR 340, roughly PKR 6.22 million. Nine mistakes that blow up Step 8 budgets

What are the mistakes that cost students most?

  • Using an outdated figure. EUR 9,147 and EUR 11,208 both appear in articles that were accurate earlier. Use EUR 11,904 for 2026 and nothing else.
  • Opening the account under a name variant that does not match the admission letter. This fails verification and sometimes the visa, and is avoidable by copying names character for character.
  • Assuming any German account counts. A standard account holding EUR 12,000 is not a blocked account. The restriction and the monthly release are the point.
  • Funding in instalments and assuming partial credit passes. The requirement is the full amount cleared and evidenced before your visa appointment.
  • Budgeting the blocked account as your total. Deposit, first rent, flights, semester fees and the reserve sit outside it, and this is the single largest cause of month-four distress.
  • Underestimating rent in a high-cost city. EUR 992 assumes average rent, and Munich and Berlin do not deliver it.
  • Forgetting Baden-Württemberg tuition. EUR 1,500 per semester for non-EU students at public universities in that state, easy to miss because it is not advertised as an international-student fee.
  • Applying for scholarships in Step 8. Those windows closed months ago. The honest plan is a blocked account plus family funds plus part-time work.
  • Transferring at the worst possible moment. Rupee volatility means a panicked transfer costs more than the same transfer made calmly a month earlier.

A 90-day Step 8 timeline

What does this look like week by week?

Weeks 1 to 2. Confirm the 2026 figure, check passport validity, and gather your admission letter and APS certificate into one folder with your name matching across every document. Choose your provider and tier, then open the account online, expecting to retry identity verification at least once. Do not transfer money until the account and account-holder details are confirmed in writing.

Weeks 3 to 12. Transfer the funds using the route with the best documented rate, keeping every receipt: bank confirmation, provider receipt, exchange rate screenshot, and the date sent. When the money clears, check the name, amount, university and start date on the confirmation. Then assemble the Step 9 file — visa application, travel insurance, proof of accommodation, certificates, APS, language evidence — and book the mission appointment. Step 9 is the visa itself, and Step 8 is the document that unlocks it. In parallel, start the housing search.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a master's in Germany cost in total in 2026?

No tuition at public universities, a semester contribution of roughly EUR 150 to EUR 400, and living costs near EUR 992 a month, so the blocked account requirement is EUR 11,904 for the year. Add flights, deposit, furnishing, visa and a reserve and a realistic first-year figure is EUR 15,000 to EUR 23,000. Baden-Württemberg is the exception at EUR 1,500 per semester for non-EU students, and you can work up to 120 full days a year to close a gap.

What is the blocked account for a German student visa?

It is a restricted German account holding EUR 11,904 in 2026, released to you at about EUR 992 a month to cover living costs. It is opened in your name, you cannot withdraw from it directly, and it exists to prove you can support yourself for the full period of your studies. Expatrio and Fintiba are the providers most Pakistani students use, because both open online from abroad.

Do scholarships replace the blocked account?

Sometimes. A scholarship or funding commitment letter that is specific, verifiable and sufficient can satisfy the consulate instead, and rules vary by mission. DAAD scholarships, the Deutschlandstipendium at EUR 300 a month, university grants and foundation awards are the main routes, and many students combine a partial scholarship with a reduced blocked account. Check your mission's list before assuming.

Final checklist before you commit

Run this list before you transfer a single euro. Every yes means Step 8 is done.

  • I have confirmed the 2026 figure is EUR 11,904 (EUR 992 × 12), not an older number.
  • My passport is valid at least six months beyond my expected arrival date.
  • My name is identical on my passport, admission letter, APS certificate and account application, character for character.
  • I have opened a genuine blocked account with an approved provider, not an ordinary German bank account.
  • The full EUR 11,904 is credited and cleared, with setup and wire costs covered on top.
  • I have checked my mission's accepted-provider and accepted-funding list for my visa category.
  • I have a separate reserve of at least EUR 500 for emergencies, outside the blocked account.
  • My first-year budget totals roughly EUR 15,000 to EUR 23,000 including deposit, travel, fees and reserve.
  • I have included Baden-Württemberg tuition of EUR 1,500 per semester if any target university is in that state.
  • I have built my exchange rate assumption at PKR 300 to 330 per euro and stress-tested the pessimistic case.
  • I know my work limit: 20 hours a week in lecture time, or 120 full days per year, with HiWi exempt.

Step 8 is administrative rather than academic, so it rewards preparation rather than intelligence. Students who confirm the figure, open the account early, fund it cleanly and budget the reserve get through it without drama; students who use a two-year-old number and open the account three weeks before their appointment get a request for more evidence, a delay, and sometimes a refusal.
