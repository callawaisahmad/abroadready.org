About this scholarship

Full tuition (free at public universities), living stipend (NOK 151,000-162,000/year), travel grant, and health insurance for students from developing countries. Masters 2 years; PhD 3 years

Norwegian Government Scholarship for Developing Countries eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of a developing country on the DAC list
  • Admitted to a full-degree Masters or PhD programme at a Norwegian public university
  • English proficiency (TOEFL 80+ / IELTS 6.0+)
  • Meet the specific admission requirements of the chosen university
  • Not already have a degree from a Norwegian institution

Benefits and coverage

  • No tuition fees at Norwegian public universities
  • Living stipend NOK 151,000-162,000 per year
  • Travel grant (depending on country of origin)
  • Health insurance through Norwegian National Insurance Scheme
  • Access to world-class research facilities
  • No tuition fees even for international students

Required documents

  • Online application through the Norwegian university portal
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • English proficiency certificate
  • Statement of purpose
  • CV/Resume
  • Two recommendation letters (for PhD)
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the Norwegian Government Scholarship for Developing Countries

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. University admissions portal opens October 1 and closes December 1. Some universities have separate deadlines for international applicants. Submit a complete application before the December deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
