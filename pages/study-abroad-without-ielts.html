Complete Guide: Study Abroad Without IELTS — 2026 Edition

IELTS is the most widely required English test for study abroad — but it is not the only option. Hundreds of universities and scholarship programmes now accept PTE Academic, Duolingo English Test, TOEFL iBT, Cambridge English, or waive the test entirely if your previous degree was taught in English. This guide covers every alternative, country by country.

Why You Don't Always Need IELTS

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is the world's most popular English proficiency exam, but it is far from the only way to prove your English. The assumption that you "must take IELTS" to study abroad is outdated — thousands of universities, colleges, and scholarship bodies now accept alternative tests or waive the requirement entirely.

Here is the reality of English proficiency requirements in 2026:

  • Multiple tests are accepted. Most top universities in the UK, Canada, Australia, and Europe accept IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic, and Duolingo English Test interchangeably. Some also accept Cambridge English (C1 Advanced, C2 Proficiency), the Oxford Test of English, and the Password Test.
  • Medium of Instruction (MOI) waivers are common. If your previous degree (bachelor's or master's) was taught entirely in English, many universities will waive the English test requirement. You submit an official MOI letter from your previous institution and skip the test altogether.
  • Scholarship bodies accept multiple formats. Chevening, Commonwealth Scholarships, Erasmus Mundus, Fulbright, and most government-funded scholarships accept IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or Duolingo. None of them require IELTS exclusively.
  • Some countries never required IELTS. Germany, the Netherlands, Scandinavia, and many non-English-speaking countries have universities that teach in English at the master's level and accept a range of proof — including TOEFL, Cambridge, or an MOI letter.
Key insight: the question is not "can I study abroad without IELTS?" but rather "which test or waiver works best for my situation?" Read on for a full comparison and country-by-country breakdown. See also our IELTS vs TOEFL comparison.

Score equivalents at a glance

If a university asks for IELTS 6.5, the approximate equivalents are:

  • TOEFL iBT: 79–93
  • PTE Academic: 58–64
  • Duolingo: 105–115

Practical tip: if you need results fast, Duolingo is the clear winner — you take it at home on your own computer and receive results within 48 hours. PTE results typically come in 2–5 business days. IELTS takes 3–13 days (paper-based) or 3–5 days (computer-delivered).

Study in the UK Without IELTS

The UK is one of the easiest major destinations to navigate without IELTS, because the vast majority of its universities accept multiple English tests. The UK Student visa itself does not require IELTS — it relies on the university's assessment of your English, whatever test the university accepts.

Graduate Route and English requirements

A key advantage: the UK Graduate Route visa does not require you to take IELTS separately. Once your university has confirmed your English proficiency (via whichever test they accept) and issued your CAS, that proof covers your Student visa and, by extension, your Graduate Route application. You do not need to sit any additional English test for the post-study work visa.

Important 2027 change: from 1 January 2027, the Graduate Route shortens from 2 years to 18 months for bachelor's and master's graduates. PhD graduates still get 3 years. If you want the full 2 years of unsponsored work, apply before the end of 2026.

Student Direct Stream (SDS) vs non-SDS

Canada's faster visa processing route — the Student Direct Stream (SDS) — requires IELTS 6.0+ in each band (academic or general) or TEF/TCF for French. However, the non-SDS route is open to students with Duolingo, PTE, TOEFL, or other accepted scores. Processing takes longer (typically 8–16 weeks vs 2–4 weeks for SDS), but it is a valid pathway.

After graduation, the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) is available regardless of which English test you used for your study permit. There is no separate English requirement for the PGWP.

Study in Australia Without IELTS

Australian universities are highly flexible with English test alternatives. PTE Academic is particularly popular in Australia — in fact, PTE was originally developed in partnership with Australian institutions, and it is accepted by every Australian university.

Tests accepted by Australian universities

All Group of Eight (Go8) universities — Melbourne, Sydney, ANU, UNSW, Monash, Queensland, Adelaide, and Western Australia — accept IELTS, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic, and Cambridge C1/C2. Many also accept Duolingo.

Student visa (subclass 500) requirements

The Australian Student visa (subclass 500) requires you to provide evidence of English proficiency. The minimum is IELTS 5.5 (or equivalent in another accepted test) for English-language intensity courses, or IELTS 5.0 if you are packaged with an English course (ELICOS). For direct university entry, the standard is IELTS 6.0–6.5 or equivalent.

Crucially, "equivalent" is defined by the Department of Home Affairs using a published table. PTE 42–50 is equivalent to IELTS 5.5–6.0, TOEFL 35–59 covers the same range, and Duolingo 75–94 does the same. As long as your score meets the equivalent threshold, the visa office accepts it.

Tip: PTE is often the easiest option for Australia because it is computer-marked (no human examiner bias), results come in 2–5 business days, and test centres are widely available across South Asia and the Middle East.

Study in Germany Without IELTS

Germany is one of the best destinations for students who want to skip IELTS entirely. Most German public universities charge zero or minimal tuition (just a semester contribution of €150–350), and many master's programmes are taught entirely in English — accepting TOEFL, Cambridge, or even just an MOI letter.

How German universities handle English requirements

  • TOEFL iBT 80–92 is the most commonly accepted alternative. Many programmes at TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, Heidelberg, and LMU Munich specify TOEFL rather than IELTS.
  • Cambridge C1 Advanced (176+) or C2 Proficiency (180+) is accepted widely across Germany.
  • MOI letter: if your bachelor's degree was taught in English, many German universities accept a Medium of Instruction letter as proof — no test needed at all. This is especially common for applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, and other countries with English-medium universities.
  • No test for some programmes: certain programmes, especially in STEM fields at universities like TU Berlin, University of Bonn, and University of Göttingen, waive the English test entirely if your previous degree was in English.

APS certificate requirement

Students from China, India, Vietnam (and recently some other nationalities) need an APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) certificate before applying to German universities. The APS evaluates your academic documents and sometimes includes an interview. It is separate from the English requirement but is a mandatory step in the application process.

Money saver: German public universities charge no tuition fees for EU/EEA students and most international students. Combined with no IELTS requirement (if you have MOI or TOEFL), Germany is one of the cheapest high-quality study destinations in the world.

Study in Europe Without IELTS

Beyond the UK, Canada, Australia, and Germany, many other European countries offer English-taught programmes with flexible English requirements. Here is a country-by-country overview.

Netherlands

The Netherlands has the highest concentration of English-taught programmes in non-English-speaking Europe. Over 2,200 programmes are taught entirely in English. Universities like University of Amsterdam, Delft, Leiden, and Utrecht accept IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, Cambridge, and sometimes Duolingo. Many programmes accept TOEFL iBT 80–90 as the minimum.

Sweden

Swedish universities (Uppsala, Lund, KTH, Chalmers) accept TOEFL iBT (typically 90+), PTE Academic, and Cambridge C1/C2. IELTS is accepted but not required. The Swedish Council for Higher Education manages applications through universityadmissions.se.

Finland

University of Helsinki, Aalto University, and Tampere University accept TOEFL iBT, PTE, and Cambridge. Some programmes waive the English test if your previous degree was in English. Tuition for non-EU students is €4,000–18,000/year with scholarship options.

Denmark

University of Copenhagen, DTU, and Aarhus University accept TOEFL iBT, PTE, and Cambridge. Denmark's state tuition is free for EU/EEA students; non-EU students pay €6,000–16,000/year.

Italy

Bocconi, Politecnico di Milano, Sapienza, and University of Bologna offer English-taught programmes. They accept TOEFL iBT, IELTS, and Cambridge. Some programmes accept Duolingo. Tuition at public universities is €900–4,000/year for non-EU students with regional scholarships available.

Erasmus Mundus

The Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters programme funds students to study in 2–3 European countries. It accepts IELTS, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic, and Cambridge C1/C2. Duolingo is accepted by some Erasmus Mundus consortia but not all — check the specific programme. For a full walkthrough, see our Erasmus Mundus step-by-step guide.

Can You Study Abroad With a Low CGPA?

While this page focuses on English tests, many students who are looking for IELTS alternatives also have concerns about their academic grades. Here is what you need to know:

  • Many universities accept CGPA 2.5/4.0 or 55%+ for master's programmes. Not every program requires a first-class degree. Universities in the UK (especially post-1992 universities), Canada (many polytechnics and some universities), Australia, and Germany have flexible entry requirements.
  • Foundation year / pre-masters programmes bridge the gap. If your CGPA is below the direct entry threshold, a foundation or pre-masters programme (typically 6–12 months) can qualify you for the full master's. These are available at university-linked pathway providers like Kaplan, INTO, and Navitas.
  • Scholarships with lower CGPA requirements exist. Turkiye Burslari requires GPA 75%+ (3.0/4.0). CSC China requires GPA 80%+ at most universities but some accept 75%. Many university-specific scholarships have CGPA thresholds as low as 2.5/4.0.
  • Work experience can compensate. For MBA and professional master's programmes, 2–5 years of relevant work experience can offset a lower CGPA. Many business schools weight experience heavily.

How to Choose the Right English Test

With multiple options available, the right test depends on your priorities. Here is a practical decision guide:

⚡ Want Fast Results?

Duolingo English Test — results in 48 hours. Take it at home on your own computer, anytime. No test centre booking needed. Ideal if you're on a tight application deadline.

🌐 Want Widest Acceptance?

IELTS Academic or TOEFL iBT — accepted by virtually every English-medium university worldwide. If you're applying to 5+ universities across different countries, these are the safest choices.

💻 Want the Easiest Format?

PTE Academic — fully computer-based with no human examiner. Shorter speaking section (you speak into a microphone, AI scores you). Less stressful for many students. Results in 2–5 days.

💰 Want the Cheapest Option?

Duolingo at ~$59 vs PTE ~$160–200, IELTS ~$250–300, TOEFL ~$185–300. Duolingo is by far the most affordable, making it accessible for students from low-income backgrounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a UK student visa without IELTS?+

Yes. The UK Student visa does not independently require IELTS. Your university's offer confirms your English proficiency. If the university accepts PTE, Duolingo, or TOEFL, that is sufficient for the visa.

Which countries accept Duolingo for student visas?+

The USA, Canada (non-SDS route), UK, Australia (some institutions), and many European universities accept Duolingo. Check specific university and immigration requirements.

Can I study in Germany without IELTS?+

Yes. Many German public universities offer English-taught Masters programmes and accept TOEFL iBT (80–92), Cambridge English, or a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your previous university.

What is the cheapest English test for studying abroad?+

Duolingo English Test at approximately $59 is the most affordable option. PTE Academic costs around $160–200. IELTS costs $250–300. TOEFL iBT costs $185–300 depending on country.

Do fully funded scholarships require IELTS?+

Not always. Some fully funded scholarships like Turkiye Burslari, CSC (China), and Stipendium Hungaricum do not require IELTS. Others like Chevening and Erasmus Mundus accept IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or Duolingo.

Can I apply for scholarships without an English test score?+

Some scholarships allow you to apply with a pending test score and submit results before the final deadline. Check the specific scholarship's requirements — many accept conditional applications.
