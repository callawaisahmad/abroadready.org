Danish Government Scholarship for International Students eligibility criteria

  • Non-EU/EEA citizen with a valid residence permit outside Denmark
  • Hold a Bachelor's degree equivalent to a Danish Bachelor's
  • Admitted to a Master's programme at a participating Danish university
  • Strong academic record (B+ average or higher)
  • Meet English language requirements (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 88+)
  • Not already residing in Denmark

Benefits and coverage

  • Full or partial tuition fee waiver (varies by university)
  • Monthly living stipend (typically DKK 3,000-7,000 per month)
  • Access to Danish higher education system
  • English-taught programmes available across disciplines
  • EU-standard health coverage

Required documents

  • University-specific admission application + scholarship application
  • Bachelor's degree certificate and transcripts
  • English proficiency certificate
  • Motivation letter
  • CV/Resume
  • Copy of passport
  • Two academic recommendation letters

How to apply for the Danish Government Scholarship for International Students

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Deadlines vary by university (typically March 1 or April 1). Check individual university pages. Submit a complete application before the March deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
