Yale University Scholarships for International Students eligibility criteria

  • International student of any nationality
  • Admitted to Yale College as a first-year undergraduate
  • Demonstrated financial need (Yale is need-blind for all applicants)
  • Strong academic record and standardized test scores
  • Leadership potential and community contribution
  • No separate scholarship application required (need-blind admission)

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees covered (100% of demonstrated need)
  • On-campus room and board
  • Travel allowance (one round-trip per year for international students)
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Books and course materials
  • Yale summer fellowship funding
  • Access to Yale's global alumni network

Required documents

  • Common Application or Coalition Application with Yale-specific questions
  • Secondary school transcripts
  • Two teacher recommendations
  • School counselor recommendation
  • SAT or ACT scores (Yale is test-flexible)
  • TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo scores (for non-native English speakers)
  • CSS Profile and Yale Financial Aid Application

How to apply for the Yale University Scholarships for International Students

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Single-choice early action November 1; regular decision January 2. Financial aid materials due same dates. Submit a complete application before the January deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
