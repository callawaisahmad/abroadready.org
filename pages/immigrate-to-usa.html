Immigrate to the USA: The Honest 2026 Guide

The US offers the world's highest salaries — but also its most competitive immigration system: an H-1B lottery, per-country green card caps and backlogs measured in years for some nationalities. Here's the straight path: every work visa, every green card route, the real fees and exactly where the waits are.

The Reality of US Immigration

Compared to Canada, Australia or the UK, the US has no points-based system. Green cards are limited to ~140,000 employment-based and ~226,000 family-based per year, with a 7% per-country cap. For applicants from India — who make up ~75% of EB-2/EB-3 demand — this means waits that can stretch past a decade. For most other countries, employment green cards are achievable in 1–4 years.

This guide is not designed to discourage you. It's designed so you know the exact route, the exact costs and the exact waits — and can decide which pathway fits your profile, or whether a different country is a smarter bet.

Started in the US already? International students with OPT experience are the most common H-1B candidates — see the study-side path in Study in the USA.

Route 1 — H-1B Work Visa (Specialty Occupation)

The workhorse visa for foreign professionals. Your employer sponsors you; you compete in an annual electronic lottery.

Cap-exempt escape hatch: universities, non-profits and government research organisations can hire on H-1B with no lottery — a realistic strategy is working at a university or non-profit while your green card processes.

Route 2 — Employment Green Cards (EB-1, EB-2, EB-3)

The actual path to permanent residence. Three tiers, three different eligibility bars — and per-country backlogs you must plan around.

EB-1 · Extraordinary Ability Fastest

For top researchers, executives and outstanding professors. EB-1A can even be self-petitioned — no employer, no job offer. Meet 3 of 10 criteria (major awards, published media about you, judging, high salary…). Fee: I-140 $665. Processing ~2–3 months. No PERM needed. India/China currently wait ~2+ years; most countries are current.

EB-2 · Advanced Degree / NIW

For master's+ holders or bachelor's + 5 years' experience. The NIW (National Interest Waiver) is a self-petition with no job offer and no PERM. Fee: I-140 $665. Wait: most countries ~1 year; India ~12–14 years; China ~4–6 years.

EB-3 · Skilled Workers & Professionals

For bachelor's holders and skilled workers (2+ years training). Requires employer PERM labour certification + I-140. Wait: most countries ~1 year; China ~5 years; India ~12 years. Note: India EB-3 is sometimes faster than EB-2 — a "downgrade" strategy.

EB-5 · Investor High cost

Invest $800,000 in a US business that creates 10 jobs (or $1,050,000 standard; rising to $1.4M for high-employment areas). A July 2026 proposed rule starts automatic inflation adjustment from Jan 2027. Source-of-funds scrutiny is intense.

What is a priority date?

Your priority date is your place in line, set when your PERM or I-140/I-130 is filed. Each month the Visa Bulletin publishes cutoffs per country and category. If your date is earlier than the cutoff, you can file for adjustment of status (I-485). The per-country cap of 7% is why India and China wait so long.

Straight talk on India backlogs: as of Feb 2026, EB-2 India's cutoff is 15JUL13 — CATO estimates the queue could take 195+ years to clear at current rates. If you're Indian-born, treat the US as a high-risk option unless you qualify for EB-1 or EB-5, or are willing to build a career in another country first.

Route 3 — Other Work Visas Worth Knowing

O-1 · Extraordinary Ability No lottery

No cap, no lottery. Meet 3 of 8 criteria or hold a major international award. Used by startup founders, AI researchers, executives and athletes as the H-1B alternative. Processing 2–6 months (15 days premium). Fees ~$1,055 + asylum fee.

L-1 · Intracompany Transfer

For managers, executives (L-1A) and specialised-knowledge employees (L-1B) transferring to a US office after 1 year abroad with the same company. Max 7 years (L-1A) / 5 years (L-1B). Direct path to EB-1C.

E-2 · Treaty Investor

For nationals of ~80 treaty countries who invest substantial capital (typically $100K+) in a real US business. Renewable indefinitely, spouses can work — but it does not directly lead to a green card.

J-1 · Exchange Visitor

Researcher, scholar, au pair, intern categories. Check your DS-2019 for the two-year home-residency rule (212e) — it can block H/L/immigrant visas until you serve 2 years at home or obtain a waiver.

Route 4 — Family Green Cards & the DV Lottery

💍 Spouse / Family Green Card

Immediate relatives of US citizens (spouse, parents, under-21 children) have no cap — typically 8–18 months total. Spouses of green card holders wait ~35+ months. Needs I-130 ($625), I-485 or consular processing, medical exam and the I-864 affidavit of support (income ≥125% of the poverty line).

🎲 Diversity Visa Lottery Free entry

Free online entry each October for nationals of low-immigration countries. DV-2027: ~14.7M entries, 55,000 visas, ~1 in 267 odds. Requirements: high-school diploma or 2 years' qualifying work. From DV-2028 a valid passport scan is mandatory. Only entry is free — any "agent" charging you is a scam.

From Student to Green Card: The Most Common Path

  1. F-1 study in a STEM field (12–36 months OPT work right after)
  2. OPT job at an E-Verify employer for the STEM extension
  3. H-1B lottery entry (employer files; ~35% odds — multiple years' attempts allowed)
  4. EB-2/EB-3 sponsorship — employer files PERM + I-140; your priority date starts
  5. Wait for the cutoff (varies by nationality — current for most, years for India/China)
  6. I-485 adjustment → green card → citizenship after 5 years of permanent residence (3 for spouses)
Compare before you commit: Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Ireland offer points-based or faster PR pathways with no lottery and no per-country caps — see all immigration guides.

Immigration FAQs

What's the fastest legal way to get a US green card?+

For most people: EB-1A self-petition (if you meet extraordinary-ability criteria — no employer, no backlog for most countries), or immediate-relative family sponsorship. EB-5 is fast but costs $800K+. For the average professional, H-1B → EB-2/EB-3 with a current priority date is the realistic route (~3–5 years total).

Can I immigrate without a job offer?+

Yes — EB-1A (extraordinary ability), EB-2 NIW (national interest waiver), family sponsorship and the DV lottery all allow self-application. Everything else (H-1B, standard EB-2/EB-3, L-1) requires a sponsoring employer.

Is the H-1B really a lottery?+

Yes. ~200,000–470,000 registrations compete for 85,000 visas each year, and selection is randomised (with higher wage levels now weighted). You can re-enter every year while your status permits. Some people win on the first try; others take 2–4 attempts.

Do F-1 students really get a green card?+

Yes, it's the most common immigrant profile — but it's a long game: OPT (1–3 yrs) → H-1B (3–6 yrs) → EB-2/EB-3 (1–4 yrs for most nationalities). Total commonly 6–10 years. Canadian, Irish and Australian students often find their home countries faster; see all guides.

How can I check my chances / status?+

Check the monthly Visa Bulletin for your category/country cutoff, and use USCIS processing times at egov.uscis.gov/processing-times. For a personal assessment, book free consultancy.
