Australia Awards Scholarships eligibility criteria

  • Citizen of a participating developing/partner country
  • Not a citizen or permanent resident of Australia
  • Meet the admission requirements of the Australian institution/course
  • Meet English-language proficiency requirements
  • Satisfy home-country-specific criteria
  • Meet the prior-award residency rule if previously an Australia Awards holder

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition fees
  • Return economy air travel to and from Australia
  • One-off establishment allowance on arrival
  • Contribution to Living Expenses (regular stipend)
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the full duration
  • Introductory Academic Program before study begins
  • Supplementary academic support

Required documents

  • Evidence of citizenship of a participating country
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • CV
  • English-language test results
  • Referee or employer details
  • Country-specific supporting documents

How to apply for the Australia Awards Scholarships

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Opening/closing dates differ by country; applicants check their own country's dates in the OASIS portal (generally around April-May). Submit a complete application before the April deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
