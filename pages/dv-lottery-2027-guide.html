DV Lottery 2027: Enter the US Green Card Lottery

🗓️ Aug 12, 2026⏱️ 12 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

The DV Lottery 2027 is the US Diversity Visa program for fiscal year 2027, the annual draw that gives up to 55,000 permanent resident visas (green cards) to people from countries with historically low immigration to the United States. It is completely random: about 10 million people enter each cycle and roughly 55,000 are selected, which makes it a genuine lottery and the only US immigration route most applicants can try without an employer, a family sponsor or an investment. This guide covers the DV-2027 eligibility rules, the new passport and $1 fee requirements, how to enter, and how to avoid the scams that surround the program.

DV-2027 covers the US fiscal year running from October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027. As of this guide's publication the registration window had not yet opened — the State Department delayed it while introducing new entry rules — so the most important advice is to monitor the official portal and prepare your documents now.

Key takeaways

  • DV-2027 awards up to 55,000 green cards; entry is free to file aside from a new $1 fee, and registration happens only at dvprogram.state.gov.
  • New for DV-2027 (effective April 10, 2026): you need a valid, unexpired passport at entry and must upload a scan of the biographic and signature pages.
  • Eligibility depends on your country of birth, plus either a high-school education or two years of qualifying work experience in the last five years.
  • You may submit only one entry; multiple entries disqualify you.
  • Results appear only on the Entrant Status Check page; the State Department never emails, mails or phones winners.
  • If selected, the visa must be issued before September 30, 2027, with no extensions.

What is the DV Lottery?

Created by the Immigration Act of 1990, the Diversity Visa program broadens the mix of nationalities among US immigrants by giving visas to people from countries that sent few immigrants in the previous five years. "High-admission" countries whose natives cannot enter include India, China (mainland), Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Brazil, Canada, the Philippines, South Korea, Vietnam and the UK (mainland-born), among others, and the list shifts each cycle, so always check the official instructions.

Eligibility for DV-2027

You qualify if you were born in an eligible country and meet one of two standards: a high-school education or its equivalent (a 12-year course of schooling), or, within the five years before applying, two years of work experience in an occupation requiring at least two years of training or experience. Two family rules expand the pool: if your spouse or parent was born in an eligible country, you may derive eligibility, and under cross-chargeability a married couple can often use the eligible spouse's birth country.

The new DV-2027 entry rules

A final rule effective April 10, 2026 changed how entries work. First, every entrant must have a valid, unexpired passport at the time of entry and upload a JPEG scan of the biographic and signature pages (maximum 5 MB). Limited exemptions exist for stateless persons and nationals of Communist-controlled countries unable to obtain a passport. Second, every entry requires a non-refundable $1 fee, paid through Pay.gov, with no waivers. The form now asks for your date of birth and sex rather than age and gender. The State Department delayed the registration window to give applicants time to obtain passports.

How to enter, step by step

  1. Get a valid passport now and scan the biographic and signature pages as a JPEG under 5 MB.
  2. Wait for the official registration window to open, announced on travel.state.gov and dvprogram.state.gov — do not enter through any third-party site.
  3. Complete the entry form (DS-5501) in one sitting; there is no save-and-return option, so budget about 90 minutes.
  4. Submit exactly one entry per person and keep your confirmation page and unique confirmation number.
  5. After the window closes, check results only on the Entrant Status Check page using your confirmation number, last name and date of birth.

If you are selected

Selection is not a visa; it is an invitation to apply. You must complete the DS-260 immigrant visa application, attend a consular interview and pay a non-refundable diversity visa fee of $330 per person (principal and each family member), plus a separate USCIS immigrant fee before your green card is mailed. Higher case numbers may only become current in the Visa Bulletin near the end of the fiscal year, so a complete file early is your best protection. Every step must finish before September 30, 2027 or the opportunity expires.

Scammers thrive on this program. No one can increase your selection odds, and winners are never notified by email, post or phone. Any site charging you to "guarantee" entry, or an agent offering to submit for a fee, is a scam, since the State Department says entrants should complete the form themselves. The only official addresses are dvprogram.state.gov (entry and status) and travel.state.gov (instructions and news). Who Is Eligible for DV Lottery 2027

Eligibility for the DV Lottery is determined by two factors: your country of birth and your education or work experience. The programme is designed to diversify the US immigrant population by favouring applicants from countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States. If you were born in a country that sent more than 50,000 immigrants to the US in the previous five years, you are not eligible. For DV-2027, this typically means that natives of countries like India, China, Mexico, the Philippines, Bangladesh and a few others are excluded, though the specific list changes each year based on immigration data.

DV lottery 2027 eligibility: who qualifies?

If your country of birth is excluded, there is one important exception: if your spouse was born in an eligible country, you can claim your spouse's country of birth on your application. This is one of the few ways to work around the country-of-birth restriction, and it requires that you and your spouse are married at the time of entry and that you intend to immigrate together.

Am I eligible for DV lottery 2027?

The education or work requirement is straightforward: you must have either a high school diploma (or its equivalent) or two years of work experience within the past five years in an occupation that requires at least two years of training or experience. The US Department of State maintains a list of qualifying occupations, which includes a wide range of roles from accountants and engineers to nurses and mechanics. Meeting either the education or the work requirement is sufficient; you do not need both.

DV lottery 2027 visa process

A common point of confusion is that the DV Lottery is open only to people living outside the United States. This is not true: you can enter the lottery from anywhere in the world, including from within the US on a valid visa. However, if you are selected and are already in the US, you may be able to adjust status to permanent residency without leaving the country, which is a significant advantage.

What documents do you need for DV lottery 2027?

For Pakistani applicants, Pakistan is an eligible country for the DV Lottery, which means Pakistani nationals can enter directly. The selection rate for Pakistani applicants is competitive, and the process is identical to that for applicants from any other eligible country, with the same passport requirement, entry fee and document checklist.

The New DV-2027 Entry Rules and Passport Requirement

DV-2027 introduced two important changes to the entry process that every applicant must understand. The first is the passport requirement: all applicants must hold a valid, unexpired passport at the time of entry. This was not previously required and represents a significant shift, because it means you cannot enter the lottery without first obtaining a passport. If your passport is expired or you do not have one, you must apply for one before the entry period opens.

What funding and benefits does DV lottery 2027 provide?

The second change is the $1 non-refundable entry fee, which must be paid by credit or debit card at the time of submission. Previously, the DV Lottery was free to enter, and the introduction of a fee, even a nominal one, is designed to reduce the number of fraudulent or duplicate entries. The fee is paid through the official DV Lottery website, and entries submitted without payment are not considered.

Both changes apply to all applicants, regardless of country of birth. The passport must be valid for at least six months beyond the intended date of entry into the United States, and it must be an ordinary passport, not a diplomatic, official or emergency travel document. If you are entering with your spouse, both you and your spouse must have valid passports.

What are the DV lottery 2027 requirements?

The entry period for DV-2027 typically opens in early October and closes in early November. The exact dates are announced by the Department of State a few weeks before the entry period begins, and all entries must be submitted within this window. No late entries are accepted, and there is no way to check whether your entry was received until the results are announced the following spring.

The introduction of these new rules means that preparation must begin well before the entry period opens. Applying for a passport can take several weeks, particularly if you need to renew an expired one or apply for your first. Starting this process in the summer, before the October entry window, ensures you are ready to enter as soon as the portal opens.

The DV Lottery entry is submitted online through the official Department of State website at dvprogram.state.gov. The process is straightforward but requires careful attention to detail, because errors in your entry can result in disqualification even if you are selected.

Start by gathering the required documents: a valid passport for yourself and any derivative family members, a recent photograph that meets the specific requirements (digital, 600 by 600 pixels, white background, taken within the last six months) and your education or work experience information. The photograph is a common source of rejection: it must be in colour, show your full face, be free of glasses and head coverings except for religious purposes, and meet the exact pixel and file-size specifications.

Complete the online entry form with your personal information, including your full name exactly as it appears on your passport, your date of birth, your country of birth and your country of residence. If you are married and your spouse is eligible, include their information as well, because both of you can immigrate if selected. If you have unmarried children under 21, include them too, because they can accompany you as derivative beneficiaries.

Which DV lottery 2027 tips actually work?

Upload your photograph and pay the $1 fee. After submission, you will receive a unique confirmation number. Save this number immediately and in multiple locations: it is the only way to check your entry status when results are announced. Without a confirmation number, you cannot verify whether you were selected, and there is no way to retrieve a lost number.

Do not submit more than one entry per person. Duplicate entries result in automatic disqualification for all of that person's entries. If you are married, each spouse may submit a separate entry, and if one is selected, the other can immigrate as a derivative. This strategy doubles your family's chances without violating the rules.

Common technical issues during submission include photograph rejections due to incorrect dimensions, background colour or resolution. The online tool checks your photo before you submit, but it is worth testing your photo with a free online resizer before the entry period begins to avoid last-minute technical problems.

If You Are Selected: What Happens Next

Selection in the DV Lottery does not guarantee a green card. It means you are eligible to apply for one, and you must complete the application process within the fiscal year specified in your selection notice. For DV-2027, selected applicants must complete all steps between October 1, 2026 and September 30, 2027.

The first step after selection is to submit the DS-260 Immigrant Visa application online through the Consular Electronic Application Center. This form collects detailed information about your background, education, work history, family relationships and any criminal or security concerns. Completing the DS-260 accurately and completely is critical, because inconsistencies between your DS-260 and your supporting documents are a common reason for visa denial.

You will also need to gather and submit supporting documents, including your passport, birth certificate, police certificates from every country where you have lived for more than twelve months since age sixteen, educational credentials, work experience documentation and medical examination results. The medical examination must be conducted by an embassy-approved physician, and the results are submitted directly to the embassy.

What DV lottery 2027 interview questions should you prepare for?

After your documents are reviewed, you will be scheduled for an interview at the US embassy or consulate in your country of residence. The interview is typically brief and focused on verifying that your information is accurate and that you are not inadmissible for any reason. Common grounds for inadmissibility include criminal history, previous immigration violations and certain medical conditions, though most applicants do not encounter these issues.

If your visa is approved, you will receive an immigrant visa stamped in your passport, which allows you to enter the United States as a permanent resident. You must enter the US before the visa expires, typically within six months of issuance. Upon entry, your green card will be mailed to your US address within a few weeks.

Common DV Lottery Scams and How to Avoid Them

The DV Lottery attracts a significant number of scams, and understanding the warning signs protects you from both financial loss and identity theft. The most common scam is a phishing email or website that claims you have been selected and asks for payment or personal information to process your visa. The Department of State never contacts DV Lottery entrants by email, and the only way to check your status is through the official entrant status check at dvprogram.state.gov using your confirmation number.

Another common scam involves third-party services that offer to enter the lottery on your behalf for a fee. These services are unnecessary and sometimes fraudulent: the entry process is simple enough to complete yourself, and the Department of State does not endorse or partner with any private company for DV Lottery entries. If you pay a service to enter on your behalf, you have no guarantee that your entry was actually submitted, and you may have shared sensitive personal information with a dishonest operator.

Some scams target selected applicants, sending emails that appear to come from the Department of State and requesting additional fees, bank account details or copies of personal documents. The Department of State does not request payment by email, and it does not ask for bank account information or copies of documents through the entrant status check website. All official communication comes through the US embassy or consulate in your country of residence.

The best protection against scams is to use only the official DV Lottery website, to never share your confirmation number with anyone other than your immediate family and immigration attorney, and to be sceptical of any unsolicited communication claiming to be from the Department of State. If something feels wrong, check the official website directly rather than clicking on links in emails or messages.

For Pakistani applicants, be particularly cautious of social media groups and WhatsApp chains that claim to have inside information about DV Lottery results or processes. The Department of State does not release results through third parties, and any claim of insider knowledge is false. Always verify information through the official website or the US Embassy in Islamabad.

The DV Lottery is a random draw, so there is no way to guarantee selection. However, there are strategic choices that can improve your odds or ensure that a selection translates into a successful immigration.

The most straightforward strategy is to enter every year. The odds of being selected in any single year are low, roughly one in twenty-five for most eligible countries, but the cost of entry is only one dollar and a few minutes of your time. Entering annually means that over several years, your cumulative probability of being selected becomes meaningful.

If your spouse is from a different country that is also eligible, both of you should enter separately. This doubles your family's chances, because if either of you is selected, the other can immigrate as a derivative. This strategy is particularly valuable if your country has a low selection rate and your spouse's country has a higher one.

Preparing your documents before the results are announced saves valuable time if you are selected. Having your birth certificate, police certificates, educational credentials and medical examination information ready to go means you can complete the DS-260 and schedule your interview quickly, reducing the risk of missing the fiscal year deadline.

Applying for a passport before the entry period opens is now mandatory under the DV-2027 rules, but it is also a good practice regardless. A valid passport is required for international travel and for many other immigration processes, so having one ready ensures you are prepared to act quickly if selected.

Finally, if you are selected, do not delay the process. The fiscal year deadline is firm, and applicants who wait until the last months to complete their DS-260, gather documents and schedule interviews often find that embassy appointment slots are full. Starting the process immediately after selection gives you the best chance of completing everything within the available window.

DV Lottery for Pakistani Applicants: What to Know

Pakistan is an eligible country for the DV Lottery, and Pakistani applicants make up a significant portion of entries each year. The selection rate for Pakistani applicants is competitive, and the process is identical to that for applicants from any other eligible country, with the same passport requirement, entry fee and document checklist.

Pakistani applicants should be aware of a few country-specific considerations. Police certificates are required from every country where you have lived for more than twelve months since age sixteen. For most Pakistani applicants, this means obtaining a character certificate from the local police station and potentially from the Federal Investigation Agency if you have lived abroad. The process for obtaining these certificates varies, and starting early prevents delays.

Medical examinations for US immigrant visas must be conducted by an embassy-approved panel physician in Pakistan. The list of approved physicians is maintained by the US Embassy in Islamabad and the Consulate in Karachi, and appointments should be scheduled well in advance, particularly during peak periods.

The interview will be conducted at the US Embassy in Islamabad or the Consulate General in Karachi. Both locations process immigrant visa applications, and you will be scheduled at the location nearest to your residence. The interview itself is typically straightforward for DV Lottery applicants, as the selection process has already established your eligibility. The consular officer will verify your documents, confirm that your information is accurate and ask a few clarifying questions.

After visa approval, you will need to pay the USCIS Immigrant Fee of $235 per person before your green card is produced. This fee is paid online and is required before you can receive your permanent resident card. Planning for this cost, along with travel expenses and initial settlement costs in the US, ensures that you are financially prepared for the transition. Many Pakistani applicants also choose to hire an immigration attorney to guide them through the post-selection process, which typically costs between $1,000 and $3,000 but provides valuable reassurance that all documents are in order.

Preparing Your Documents Before the Entry Period Opens

The DV Lottery entry period is brief, typically lasting about thirty-five days from early October to early November, which means you cannot afford to spend that time gathering documents. Everything you need must be ready before the portal opens, so you can complete your entry quickly and accurately.

The most time-consuming document to prepare is the passport. If you do not have a valid passport, applying for one should be your first priority. Pakistani citizens can apply for a passport through the Directorate General of Immigration and Passports, and the process typically takes two to four weeks for standard processing. The passport must be valid for at least six months beyond your intended date of entry into the United States, which for DV-2027 means it should be valid through at least March 2028.

The photograph requirements are specific and unforgiving. The photo must be 600 by 600 pixels, in colour, with a plain white background, taken within the last six months, with your full face visible and no glasses, hats or head coverings except for religious purposes. Many otherwise eligible applicants are disqualified because their photo does not meet these specifications. Taking a photo that meets the requirements before the entry period begins, and testing it with the official photo checker tool on the DV Lottery website, eliminates this risk.

Having these documents on hand ensures you can accurately complete the entry form without searching for information at the last minute.

Finally, if you are entering with a spouse, you need all of the above documents for your spouse as well. The passport, photograph and supporting documents for your spouse must be ready before the entry period, because the entry form requires your spouse's information and a separate photograph. Coordinating this preparation in advance prevents one person's missing document from delaying the entire family's entry.

Life After the Green Card: Adjusting to Permanent Residency

Winning the DV Lottery and receiving your green card is the beginning of a new chapter, not the end of the process. Adjusting to permanent residency in the United States involves several practical steps that you should plan for before you arrive. The first step is obtaining a Social Security number, which you can apply for at a Social Security Administration office after you enter the US. Y

our Social Security number is essential for opening bank accounts, filing taxes, applying for credit and many other daily activities. The second step is opening a US bank account, which you can do with your passport and green card at most major banks. H

aving a US bank account allows you to receive direct deposits, pay bills electronically and build a US credit history, which will be important if you ever want to rent an apartment, buy a car or obtain a credit card. The third step is understanding your tax obligations. As a permanent resident, you are required to file a US federal tax return every year, reporting your worldwide income, regardless of where you live or work. F

ailing to file taxes can result in penalties and may jeopardise your permanent residency status. The fourth step is understanding the travel restrictions associated with your green card. As a permanent resident, you can travel outside the US freely, but extended absences of more than six months can raise questions about whether you intend to maintain your US residency. Absences of more than one year without a re-entry permit can result in your green card being considered abandoned. P

lanning your travel carefully and applying for a re-entry permit if you need to be outside the US for an extended period protects your status. Finally, be aware that permanent residency is a stepping stone to US citizenship. After five years of continuous permanent residency, you can apply for naturalisation, which grants you full US citizenship and the right to vote in federal elections.

Understanding the Odds: DV Lottery Statistics and Reality

The DV Lottery is one of the most misunderstood immigration programmes, and understanding the actual statistics helps you set realistic expectations and make informed decisions. Each year, approximately 10 to 12 million people enter the DV Lottery, and approximately 55,000 are selected, which means the overall selection rate is roughly 0.5 percent, or one in every 200 entrants. H

owever, this overall rate masks significant variation by country: countries with fewer eligible applicants tend to have higher selection rates, while countries with large populations of eligible applicants have lower rates. For Pakistan, the selection rate is generally in line with the overall average, though it fluctuates from year to year based on the number of entries and the country-specific allocation. It is important to understand that being selected does not mean you will receive a green card. O

f the 55,000 people selected, only a fraction ultimately complete the immigration process, because many are found ineligible during the visa interview, fail to meet the education or work requirement, or simply do not complete the process within the fiscal year. The Department of Stateselects roughly twice as many people as there are available visas, specifically to account for this attrition, which means that even if you are selected, you are not guaranteed a visa. U

nderstanding these statistics helps you approach the DV Lottery as one strategy among many for immigration, rather than as a guaranteed path. Entering every year, preparing your documents in advance and pursuing alternative immigration pathways in parallel are all strategies that reflect a realistic understanding of the odds and a proactive approach to your immigration goals.

Before the DV Lottery 2027 entry period opens, working through a final checklist ensures that your entry is complete, accurate and eligible. The passport check is first: verify that you and every derivative family member entering with you have a valid, unexpired passport that meets the eligibility requirements. If any passport needs renewal, complete that process well before the entry period opens. T

he photograph check is second: confirm that your photo and your family members' photos meet the specifications, including dimensions, background, lighting and recency, and test them with the photo checker tool on the DV Lottery website. The fee check is third: ensure you have a credit or debit card available to pay the one-dollar entry fee for each entry you submit. T

he information check is fourth: verify that your name matches your passport exactly, that your date and country of birth are correct and that your education or work experience qualifications are accurate and documented. If you are entering with a spouse, confirm that all family information is complete and accurate. T

he timing check is fifth: mark the entry period dates, which typically run from early October to early November, and plan to submit on the first day the portal opens, because early submission gives you the entire entry window to resolve any technical issues and ensures you never miss the deadline. F

inally, save your confirmation number in at least two places, including a password manager or a secure file, because it is the only way to check your status when results are announced the following spring.
