What the AI Study & Immigration Advisor can answer

The advisor gives plain-English answers to the questions that decide whether an application actually succeeds: whether you are eligible for a scholarship, when its deadline falls, what a student visa costs, how long you may stay and work after graduating, and which English test score each country expects.

Every answer is built from AbroadReady's own scholarship database and our 2026 country and visa guides, so the figures it quotes are the same ones published on the guide pages it links to. It is not a general search engine, and it will tell you when a question falls outside our guides rather than inventing a deadline or a fee.

How to get a useful answer

Be specific about your situation. “I have a 3.2 CGPA and want a fully funded master's in Germany for the 2027 intake” gets a far better answer than “scholarships?”, because the advisor can then check eligibility rules, funding level and deadlines against the exact case you mean. Mention your degree level, subject and intake year if you already know them.

Before you act on an answer

Scholarship rules, visa fees and work-permit limits change between intakes, and a single figure copied out of a chat box is not proof. Treat the advisor as a fast first pass that narrows your options, then confirm the final detail on the official scholarship or immigration website before you pay a fee or build a plan around it. Our country guides and immigration guides are where the underlying numbers are published, and they are updated each intake.
