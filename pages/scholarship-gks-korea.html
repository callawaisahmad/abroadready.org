Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) eligibility criteria

  • Applicant and both parents must hold non-Korean citizenship
  • Age under 25 (undergraduate) or under 40 (graduate)
  • Cumulative GPA at or above 80% (100 scale) or top 20% of class
  • High school diploma (undergraduate) or Bachelor's/Master's (graduate)
  • Good physical and mental health
  • Not a previous GKS awardee at the same level

Benefits and coverage

  • Tuition covered (NIIED subsidy up to 5,000,000 KRW/semester; excess paid by university)
  • One year of Korean language training
  • Round-trip economy-class airfare
  • Monthly living allowance
  • Settlement/resettlement allowance on arrival
  • Korean Proficiency (TOPIK Level 5-6) grant
  • Medical insurance
  • Degree completion grant and thesis printing support

Required documents

  • Application form
  • Personal statement and study/research plan
  • Letter(s) of recommendation
  • Academic transcripts and graduation/degree certificates
  • Proof of citizenship for applicant and both parents
  • Passport copy
  • TOPIK or English proficiency certificate (if held)

How to apply for the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Graduate track applies ~February-March; undergraduate ~September-October. Embassy-track deadlines are set by each embassy. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
