MBZUAI Scholarship 2027: Fully Funded AI in the UAE

🗓️ Oct 10, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ AbroadReady Team

The MBZUAI scholarship 2027 funds master’s and PhD study at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence in Abu Dhabi, one of the few universities in the world dedicated entirely to AI. It is trending hard among Pakistani applicants because it combines full tuition, a monthly stipend and visa sponsorship with a 15 December 2026 deadline for the Fall 2027 intake.

The direct answer: for most MSc and PhD students the package is effectively fully funded, but you must first be admitted and, for research funding, match with a faculty advisor. The Master in Applied AI is the exception and is not part of the standard funded route, so do not confuse the two.

MBZUAI Scholarship 2027: What It Covers

The core offer is simple to state and generous to hold. Full-time admitted MSc and PhD students normally receive full tuition coverage, a monthly stipend, health insurance and university-sponsored UAE residence visa support. The stipend level is far higher for UAE nationals, while international students receive a strong monthly amount that makes living in Abu Dhabi workable.

The university is research-led, so the funded places cluster around well-resourced labs in machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing and robotics. The stronger your fit with an active lab, the more likely the funding follows.

How the Funding Actually Works

There is a nuance that trips up applicants. PhD students are supported by the university in the first year while they complete the faculty advisor matching process, and funding then transfers to the advisor from the second year, subject to a confirmed match and satisfactory progress. If the match does not happen, the funding can stop, so the advisor relationship is not a formality.

For master’s students, funding is not automatically attached to admission. You secure it through merit-based funding, a faculty-sponsored research or teaching assistantship, or an approved government scholarship. In practice this means the quality of your research fit, not just your grades, decides whether the money follows the offer.

If an external scholarship is involved, it normally replaces the university stipend rather than stacking on top, so read the terms before you accept two awards at once. Double-dipping is not permitted and can cost you both.

Eligibility and the Application

The academic bar is clear. You need a completed bachelor’s degree in a STEM field such as computer science, electrical or computer engineering, mathematics or physics, with a CGPA typically between 3.0 and 3.5 on a 4.0 scale. Final-year students may apply with an expected graduation letter, but transcripts must follow before enrolment.

Documents include a CV, a statement of purpose of about 500 to 1,000 words, a research statement for PhD applicants, two to three references, official transcripts, an English test and a passport copy. The GRE is optional but strengthens an application. Degrees earned outside the UAE need a Certificate of Recognition, usually by the first semester.

Applications go through the university portal, which opened on 7 September 2026, with decisions expected around the end of March 2027 and the Fall semester starting in August 2027. Applying well before the 15 December 2026 deadline gives referees and your advisor match time to fall into place.

Application Tips That Improve Your Chances

Research fit is the lever. Before applying, read the faculty profiles and identify two or three advisors whose work matches yours, then name them and your intended research in the statement. Applications that reference a specific lab and a concrete problem stand out from generic ones.

The AI portfolio spans machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, robotics and computational biology. Choose the track that matches your transcript, because admissions committees weigh prerequisite coursework heavily and a mismatch is hard to explain away.

Prepare your referees early. Ask two or three people who can speak to your technical ability and research potential, brief them on the deadline, and give them your CV and statement so their letters reinforce the same story. Weak or generic references are a common reason strong candidates are turned down.

For PhD applicants, the research statement is not optional. State a problem, explain why it matters, summarise what you have done, and outline what you want to do with the advisor. Keep it specific and honest about your experience, and avoid padding it with general enthusiasm.

The stipend deserves context rather than a number. Because the UAE has no income tax and accommodation support is often available, the monthly amount stretches further than a similar figure in Europe. Graduates also benefit from a visa and a job market that actively values AI talent, which is why the programme is both attractive and competitive.

Timing helps too. The portal opened in September 2026 and the main deadline is 15 December 2026, so aim to submit by early December. Early applications move through review sooner and leave room to fix anything that is missing.

Is It Worth Applying from Pakistan?

Yes, if your background is STEM and you can tell a focused AI story. The UAE has become a genuinely distinctive destination, with dozens of international branch campuses and a tax-free environment, and an AI-only university with industry ties is a strong launch point for research or industry careers.

Treat it as one option in a portfolio rather than a single bet. It pairs well with a German research route, covered in our fully funded PhD guide, and with the wider comparison in fully funded master’s scholarships. The statement of purpose is where most applications are won or lost, so use our motivation letter guide.

Think carefully about the Master in Applied AI. It is designed with a different structure and is not part of the standard funded research pathway, so if a stipend is essential to your plan, apply to the research MSc or PhD tracks rather than the applied programme.

Campus life in Abu Dhabi is safe, modern and family-friendly, and the city is a global hub with direct flights to Pakistan. That convenience reduces the practical distance for families and makes visits home easier than from more remote campuses.

Weigh the outcome, not just the offer. An AI degree from a research-led institution with industry links can lead to roles in the UAE, the wider Gulf or globally, and the university’s standing in the field is rising quickly. If your goal is a funded PhD, this is a credible first step.

Have a backup plan. Because funding is competitive and advisor matches are not guaranteed, apply to at least one other fully funded programme so that a single rejection does not cost you the whole year.

Confirm every deadline and benefit on the official MBZUAI graduate admissions page, and check the AI programme options on the official MBZUAI site. Deadlines and benefit levels move between cycles.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MBZUAI scholarship fully funded?+

For most MSc and PhD students it covers full tuition, a monthly stipend, health insurance and visa sponsorship. The Master in Applied AI is the exception and is not part of the standard funded pathway.

What is the MBZUAI deadline for 2027?+

The main deadline for Fall 2027 entry is 15 December 2026, except for the Master in Applied AI, which closes on 28 February 2027. The portal opened on 7 September 2026.

Do Pakistani students need the GRE for MBZUAI?+

The GRE is optional and is treated as a plus if submitted. You do need a STEM bachelor’s degree, a strong CGPA, an English test and a statement of purpose, with a research statement for PhD.
