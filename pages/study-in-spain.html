Study in Spain: The Complete 2026 Guide

Spain combines warm weather, 4-year bachelor's degrees, and some of Europe's most affordable public tuition — as low as €820/year in Andalusia. The Type D visa is straightforward (financial proof from ~€600/month), students can work 30 hrs/week, and 250+ fully English-taught degrees exist for those who don't speak Spanish. The catch: for non-EU students, regions like Madrid charge 4–8× the local rate.

Why Study in Spain?

Spain is the value + lifestyle pick of Western Europe: public tuition from ~€820/year in the cheapest regions, vibrant cities, and the world's second-most-spoken native language. Universities like Barcelona (#160), Autónoma de Barcelona (#172) and Complutense de Madrid (#187) are strong, while elite business schools (IE, IESE, ESADE) anchor the private tier. Non-EU students may work up to 30 hrs/week alongside study.

Heads-up: since Royal Decree 1155/2024 you can't convert a tourist stay into a student visa inside Spain — apply at a consulate before you travel. And region matters hugely for fees: Madrid non-EU bachelor's run €6,000–8,200, while Andalusia charges locals' rates (~€820–850).

Affordable & Easy-Entry Universities

Spain's cheapest route is the public universities of Andalusia (Sevilla, Granada, Jaén, Málaga), which charge the same ~€750–820/yr as Spanish students even for non-EU applicants — plus UNED, the national distance university, which has no entrance cutoff at all. Foreigners enter via UNEDasiss (foreign-diploma validation); if your converted grade is below a course's cutoff you sit PCE exams, which is how most internationals get in. You also get a 30 h/week work right.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak Spanish to study in Spain?+

Not necessarily — 250+ degrees are taught fully in English (mostly at private universities and some public ones like Carlos III and Pompeu Fabra). For Spanish-taught programs you'll need DELE B2/C1. Learning Spanish is a huge asset for jobs and citizenship, but it's not a barrier to entry.

How much money do I need for a Spanish student visa?+

The legal floor is 100% IPREM ≈ €600/month (€7,200/year). Many consulates expect ~150% IPREM (€900/month) or €8,000–10,000/year in big cities. Bring 3–6 months of bank statements; lump sums must be explained.

Can I work while studying in Spain?+

Yes — non-EU students may work up to 30 hrs/week alongside their studies, with authorisation. This is one of the most generous student work allowances in Europe.

Is the Golden Visa still available for students?+

No — Spain abolished the investor Golden Visa on 3 April 2025 (Organic Law 1/2025). Study visas, the Digital Nomad Visa and standard work permits are the routes now. See Immigrate to Spain.

Can I stay in Spain after studying?+

Yes — switch to a work permit (cuenta ajena) or self-employment (cuenta propia), the Digital Nomad Visa (if eligible), or the Non-Lucrative Visa. Permanent residence comes after 5 years; citizenship after 10 (with the CCSE exam and DELE A2, and 2-year routes for Ibero-American nationals).
