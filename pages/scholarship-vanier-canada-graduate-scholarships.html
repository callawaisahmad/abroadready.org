Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships eligibility criteria

  • Pursuing a doctoral (PhD) degree at a Canadian institution
  • Must be nominated by the institution where you intend to study
  • Open to Canadian citizens, permanent residents, and foreign citizens
  • Assessed on academic excellence, research potential, and leadership
  • Must attain an average score of at least 3.1 in each of the three criteria
  • Institution must have an available nomination quota

Benefits and coverage

  • $50,000 CAD per year for three years
  • Prestigious nationally recognized doctoral award
  • Supports study at any Canadian institution with a Vanier quota
  • Open to Canadian and international students
  • Recognizes leadership alongside academic and research excellence

Required documents

  • Personal Leadership Statement (max 2 pages)
  • Research Proposal (max 2 pages)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Letters of reference / referee assessments
  • Canadian Common CV
  • Voluntary Self-Identification Form for Indigenous applicants (optional)

How to apply for the Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. National competition deadline in early November; institutional internal deadlines earlier. Note: transitioning to the Canada Graduate Scholarship - Doctoral; verify current program status. Submit a complete application before the November deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
