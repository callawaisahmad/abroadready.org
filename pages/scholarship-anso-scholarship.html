About this scholarship

Full tuition, monthly stipend (CNY 6,000-7,000/month for PhD, CNY 3,000 for Masters), accommodation, and health insurance through CAS institutes. Masters 2-3 years; PhD 3-4 years

ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents (China) eligibility criteria

  • Non-Chinese citizen under 35 years old
  • Bachelor's degree for Masters; Master's for PhD
  • Strong academic record and research potential
  • English proficiency (or Chinese proficiency)
  • Not already enrolled in a Chinese institution
  • Endorsed by a CAS supervisor

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition waiver
  • Monthly stipend: Masters CNY 3,000, PhD CNY 7,000
  • On-campus accommodation
  • Comprehensive health insurance
  • Research mentoring from CAS scientists
  • Access to world-class research facilities
  • International conference travel support

Required documents

  • ANSO online application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Research proposal
  • Supervisor endorsement letter
  • Two recommendation letters
  • English/Chinese proficiency certificate
  • CV/Resume
  • Copy of passport

How to apply for the ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents (China)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Applications typically open in November and close in mid-February. Submit a complete application before the February deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
