About this scholarship

Partial scholarship of €3,000–€22,000 per year as compensation for study-related costs for excellent non-EU/EEA Master's students. One year, extendable to two years for two-year Master's programmes on meeting performance requirements

University of Twente Scholarship (UTS) eligibility criteria

  • Non-EU/EEA (international) student
  • (Provisionally) admitted to a qualifying UT Master's programme starting September
  • Has a UT student number
  • Among the best 5–10% of your graduation class (excellent students)
  • Academic IELTS 6.5 (or TOEFL iBT 90), with 6.0 (TOEFL iBT 20) on speaking
  • Not eligible for a Dutch study loan
  • Has not graduated from a UT (under)graduate programme

Benefits and coverage

  • Award ranging from €3,000 to €22,000 for one year
  • Compensation for study-related costs, spent at the student's discretion
  • Possible continuation into a second year for two-year Master's programmes
  • Applicable to 30+ qualifying UT Master's programmes
  • Supports talented non-EU/EEA international students

Required documents

  • UT student number
  • Proof of (provisional) admission to a qualifying Master's programme
  • English proficiency test scores (IELTS/TOEFL)
  • Completed online UTS scholarship application form

How to apply for the University of Twente Scholarship (UTS)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. One annual round; applications open around mid-October and close 1 April (16:00 CET) for the September start. Submit a complete application before the April deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
