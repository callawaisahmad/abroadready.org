Schwarzman Scholars eligibility criteria

  • Hold an undergraduate degree by August 1 of enrollment year
  • Aged 18-28 as of August 1 of enrollment year
  • English proficiency: TOEFL 100+, IELTS 7, Cambridge C1 185+, or Duolingo 130+ (waivable)
  • Demonstrated academic excellence and leadership qualities
  • Open to all nationalities (separate track for Chinese applicants)
  • No specific field of study required

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition and fees
  • Room and board
  • Round-trip travel to/from Beijing
  • In-country study tours
  • Required textbooks and course materials
  • Lenovo laptop
  • Health insurance
  • Monthly personal stipend

Required documents

  • Resume/CV (max 2 pages)
  • Leadership Essay (750 words)
  • Statement of Purpose (500 words)
  • Official transcripts from all post-secondary institutions
  • Three letters of recommendation
  • One-minute video introduction

How to apply for the Schwarzman Scholars

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Global/U.S. deadline is early-to-mid September; Chinese applicants apply in a separate window. Submit a complete application before the September deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
