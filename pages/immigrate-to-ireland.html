Immigrate to Ireland: The 2026 Permits & Settlement Guide

Ireland's skilled route is the Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP): a role on the critical-skills list paying €40,904+ (or any role paying €68,911+), no labour-market test, immediate family reunification with work rights — and Stamp 4 settlement after just 2 years. Graduates get a turbo-start via the Stamp 1G scheme and recent-graduate thresholds.

The Irish System in One Paragraph

Ireland runs on employment permits, not points. The Critical Skills Employment Permit is the flagship: for occupations on the Critical Skills Occupations List, or any non-ineligible role paying €68,911+, with no Labour Market Needs Test. After 2 years you're released from the permit system entirely onto Stamp 4 (free access to the labour market). Everything else flows from that: PR, citizenship after 5 reckonable years.

Graduate advantage: Irish graduates move onto the Stamp 1G (12–24 months, any employer, no permit), then convert to a CSEP at the discounted recent-graduate threshold (€36,848) — and the CSEP clock to Stamp 4 starts immediately.

Stamp 1G & the Graduate Route

Non-EEA graduates of recognised Irish institutions hold current Stamp 2 and apply within 6 months of their award: Level 8 → 12 months; Level 9/10 → 24 months (12 + 12 renewal while job-seeking). Full-time work with any employer, no employment permit. Must respect the 7-year (or 8-year for Level 9+) overall student limit.

Transition: after Stamp 1G, join the CSEP/GEP at recent-graduate rates or a research hosting agreement. The CSEP's 2-year-to-Stamp-4 clock then runs from your permit grant.

Tourist & Visitor Visa (Short-Stay C)

Ireland is not in Schengen — and unlike Schengen, its visa-free list is short. Far more nationalities need an Irish visa than a Schengen one, and a Schengen visa does not let you enter Ireland.

Short-Stay Visa Waiver: nationals of ~20 countries (India, China, Turkey, the Philippines and others) who already hold a valid UK short-stay visa can enter Ireland without a separate Irish visa for up to 90 days. It's not reciprocal, and direct flights from home still need the Irish visa.

Business Visa (Short Business Trips)

Business visits — conferences, meetings, negotiations and client visits — are covered by the same short-stay C visa with business as the stated purpose. Irish immigration explicitly lists "attend a conference/event" and "business activities related to your job" as valid C-visa reasons.

📋 Requirements C Visa

Invitation letter from the Irish host, employer's letter confirming purpose, proof of funds, accommodation and return travel. Same €60/€100 fees and 90-day limit as tourism.

🚫 No local work Restriction

You can attend meetings and negotiate but cannot take up employment or be paid for work performed in Ireland. Real employment needs an employment permit (CSEP/GEP) approved before travel.

🔄 Business residency Permit route

Working in Ireland for weeks or months requires an employment permit sponsored by the Irish employer — see the permit sections above.

All Work Routes: ICT, Dependant/Partner & Stamp 4/5

Beyond CSEP and GEP, Ireland has other employment-permit types and the settlement stamps that follow them.

🔄 Intra-company Transfer (ICT) €1,000

For managers, specialists and graduate trainees transferred within a multinational. Requires prior employment with the company (usually 12 months; 6 in defined cases) and a salary of at least €40,904. Fee €1,000, processing ~2–4 weeks, valid up to 2 years (longer for graduate trainees).

👩‍❤️‍👨 Dependant/Partner Employment Permit Spouses

Lets spouses/partners of ICT, researcher and certain other permit holders work in Ireland. (Spouses of CSEP holders need no permit — they get Stamp 1/1G work rights from day one.)

🪪 Stamp 4 — free market access 2 or 5 yrs

After 2 years on CSEP (or 5 on GEP) you're released from the permit system: work for any employer and change roles freely. Long-term residents get Stamp 4 after 5 years of work-permit time.

🪪 Stamp 5 — unconditional 8 yrs

After 8 years of continuous legal residence (with no dependence on State support), Stamp 5 permits residence without conditions or time limit.

📏 Salary thresholds at a glance 2026

CSEP: €40,904 (recent grads €36,848; high-earner route €68,911). GEP: €36,605 (recent grads €34,009; sector rates €32,691). ICT: €40,904. Below these, there's no permit route.

🛡 Other permit types Research etc.

Ireland also issues permits for researchers under a hosting agreement, sportspersons, religious workers and contract workers — all employer-driven with similar labour-market logic.

Plan the chain: CSEP (2 yrs) → Stamp 4 → citizenship after 5 years of reckonable residence. GEP (5 yrs) → Stamp 4. ICT holders receive Stamp 4 for the duration of the permit but it stays employer-tied and does not count toward the CSEP's 2-year release — switching to CSEP/GEP is the normal path to settlement.

Family & Partner Reunification

Ireland's family-reunification rules depend on the main applicant's status. The headline: CSEP holders reunify immediately; GEP holders after 1 year.

ItemDetails (2026)
CSEP holdersSpouse/partner and children join immediately; the spouse receives Stamp 1/1G work rights from day one
GEP holdersFamily reunification after 1 year of the permit
IncomeThe main applicant must show they can support the family without State support — income at or above the relevant permit threshold (€40,904 CSEP / €36,605 GEP) plus accommodation is the practical yardstick
RequirementsMarriage/civil-partnership certificate (or durable de-facto partnership evidence), birth certificates, proof of funds, accommodation
CostVisa fees for visa-required family members (€60/€100) + Stamp registration of €300 per person
ProcessingPreclearance / join-family visa: ~4–12 weeks after the permit is approved
De-facto partners: unmarried partners are recognised with evidence of a durable relationship (typically 2 years' cohabitation) — gather joint finances, correspondence and photos before applying.

Investor & Entrepreneur Routes

Two big changes matter here: the Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP) closed in February 2023, and the start-up route (STEP) remains open.

🚫 Immigrant Investor Programme Closed

The €1M investment / €500K endowment route to Stamp 4 stopped accepting new applications on 15 February 2023. Pending applications continue, but no new IIP applications are accepted and there is no replacement scheme.

🚀 Start-Up Entrepreneur Programme (STEP) Open

For innovative, export-focused start-up founders: €50,000 minimum funding from an eligible (non-State) source within 6 months, an innovative product with global potential and a job-creation plan. Successful applicants get Stamp 4 and run their company without a permit.

🏢 Company + work permit Employer route

Founders who don't meet STEP's criteria should note that directing your own company normally still needs a permit route (e.g. as an executive via CSEP/GEP with an eligible role).

🧓 Elderly dependent relative Long wait

A separate, slow and strict category lets elderly/dependent relatives live with a resident family member — medical, financial and dependency evidence required, with processing often running over a year.

Special Categories: Working Holidays & Other Routes

🎒 Working holiday agreements 18–35

Ireland has bilateral working-holiday arrangements with a number of countries (Australia, New Zealand, Canada, the US, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, Argentina, Chile, Taiwan and others) letting young people work and travel for up to 12 months.

🏥 Private medical treatment C Visa

Accessing treatment at a private hospital is an accepted short-stay C visa purpose — with a consultant's letter, proof of funds and insurance. Public treatment needs a longer-stay arrangement.

🕰 Long-term residence Stamp 4/5

Settlement runs through the Stamp system — Stamp 4 (no permit) after 2/5 years on CSEP/GEP, Stamp 5 (unconditional) after 8 years. Covered in the work-routes section above.

📄 Investor-closed note 2023

The Immigrant Investor Programme is closed to new applications since 15 February 2023 — don't pay any agent promising "new IIP applications"; only pending cases are processed.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to Ireland?+

Yes — Ireland's employment-permit system is entirely job-offer driven. There's no points-based independent route. The good news: CSEP needs no labour-market test, so employers face less red tape, and graduates buy time on the Stamp 1G to find the right role.

What's the fastest route to Irish permanent residence?+

A Critical Skills permit delivers Stamp 4 (free labour-market access) after just 2 years — the fastest settlement trigger in Europe. Citizenship needs 5 years of reckonable residence (the "5-in-9" rule) with one continuous year before application. Spouses of Irish citizens qualify in 3 years.

Can my family come with me on an Irish work permit?+

Yes — CSEP holders get immediate family reunification and their spouse receives Stamp 1/1G work rights (no separate permit). GEP holders can reunify after 1 year. Children attend school free.

Is Ireland a good post-study country?+

Excellent. The Stamp 1G gives 12–24 months of unrestricted work, recent-graduate CSEP/GEP thresholds are lower (€36,848/€34,009), and the tech economy (Dublin is Europe's EU tech hub) absorbs graduates readily. One catch: study time doesn't count toward the citizenship clock, so plan the work phase carefully.

How expensive is it to apply?+

CSEP €1,000 (up to 24 months), GEP €500–1,000, Stamp 1G registration €300. Permits refund 90% of the fee if refused. There's no separate PR application fee path in the same way as some countries — settlement happens via the Stamp system at registration.

Can I switch from a tourist visa to a work permit in Ireland?+

No. The short-stay C visa is visit-only and cannot be converted in-country. You must leave and have the Irish employer apply for an employment permit (CSEP/GEP/ICT) before you return. The main in-country family route is the join-family / preclearance process.

Do I need a separate business visa for a conference in Dublin?+

No. The short-stay C visa with business as the purpose covers conferences, meetings and negotiations for up to 90 days; visa-free nationalities need nothing. Bring the conference invitation and your employer's letter — immigration checks the business reason on arrival.

Is there a retirement or investor visa for Ireland?+

No retirement visa, and the Immigrant Investor Programme closed to new applications on 15 February 2023. Retirees have no passive-income route — realistic paths are employment, family ties, or the Start-Up Entrepreneur Programme for genuine founders.
