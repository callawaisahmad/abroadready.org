About this scholarship

KAUST Fellowship eligibility criteria

  • Open to international applicants worldwide
  • Strong academic record meeting program admission requirements
  • English proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL iBT, Pearson PTE or Cambridge CAE)
  • Three letters of recommendation
  • GRE optional (welcomed but not required)
  • Applicants with external full sponsorship may not qualify for the fellowship

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition and fees (up to ~USD 35,000/year) plus bench fees
  • Living stipend (~USD 20,000/year MS; ~USD 25,000-30,000/year PhD)
  • On-campus housing
  • Medical/health insurance for students and eligible family
  • Relocation, visa and travel support

Required documents

  • Official academic transcripts (translated if not in English)
  • English proficiency test scores
  • Three letters of recommendation
  • Statement of purpose / research interests
  • CV
  • GRE scores (optional)

How to apply for the KAUST Fellowship

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Two intakes; Fall deadline in spring (~May), Spring deadline ~October. Applications typically open mid-August. Submit a complete application before the May deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
