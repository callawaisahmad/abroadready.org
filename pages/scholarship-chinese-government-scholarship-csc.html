Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) eligibility criteria

  • Non-Chinese citizen in good health
  • Bachelors: high school graduate, generally under 25
  • Masters: hold a bachelor's degree, generally under 35
  • PhD: hold a master's degree, generally under 40
  • Not concurrently enrolled at a Chinese university
  • Meet the admission requirements of the host university and program

Benefits and coverage

  • Full tuition waiver
  • Free on-campus accommodation or housing allowance
  • Monthly living allowance: Bachelors CNY 2,500, Masters CNY 3,000, PhD CNY 3,500
  • Comprehensive medical insurance for international students in China
  • Exemption from application/registration fees
  • Chinese language study support where required

Required documents

  • Chinese Government Scholarship application form (completed in online system)
  • Notarized highest diploma/certificate of expected graduation
  • Academic transcripts
  • Study plan or research proposal in Chinese or English
  • Two recommendation letters (for Masters/PhD)
  • Foreigner Physical Examination Form
  • Copy of passport photo page
  • Language proficiency certificate (HSK or English) if applicable

How to apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

  1. Review the eligibility criteria above and confirm you meet every requirement.
  2. Prepare all required documents early — transcripts, CV, recommendation letters and motivation letter.
  3. Varies by agency/embassy/university, typically January–April; confirm with your channel Submit a complete application before the April deadline.
  4. Always confirm the latest details on the official website before applying.
