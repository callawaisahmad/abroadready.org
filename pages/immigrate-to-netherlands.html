Immigrate to the Netherlands: The 2026 HSM & Settlement Guide

The Netherlands' main skilled route is the Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant) permit — a job offer from an IND-recognised sponsor, a salary threshold, no labour-market test, and the 30% tax ruling for qualifying expats. Graduates get an unfair advantage: the Orientation Year plus a reduced HSM threshold of just €3,122/month.

The Dutch System in One Paragraph

The Netherlands is a sponsor-based system: to work, you need a job from an employer that's a recognised sponsor with the IND (the Dutch immigration service). Meet the salary threshold (indexed every January), and the permit follows — no labour-market test, no points. Settlement needs 5 continuous years of lawful residence, then permanent residence or naturalisation (with an A2 integration exam).

Graduate edge: if you studied in the Netherlands (or at a foreign top-200 university), the Orientation Year gives you 1–2 years of free job-search, and switching to HSM afterwards uses a reduced threshold (€3,122/month) until you hit 30. The Netherlands is one of the easiest EU countries for a fresh graduate to stay in.

Orientation Year — The Graduate Bridge

Full labour-market access for 1 year, renewable once (max 2 years).

  • Apply within 3 years of completing an NL bachelor's or master's (10+ months), a foreign top-200 university master's/PhD, or an Erasmus Mundus master's
  • Not available for MBO-level study only
  • No job offer or salary requirement during the year itself
  • After it: switch to HSM using the reduced €3,122 threshold, or another route
  • Official form: IND form 7523

Self-Employment & Other Routes

🧑‍💼 Self-employed residence permit

For freelancers whose activity serves an "essential interest" of the Dutch economy; requires KvK registration and a solid business plan. Harder than HSM — discretionary.

🚀 Startup

A recognised facilitator/mentor organisation sponsors your startup; no salary threshold. Common route for founders.

💍 Family reunification

Spouse/partner of a permit holder — partners get work rights; 21+ age requirement and income rules apply.

🎓 EU Blue Card

Higher threshold (€5,942) but faster track to long-term EU residence; reduced rate €4,754 for graduates.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to the Netherlands?+

For the HSM route, yes — the employer must be an IND-recognised sponsor. The exceptions are graduates on the Orientation Year (no offer needed, 1–2 years) and the self-employment/startup routes. Unlike Canada or Australia there's no points-based skilled route without a job.

How hard is the HSM salary threshold to meet?+

€4,357/month (under 30) is roughly a €62K annual salary — very attainable in tech, engineering, finance and consulting, less so in junior humanities roles. Graduates using the Orientation Year-to-HSM switch get €3,122/month, which helps a lot. Thresholds index every January.

What's the 30% tax ruling?+

For expats recruited from abroad, 30% of gross salary is paid tax-free for up to 5 years — a huge effective pay rise. Eligibility rules tightened in recent years (salary minimum applies). Your employer's HR handles the application to the tax authority.

How long to permanent residence in the Netherlands?+

5 continuous years of lawful residence on non-temporary permits, with an integration requirement. Citizenship also runs at 5 years but adds an A2 civic exam and (for most) renunciation of your previous nationality — check your exemptions.

Does studying in the Netherlands count toward PR?+

Time on a student permit generally does not count toward the 5-year PR residence clock — but the Orientation Year and HSM years after it do. That's why the study → Orientation Year → HSM pipeline is the standard Dutch settlement path.
