Immigrate to the UK: The 2026 Work Visa & ILR Guide

The UK is a points-adjacent system with no lottery and no per-country caps: find a licensed sponsor, meet the salary bar, and you're in. The path to settlement (ILR) runs through 5 years on a work route. This guide covers every visa, the real thresholds, and how study sets you up for it.

The UK System in One Paragraph

The UK doesn't use a points lottery like the US, nor a transparent ranking like Canada. Instead: a job offer from a licensed sponsor + a qualifying salary + English = a visa. Settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain) comes after 5 continuous years on qualifying routes, then citizenship after 12 months more. There are no annual caps on the main skilled routes — the bottleneck is finding a licensed-sponsor employer.

Biggest leverage for graduates: the Graduate Route (2 years, no sponsor) gives you time to find a Skilled Worker sponsor — and for many roles, your UK degree plus English may satisfy the salary/points requirement through the "new entrant" discount.

Other Routes Worth Knowing

🎓 Graduate Route

2 years after a UK degree (18 months from Jan 2027 for bachelor's/master's; PhD stays 3). No sponsor, no salary requirement, any job. The bridge to Skilled Worker. See Study in the UK.

⭐ Global Talent

For recognised leaders or emerging leaders in academia, tech, arts, medicine — endorsed by a Home Office body. No salary requirement, no cap, leads to ILR faster (3 years). Best for researchers and senior tech talent.

🎯 High Potential Individual (HPI)

For graduates of the top non-UK universities on the Home Office list. 2 years (3 for PhD), no job offer needed — but the Graduate Route requires a UK degree, HPI doesn't.

💍 Family & Ancestry

Spouse of a UK citizen/ILR holder (5-year route), British national by descent, or UK Ancestry (Commonwealth citizens with a British grandparent — 5-year route).

More Work Routes: Innovator, Scale-up & Seasonal

The UK's work menu doesn't stop at Skilled Worker — these routes cover founders, fast-growing tech firms and temporary labour.

Innovator Founder Settlement route

For entrepreneurs with a genuinely innovative, viable and scalable business plan that is endorsed by an approved body. The £50,000 funding requirement applies only if you haven't previously been endorsed on an Innovator-related route. 3-year visa, extendable, and can lead to settlement. Fee ~£1,486 + £500 endorsement fee.

Scale-up No sponsor

For skilled workers hired by a "scale-up" sponsor — a company that grew staff or revenue by at least 20% a year for 3 years and employs 10+. A 2-year visa with no ongoing sponsorship requirement: after year 2 you can stay on other routes or apply for extension. Ideal for fast-moving tech companies.

Seasonal Worker Temporary

Up to 6 months of temporary work in horticulture and poultry packing through an approved scheme operator (≈43,000 places a year). Low barrier, but it does not count toward settlement and won't let you switch into Skilled Worker from inside the UK.

Youth Mobility Scheme (YMS) 18–30

For young people from partner countries (Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, India and more). Live and work in the UK for 2–3 years with no sponsor and no job offer; only limited balloted places per country each year. Fee £298 + IHS. You can switch to a work route (like Skilled Worker) before it ends — that's the standard route to ILR.

The £29,000 hurdle is real: many couples fall short on the income requirement alone. You can combine salaries, savings above £16,000 (counted 2.5:1), self-employment income or a job offer in the UK starting within 3 months. If the applicant is a parent with custody, the financial bar can rise to £62,500 in savings or £38,700 in income — plan before you apply.

Visitors, Tourists & Business Trips

Tourist and business travel to the UK is visa-free for many nationalities — with the new digital ETA — and a Standard Visitor visa for the rest. Neither allows you to work.

Business visits on visitor status

Both ETA and the Standard Visitor visa cover business visits: meetings, conferences, negotiations, site visits and fact-finding. The rules are strict — you can't be paid by a UK source, can't do productive work for a UK client, can't fill a UK vacancy and can't be "self-employed" in the UK. An invitation letter from the host company stating purpose and dates strengthens your application.

Visitor hard rules: no working, no accessing public funds, no marriage, and study is limited to short recreational courses (max 30 days). If you're offered a UK job while visiting, you must leave and apply for the Skilled Worker visa from abroad — visitor status cannot be switched to work in-country.

Indefinite Leave to Remain & Citizenship in Brief

  • ILR: 5 continuous years on a qualifying route (Skilled Worker, HPI, UK Ancestry, partner), with no more than 180 days' absence per year, plus the Life in the UK test and B1 English. Fee ~£2,885
  • Citizenship: 12 months after ILR, residence in 5 of the last 6 years, Life in the UK test + B1 English. Fee ~£1,580
  • Faster routes: Global Talent reaches ILR in 3 years; the Global Talent + spouse dependants route can reach ILR sooner still
Keep it simple: for most people the sequence is work route (5 years) → ILR → 12 months → citizenship — about 6–7 years from first visa to British passport. Time on a Student visa counts toward the residence requirement for citizenship but not toward the ILR clock.

From Student to Settlement: The 5-Year Plan

  1. Study (1–3 yrs): UK degree on a Student visa
  2. Graduate Route (2 yrs): work freely, no sponsor — find a sponsor role paying the going rate
  3. Skilled Worker (3 yrs): switch to a sponsored role; use the new-entrant discount if needed
  4. ILR (at 5 yrs on qualifying routes): apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain — no immigration time limit
  5. Citizenship (+12 months): apply after 12 months of ILR (pass the Life in the UK test + B1 English)

Total: roughly 6–8 years from first day of study to passport — comparable to Canada, faster than the US. Time on the Student visa itself counts toward the residence requirement for citizenship but not toward the 5-year ILR clock.

Immigration FAQs

Do I need a job offer to immigrate to the UK?+

For the Skilled Worker route, yes — a sponsored job is mandatory. But the Graduate Route (UK degree), High Potential Individual (top global university) and Global Talent (endorsement) all allow entry without a job offer, buying you time to find sponsorship.

Can I get ILR without a job?+

ILR requires 5 years on qualifying routes; if that route is Skilled Worker you must remain employed (with allowances for unemployment gaps). Routes like family settlement or UK Ancestry let you reach ILR without sponsorship. The Global Talent route reaches ILR in just 3 years.

Is the UK harder than Canada or Australia?+

Different: the UK has no points ranking, so it's not harder, just different — it rewards finding a sponsor more than raw points. Canada/Australia reward points first. The UK's salary threshold (£41,700) is a real hurdle for junior roles; Canada's points system favours younger candidates. Compare all in our immigration hub.

What's the fastest route to UK settlement?+

Global Talent: ILR after 3 years with an endorsement (researchers, exceptional tech talent). Otherwise the standard path is 5 years (Skilled Worker/HPI/family), plus 12 months for citizenship. Recent White Paper proposals aim to introduce "earned settlement" rules — keep an eye on changes.

Does studying in the UK count toward ILR?+

Not toward the 5-year ILR clock (only work-route years count). But Student visa time counts toward the total residence requirement for citizenship (5 of the 6 years, 12-month absence rule). The Graduate Route's 2 years do count toward ILR only if you then hold a qualifying work visa for the remaining 3.

Can I work on a Standard Visitor visa or ETA?+

No. Both are strictly non-working statuses. Business meetings, conferences, negotiations and site visits are fine, but any employment — paid or unpaid — is prohibited, and you can't be paid by a UK source. Working illegally on visitor status risks refusal and a future ban.

Can I switch from a visitor visa to a work visa in the UK?+

Generally no. Visitor status cannot be switched to a work route from inside the UK — you must leave and apply for the Skilled Worker (or other) visa from your home country. In-country switching is only allowed between qualifying routes that you already hold (e.g., Graduate Route → Skilled Worker).

Who qualifies for the Youth Mobility Scheme or UK Ancestry?+

YMS: citizens aged 18–30 of listed partner countries (Australia, NZ, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, India and more) — 2–3 years of free work, no sponsor, limited places per country. UK Ancestry: Commonwealth citizens with a British-born grandparent — a 5-year route to ILR with full work rights and no sponsorship.
